; romfs.txt - the ROM disk's files (tools/romfs.js): a line a file, its path on the ROM disk (/rom), then its source,
; relative to this folder; ';' starts a comment.  The build makes a read-only HydraFS volume of them in the paged ROM,
; in the banks after the modules (the disk x: mount '#f' /rom x), and reads every file back from the image to check
; it.  The directories are made for the paths.
