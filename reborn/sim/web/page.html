Getting started

This is HydraOS on an emulated Hydra-16 (the V1 board, cycle by cycle), running in this page: nothing is sent anywhere. Click the terminal and type. HyForth is the login shell; rc starts the Plan 9 shell, hylang, basic and edit the others, and ps lists the tasks.

The multitasking demos: bind -a /rom/sample/c /bin, then race, chorus, philo 4 (philo -d 4 to see a deadlock), prodcons and round (turn the Sound on first). Ctrl-C stops one. (With the Vera X, its driver has a task: five philosophers want one more than are free.) ls /rom shows what else is on the ROM disk.

Keys: Ctrl+Shift+C copies the screen, Ctrl+Shift+V pastes; the browser keeps Ctrl+W, Ctrl+T and Ctrl+N. The mouse wheel scrolls back through the terminal's lines. With the Vera X and its keyboard on (Setup), click its screen to type there and use its mouse; click elsewhere to give them back.

SD cards (Setup): a new card is a blank HydraFS card, seen as /sd/0 (the Vera X's as /sd/v). Cards are kept in this browser, and Save card downloads one as an image file, which sim/tools/hydrafs.js reads and a disk imager can write.
