# styles.txt - (dq0) the styles' proportions, in sets (ENGINE-CRITIQUE.md's §5, step 3; DECISIONS.md's "The styles'
# proportions in a styles file, the theme naming a set"): what the maker's growers make in a stratum of each style
# (DUNGEON.md), and how often.  The build makes it lib/dq0/styles.dat (tools/dq0build.js's styles: theme.h's
# struct style, a set's four in level.h's ST_* order, then the sets' names), which the maker reads as it makes its
# first chunk (dq0m/uses.c), the set its theme names: a theme file's styles line (themec's: the set's number in the
# record's mission, TM_STYLES), or none, the first set, the house's.  A set's number is its place here: a set is
# added after the others, never put between them, so a theme compiled before still names the one it named.
#
# A set: its line (set | its name, 11 letters at most | the set it begins as a copy of, or none), then its rows, each
# a field of struct style and its values for the four styles, built | natural | mine | mixed (a field of more than
# one value, its values in a column with spaces between; a thing's kind by items.h's name, KI_ left off, in lower
# case).  A set begun from another gives only the rows it changes; one begun from none gives every row.  16 sets at
# most (TM_STYLES's four bits).  Each a chance in so many (its die's sides, as grow.h has it), or weights:
#   lit          a room built with sconces (lit only if it's kept): in 8, one fewer each stratum down
#   zones        a long room (6 squares) in zones: in 10;  zones3  three of them (9 squares): in 3
#   slope        a zone's floor sloping: in 4
#   ceil         ceilings by kind (flat, barrel, dome, cathedral, sloped, rough): weights, summing to 20
#   doors        a way out with a door at all (else a doorway): in 20
#   d_open d_locked d_barred    a door open, locked, barred: in 20
#   d_hidden     a door hidden: in 15;  d_magic  of those, hidden by magic: in 4
#   m_wood m_ironbound          a door's wood, iron-bound (the rest iron): in 10
#   chambers     (caves) chambers along the tunnels: up to, less one;  wide  a tunnel three squares wide: in 3;
#                glow  a chamber with a glow: in 10
#   things       a thing in a room (two tries): in 20;  hidden  concealed: in 20;  h_lo h_span  how well: a
#                difficulty (steps) from h_lo, two more for each of h_span grades
#   kinds        what's lying about (eight);  hide_kinds  what's concealed (four)
#   f_earth p_earth             a room's floor bare earth (else flagged; a cave's stone), a chunk's passages': in
#                20, one more each 4 centuries old
#   w_built c_built             a room's walls masonry, its ceiling (else the rock): in 20
#   windows      a room's side with a window, where a passage runs beside it: in 20;  slits  of those, an arrow
#                slit: in 20;  w_glass w_shut w_bars  a window's glass, shutters, bars: in 4 each
#   portc        a door a portcullis instead: in 20
#   traps        a trap in a room: in 20;  t_pit  a pit (open or covered): in 4;  t_hidden  hidden: in 10;
#                t_magic  of those, by magic: in 10
#   d_trap       a closed door trapped: in 20
