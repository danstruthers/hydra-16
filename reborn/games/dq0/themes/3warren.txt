# 3warren.txt - (dq0) a stand-in theme: a kobold warren, mines under a ruin (1crypt.txt has the format).  Its
# mission either: out by the warren's own key, or to rule the kobolds by their totem on the chieftain's throne.

           behind you.  The warren's gate is locked with brass, and the kobolds keep its key in pieces; and they
           obey whoever sets their totem on the bone throne of their chieftains.

rule     | read | map scrap | Scratched on the hide in soot: the gate, a key in two pieces, the bow and the blade;
           and far below, a throne with a stick-figure on it, kneeling kobolds round.
rule     | combine | key bow + key blade = brass key | The bow and the blade of the key screw together into one
           brass key.
rule     | out | brass key | open | The brass key turns in the gate's lock with a squeal, and the gate swings open.
rule     | put | totem | bone throne | rule | You set the totem on the bone throne.  From every tunnel comes the
           yapping of the kobolds, and then a hush: they come, one by one, and kneel.

lock     | The way out is shut fast. Whatever opens it is somewhere below.  A gate of iron bars, its lock of brass.
opened   | The gate stands open on the ruin above: go up to leave.
ending   | escaped | You climb up through the gate and out of the ruin into the open air.  The Kobold Warren yaps
           behind you.  You have escaped.
ending   | ruled | The kobolds are yours, every one, and the warren and all its diggings with them.  Long live the
           chieftain!
ending   | died | The kobolds pick your bones, and argue over your boots.
