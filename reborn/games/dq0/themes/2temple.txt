rule     | read | scroll | The scroll is swollen with damp, but you make it out: "The Mother takes her pearl in her
           cup, and gives back the way.  Lay them on her altar, together."
rule     | combine | pearl + chalice = full chalice | You drop the pearl into the chalice.  It rolls, and settles, and
           shines there like a drop of the moon.
rule     | put | full chalice | altar | open | You set the chalice on the altar.  Water wells up round its foot and
           runs away down the steps; far above, a great door groans as it opens.
rule     | out | full chalice | - | The chalice is the Mother's: it belongs on her altar, not here.

lock     | The way out is shut fast. Whatever opens it is somewhere below.  The temple's doors are sealed with green
           bronze, the water's mark high on them.
opened   | The bronze doors stand open on the marsh: go up to leave.
ending   | escaped | You climb out into the reeds and the grey light over the marsh.  Behind you the Drowned Temple
           sinks back into the water.  You have escaped.
ending   | died | The water closes over you, and the Mother has one more of her own.
