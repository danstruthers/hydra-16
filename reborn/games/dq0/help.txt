# Dingus Quest Zero's help: what "help" (or "?") prints, each line a paragraph (help.c's o_help reads it; a line
# begun with # isn't printed). Each line under 400 characters.
Moving: north, northeast, east ... northwest (n, ne, e, se, s, sw, w, nw, or go north ...); forward, back, left, right (f, b: as you face); up and down (u, d: the stairs); turn left, right, half left, half right or around; face north ...; climb (up, down, through a window); jump down (a ledge, a shaft); dig north ... or down (a wall, the rock, a shaft; the best tool you carry, or say which: dig down with the knife).
The sky: a window on the surface, a skylight or a shaft open to the sky shows the time of day (time); wait there, or watch the sun, to read it the better. A shaft is climbed as a ledge is, if its mouth's in reach; a thing dropped where it goes down falls down it.
Doors and traps: open, close, unlock (pick the lock), force (the door ahead), raise, lower (a portcullis), break (a door, a window's glass, a portcullis's bars), bend (bars), lift (a bar), bar (a door: its bar put back), disarm (a trap), search.
Searching: search (about you, a minute), search nearby, the east wall, the floor; search for the key; search the straw (a chest, a body: what's in it or under it); search the room (the whole of it, wall by wall and thing by thing, told as you go; stopped, search it again to go on).
Things: get (take, pick up), drop, hold (wield), stow, put the dagger in the sack (or on the table), take the rope from the pack (a worn pack comes off, and back on), hang the pouch (on your belt), wear, take off, light and douse (a torch, a candle, a lantern: light a new torch), examine (a thing, a door, the room: look closely), hide the dagger; inventory (i).
Hiding: hide (behind the table, in the straw, in the shadows): not with a light on you, nor with one watching. A creep keeps you hidden; a walk, standing up (stop hiding) or doing a thing ends it. Looks, listens and waits don't.
Looking and the rest: look, listen, smell, map (m), sheet (the character), status (your pace, what you watch for, your condition, hunger and thirst), wait (z), pause (or Escape), help (?), quit (q).
The map: what you see now bright, what you remember dim (fading as time goes, the better kept where you've been often), what you've drawn on your map brown; map as you go to keep it the better, with paper or parchment, and charcoal, a pencil, or a quill and ink to hand, to draw it; colour off for plain letters (colour on again).
The puzzle's pieces: read (a tablet), combine the one with the other, put the one on the other, use the key on the way out (or open the way out with it).
Several at once: north then east; open the door; go through it; take the sword and the shield (each in turn).
Pace: creep (sneak), walk slowly, walk, jog, run, sprint (set pace to jog; run north); search for traps as you go (move carefully), listen as you go, map as you go; stop searching (listening, mapping, running).
Going: a way word alone walks straight on till something stops it (f, n ...); go through the door, leave; go to (approach, head for) anything you've seen here: the pot, the rat, the door, the centre, the far left corner; cross the room.
Small moves: step forward, move left one space, go north 20 feet, back up two squares (a square 5 feet). Look north, listen at the door; stop.
Fights: attack the rat (hit, fight), with what you hold; defend (dodge, block, counter, none, best); disengage (back away), flee (run away), pass.
Names: name my sword Linda (or name the sword as Linda), then drop Linda, attack the rat with Linda; call this room the Mausoleum; forget the name Linda.
Shortcuts: alias nd is north then dig down makes nd stand for that line (words typed after nd go after its line); alias nd shows it, alias lists them and the game's own words, unalias nd removes it.
With the denizens: talk to the kobold, persuade, deceive, threaten; calm, befriend (an animal); examine one (how it seems); an ally's requests: follow me, stay here, tell Grub to wait, fetch the key, open the door, dismiss it; name the rat Grub.
Trading (with those friendly to you): give the bandit 5 pennies; how much for the rope, buy the rope from the dwarf; what will you give for this, sell my dagger; haggle for the rope; entertain the dwarf, sing to it. Antidotes and remedies: drink the antidote.
Played over the serial port, the game answers quickest at 19200 or 115200 baud (echo b115200 >/dev/serctl, then the terminal set to match); on the Vera X's screen and keyboard it needs neither.
