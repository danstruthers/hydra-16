# Dingus Quest Zero's help: what "help" (or "?") prints, each line a paragraph (help.c's o_help reads it; a line
# begun with # isn't printed). Each line under 400 characters.
Moving: north, northeast, east ... northwest (n, ne, e, se, s, sw, w, nw, or go north ...); forward, back, left, right (f, b: as you face); up and down (u, d: the stairs); turn left, right, half left, half right or around; face north ...; climb (up, down, through a window); jump down (a ledge, a shaft); dig north ... or down (a wall, the rock, a shaft).
Doors and traps: open, close, unlock (pick the lock), force (the door ahead), raise, lower (a portcullis), break (a door, a window's glass, a portcullis's bars), bend (bars), lift (a bar), bar (a door: its bar put back), disarm (a trap), search.
Things: get (take, pick up), drop, hold (wield), stow, put the dagger in the sack (or on the table), take the rope from the pack, hang the pouch (on your belt), wear, take off, light and douse (a torch, a candle, a lantern), examine (a thing, a door, the room: look closely), hide; inventory (i).
Looking and the rest: look, listen, smell, map (m), sheet (the character), status (your pace, what you watch for, your condition), wait (z), pause (or Escape), help (?), quit (q).
The puzzle's pieces: read (a tablet), combine the one with the other, put the one on the other, use the key on the way out (or open the way out with it).
Several at once: north then east; open the door; go through it; take the sword and the shield (each in turn).
Pace: creep (sneak), walk slowly, walk, jog, run, sprint (set pace to jog; run north); search for traps as you go (move carefully), listen as you go, map as you go; stop searching (listening, mapping, running).
Small moves: step forward, move left one space, go north 20 feet, back up two squares (a square 5 feet). Places: go to the centre, the far left corner, the other side. Look north, listen at the door; stop.
Fights: attack the rat (hit, fight), with what you hold; defend (dodge, block, counter, none, best); disengage (back away), flee (run away), pass.
Names: name my sword Linda (alias the sword as Linda), then drop Linda, attack the rat with Linda; call this room the Mausoleum; forget the name Linda.
With the denizens: talk to the kobold, persuade, deceive, threaten; calm, befriend (an animal); examine one (how it seems); an ally's requests: follow me, stay here, tell Grub to wait, dismiss it; name the rat Grub.
Played over the serial port, the game answers quickest at 19200 or 115200 baud (echo b115200 >/dev/serctl, then the terminal set to match); on the Vera X's screen and keyboard it needs neither.
