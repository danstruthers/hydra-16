# said.txt - Dingus Quest Zero's phrase table: the words of what came of an action (ENGINE-CRITIQUE.md's Â§3.2, narration
# as data).  The world tells an outcome event (msgs.def's outcome events: E_DIG, E_CLIMB, E_PORTAL, E_TRAP, their head
# E_OUTCOME), and the UI's one renderer (said.c, overlay 27: outcome_words) words it by its row here, chosen by its kind and its
# outcome.  Made by the build (games/dq0/saidgen.js, after hubgen.js) into obj/gen/dq0said.h: the rows packed, their
# fields' places in struct msg (msgs.def's, as cc65 lays them out), the names of the UI's own outcomes (SAID_*).
#
#   kind KIND FIELD [ACTOR]     KIND (E_KIND) worded here too, its outcome its own field FIELD, not E_OUTCOME's (a kind
#                               older than the head: E_BEAT's beat, its bytes where they've always been); its actor
#                               its field ACTOR (0 the character, else the actor plus 1), not the event's who
#   own KIND NAME ...           outcomes of the UI's own for KIND: rows it chooses itself (said_as), numbered after its
#                               enum's (SAID_KIND_NAME: SAID_CLIMB_RISES)
#   last KIND ...               KIND's rows placed after every other kind's and the pieces, in the rows' last bank with
#                               room (said seldom: said_b's few cycles more a byte there the least felt)
#   @NAME "text"                a piece of text, put where a row has {@NAME}
#   KIND.OUTCOME[COND] "text"   a row: KIND the event's kind less its E_ (DIG: E_DIG); OUTCOME its outcome's name less
#                               its enum's prefix, in lower case (begun: D_BEGUN), or an own one; [COND] (F=V,V ...: a
#                               field and its values, the headers' names or numbers under 8; or one value, any number
#                               under 256: task=KI_SLING) the row's only where the field's one of them (such rows tried
#                               first, in their order, then the one without).  OUTCOME any: a row for any outcome the
#                               kind's other rows don't take (tried after them, the same way)
#   ... +"text"                 said on after what's said already (else on a line of its own: msg's)
#   ... once "text"             said as a look's sentences are (lbsaid.s's lb_said): none said twice in an answer
#   A line begun with a space goes on with the one before it: "text" "text" one text.  In the text, \n a new line, \"
#   a quote, \\ a backslash, and in braces:
#       {nl}                    a line of its own here (msg's: a new line if the story's line has words)
#       {F}                     the event's field F (msgs.def's name for it: {feet}); F-K less K (0 at least), F*K
#                               times K, F>K 1 if it's more than K, else 0 (K a number or a header's name: far*SQ_FT)
#       {F?a|b|c}               a, b, c ... by F's value (0, 1, 2 ...; the last for any past them); each may hold braces
#       {obj} {Obj} {instr} {Instr} {text} {Text}   the words the UI gives (said_s): the thing done to, the thing done
#                               with, a word of the row's own; capitalised.  {obj?a|b} ...: a if none's given, else b
#       {actor} {Actor} {s}     "you" or the denizen ("the giant rat", "Grub"); "s" for a denizen, "" for you
#                               ("open{s}"); {actor?a|b}: a for the character, b for a denizen.  A row's words are any
#                               actor's: one wording an action, the denizens' (DEN's) and the character's sharing it
#                               by a piece (@open, @close ...: the user's, 2026-11-11).  The actor is the event's who,
#                               or the kind's own field (said.txt's kind line's third word: E_BEAT's whose)
#       {its} {'s}              the actor's: "your" or "its" ("their", a person's: one of the speaking peoples, the
#                               hub's folk, the user's people "they", beasts "it", 2026-11-17; one not seen,
#                               "something", "its": UK_FIGHT's, blows.c's); after {Actor}, "r" or "'s" ("Your", "The
#                               bandit's")
#       {they F?a|b}            a if the actor field F holds (its entry plus 1: E_TASK's by) isn't a person, else b:
#                               "{they by?it finds|they find}" (a pronoun the subject, its verb plural: "they find";
#                               "the elf" keeps the singular: "The elf finds").  The UI's own kinds give their actors'
#                               as fields of their own (UK_FIGHT's afolk and dfolk, UK_SWAY's, UK_LIST's, UK_NO's folk)
#   A field named in more than one of the kind's forms is the row's outcome's form's (E_BEAT's fa, its met form's or
#   its hit's), else the kind's own form's (E_TASK's task, 8-15, not its light form's 8-13)
#       {arg} {arg?a|b ...}     the UI's number (said_n): a choice it's made for the row
#       {a}                     "a", or "an" if the word after it begins with a vowel
#       {rel F}                 the heading F (H_N ... H_NW), from the way the character faces ("to your left")
#       {room F} {light}        the theme's names: a room's kind F, the light it has (theme_named's)
#       {trap F G}              a trap's name, its kind F and its trigger G (words.c's trap_name)
#       {kind F}                the name of the thing's kind F (the catalogue's: "dagger")
#       {name F}                the name of actor F (its entry plus 1) as it is ("giant rat", "Grub")
#       {-s}                    the word before it made one: its last s gone ("1 arrow")
#       {F!text}                said only if F isn't as it was the last time this was said for the kind (and F kept);
#                               {forget}: as if it were 0
#       {@NAME}                 a piece's text
