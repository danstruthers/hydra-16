## 1. The world's banks under 64 (item 1: won back)

Nothing common to the 13 banks grew. 0e042d00 and 4144bb06 built side by side: the world's parts grew 1,936 bytes in
all (H15's pools, wpool.c in part 99: +2,330; the rest a few bytes up or down), and the banks' free fell by the same
1,936. The banks are filled by the build (parts.js: parts.txt's wish, then the best fit, the biggest first), keeping 64
in each if it can (spread()). At 4144bb06 the best fit could keep only 45 in each (obj/games/dq0w/spare.txt said 45),
though 4,651 bytes were spare among the 31 .ovl banks over 64 each: a bin-packing limit, not a lack of room.

The fix: spread() mends banks()'s packing (none kept) when its own try fails: a bank short, one of its parts moved to a
bank of its file that keeps its 64 after, else swapped with another bank's part (the swap leaving the two the most);
again till none is short. No chance in it: the same packing each build. dq0w.ovl the same size (OV31, the file's last
and unfilled, 215 free where it had 48).

## 2. The profiler (item 5)

`node sim/tools/lineprof.js TEST [LINE] [--nth N] [--list] [--top N] [--lead N] [--out FILE]` (tests/TESTING.md): the
test run once for its lines (Enter, first letter: dq0Timer's new `at(i)`), then again with the profile on from the
line's Enter to its first letter; by task, each part copied in (its cycle, the copy's cycles, sw_call's or sw_back's
and whose), by C function (helpers charged to the caller), by routine and two callers. Hooks: machine.js's
`profileStep` (and the quick runs kept till the window: a profile of a 3,000M-cycle test takes ~2 minutes),
sim/test.js's `addInstrument` and its tests exported. The second run checks its window is the same.
