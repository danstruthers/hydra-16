## What was built, and why

### The senses' look one way made quicker as it is

- **No giving way to its own answer** (dq0p.c's look).  A look gave way to the UI's command in hand at the real paces,
  half a second at most; for a look one way that command is the look itself, the world's E_VIEW waiting on it, so it
  slept its whole half second for nothing.  A look one way's ticks are given at once now; a look about, the sound
  layer's pulse and a denizen's senses give way as before.
- **Its grid made only where it can see** (view.c's way_grid).  The lines are cast to a glance's reach every way not
  behind, and twice as far only in the quarter ahead (cast.s's), so only the squares the look can see are made now,
  with the walls round them and the lines' squares between: on a compass way the half ahead and two squares back
  (34 of the 63 columns); on a diagonal the quarter ahead and a glance's reach.  The rest of the grid is left as it
  was: cast.s passes over a square there (behind, or past a glance's reach outside the quarter ahead) on its geometry
  alone, before anything of it but its flags is read, and marks nothing there.  The same squares seen.
- **The lamps' code in part 3 by its pragmas** (things.c's things_lamps, light.c's light_lamps_end): the packer had
  already moved it there from part 1 for the swap area's room; part 1's grid code made part 1 too big for a bank whole
  (the packer's check), so the source now says where it lives.  Nothing copied differently.

### What's near told first

A look one way is told in two parts: the world's s_look_way asks the senses for its near part (LK_NEAR), tells it if
they made it (E_VIEW V_LOOKED; the reply's ch LC_FAR), then asks the whole look one way (LK_WAY) as before and tells
that.

- **Its near part** (dq0p.c's look_near) is made from the last whole look, if it stands: the same square, facing,
  light, sight and eyes' height, nothing the world has changed since (no E_LIGHT: a door, a square, a light), and not a
  look through a door's little window nor a blind one.  Every look the world asks leaves the senses' record and the
  squares' marks as the character sees them now, so the near part is the look one way's own record made from them
  (way.c's way_seen, into WAY_NEAR): the openings ahead and what's beyond them, the things, denizens and lights far off
  ahead, in a look one way's words (its eye, what it makes out twice as far).  Some 150,000 cycles, no view made.
- **What it holds back** for the far part, so nothing is told and then contradicted:
  - an opening whose way the eye follows into a chunk not made (none is made for the near part: the far part's to);
  - an opening whose way runs out past what a glance is sure of (a glance's reach less two, each way's sum), where the
    far part may see more: something lights squares past a glance's reach within a look one way's (far_may: the
    character's own light or ultravision reaching that far; a lit thing lying about; a lit room some of which is within
    a look one way's reach and not all within two thirds of a glance's), erring toward holding back;
  - "You see nothing more that way." where the far part may see more at all (those, or a chunk not made within reach).
- **Its far part** is the whole look one way, as before (WAY_SEEN).  If its record is all as the near part's (WM_SAME:
  the common case, the far ring ahead dark) nothing more is said; else only what's new: the openings held back or new,
  and the things, denizens and lights far off the near part didn't tell (a thing with others on it told again only if
  one of them is new).  None of the near part's sentences is said twice (the look's own sentences aren't the told
  list's: the UI compares the openings).
- **When the last look doesn't stand** (moved by a test's poke, a door's little window, a blind look, the lights
  changed) the whole look one way is made at once and told as before.
- **Room**: the far check (far_may) is part 7, in a fifth overlay bank of the senses (dq0p.cfg's OV5): in the main RAM
  it cost a further 1,231 bytes.  The UI's own (overlay 13) uses no C library routine the UI's main RAM didn't have
  (memcmp would have taken it 13 bytes under its floor).

## Open questions

1. **A stir between the parts.**  The near part's E_VIEW is the character's news: the UI's command is done with as it
   comes, so a denizen's stir held while the line was answered is told after the near part's words and before the far
   part's ("Something stirs." between them), where before it came after the whole look.  Only where the far part has
   something to add.  Keep it, or hold the stir for the far part?
2. **The far check errs toward holding back.**  A lit room anywhere within a look one way's reach that isn't wholly
   near makes every opening whose way runs out past a glance's sure squares wait for the far part, though the way may
   not pass near it; the way it faces isn't asked.  The words are right either way, only their order.  Finer (the lit
   squares themselves, the quarter ahead) costs part 7's room and the near part's time.
3. **The look across and the map** wait on the maker's chunk (the maker branch's): the look across 19.5M to 15.0M with
   the smaller grid.  The test puts the character there by a poke, so its last look doesn't stand and the whole look
   one way is made at once; in play its near part would come first, the way into the chunk not made held back.
