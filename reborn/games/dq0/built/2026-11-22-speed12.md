## 1. D17: dq0felled's "wait" (and the fights' windows)

lineprof of the window (708,853): the referee's blow 175,000 of its cycles, the UI's part 2 copy for the parse 44,848,
then the blow's words rendered (said.c's run, fv, said_b: ~120,000) before the first letter was out.

- **The blow's words said as they're made** (said.c, overlay 27, the one renderer): a row's words were built whole in
  lb and said at its end. Now, at a literal space with 16 letters or more in lb, the words so far go to say() and lb
  starts again. say keeps its column and the space owed, so the lines wrap exactly as before (the deterministic tests'
  transcripts are byte-for-byte the same in their sentences: dq0examine, dq0dig, dq0things, dq0pour, dq0strike,
  dq0hardblow, dq0namedblow). Not done while an "a" waits for its word (an), while a thing's name's mark ("#12",
  names_say's) is in lb, or for words kept (said_q, a look's lb_said: `once`). Every outcome's first letter benefits,
  not only a blow's.
- **run() cheaper**: its locals static (in OV27BSS: no main RAM), p kept in a local only round its two calls that need
  it after (a piece, a list); a choice's field read once, not twice (`fv (f) > 255 ? 255 : fv (f)`).
- **The referee's EP held for a blow** (speed11's lead): harn_get_ep (load summed and divided, endurance averaged,
  the quotient: ~900 cycles) was called ~10 times a blow through harn_eml, harn_get_pp, the fumble and stumble rolls.
  The SDK's harn_get_ep now returns `harn_ep_kept` for `harn_ep_of`; dq0harn sets them round fight() (RS_ATTACK,
  MORALE, WIELD, ROUSE, NEW_ACTOR: nothing there changes the character's load or endurance; set_load is only RS_EQUIP's
  and the outfit's) and clears them after. Same numbers, no die changed.

dq0felled's "wait" typed as the character was busy: **708,853 (98%) -> 466,365** after the first two (the window's last
run: 211,450-259,083 at the final build, its phases moved).
