## 1. dq0follow

The failure: "out of reach as the follow began: not said once": the check's text, from "> follow the rat" on, read
"You follow the giant rat. [######....] torch 59m UP 0 facing W You can't get near enough to the giant rat.", and its
regex wants the two sentences together.

What the screen got (mB14/dq0follow.txt): after "You follow the giant rat." the cursor went to row 1, column 39, and drew the
status line in reverse video (`ESC[1;39H ESC[7m [######....]  torch 59m  UP 0  facing W ESC[0m`). Then it came back to the
story's row for "You can't get near enough to the giant rat.", and after that blanked the bar on row 1. Before (looks3's
and earlier transcripts) the status line's only draw there was the "W" after the second sentence. The follow is a long
task; its progress bar is now drawn while the task is under way, when the UI comes round between the world's two
events. That depends on the order the tasks run in (fw4's foreground change, this branch's naps).

**Which:** the UI's behaviour is right. The status line is its own row, drawn by cursor moves, and the answer on screen
isn't split: the two sentences sit together on the story's rows. The fault is the check's text. dq0Story leaves the
status line's reverse-video draws in, because other checks read the status line from the story: dq0helpless's OUT,
dq0venom's BLEEDING, dq0legs' PRONE, dq0hunger's "thirsty". Taking them out of dq0Story failed those four, as I found by
trying it first. So dq0Story stays as it was. A new helper, dq0Prose (tests/lib/dq0.js), is the story with the status
line's draws left out, as dq0Timer's story already has it. dq0follow's check of an answer's words in order now reads
dq0Prose.

dq0follow, ten runs at its pace (the turns pace, its only one; the runs are deterministic): 10 of 10 pass.
