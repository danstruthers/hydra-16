## 2. What was tried and taken out (and why)

- **A reader's beat, and the clock held while keys go on** (Ti1, "typing costs nobody time"): after the busy
  character's news the UI held the world a tenth of a second, and held its slices and its E_READY while a line's keys
  went on, so a line typed at the news landed there. It ended dq0reachstop's and dq0tiring's races, but each busy news
  cost 179,000 cycles of the world's, and in the full suite it broke dq0longwait (a wait 10 tenths short), dq0hunger,
  dq0saveout (a hang), dq0packgrope, dq0reachflee, dq0reachflee2 and dq0felled, a first letter in dq0fightcmd (1.1M at
  one point), and it made turns games part between layouts that never had (dq0engage's lines typed ahead in a fight,
  dq0reachflee's flee 3.1M cycles after its words, dq0battering, dq0handy, dq0reach). Taken out.
- **The prompt drawn only at the world's quiet; that quiet taken back by any event after it (hub_event, hub_devent);
  the world's own jobs frozen from its quiet till the next line**: each broke tests that rely on the world going on
  while the player reads (dq0hold's turns-pace job not held for keys; dq0noise, dq0flightact, dq0flightfoe and
  dq0lightden told the same bandit twice in one answer). Taken out.
- **Stirs not held for keys at the turns pace**: made dq0line tell its dwarf twice. Taken out (dens.c as it was).
- **act.fight's 1 in the quiet** ("a foe may engage it"): no quiet ever came beside a foe standing by (dq0reachflee's
  third game hung at its inventory). Taken out of the quiet.

## 4. Where the rest comes from (findings for the next round)

- **The bodies' changes after the quiet.** In dq0line the world finds its quiet twice, gives the UI `w_idle`, and then
  `den_seq` moves by 2 with no write the hub writers' hook sees (sim/test.js's sharedWrite missed it), the actors'
  `ready` fields with it: dseq_job looks again, a V_STIR, a turn, while the UI takes its line (and tells the dwarf
  twice in one answer, in one layout). Something writes the hub's bodies outside the hooked path after the minds'
  turn: a block from `actor[]` to `den_seq` (429 bytes) changes at once. Found with a step watch on the hub byte; not
  yet attributed. Until it is, a quiet can be given and then undone by later work.
- **Lines typed while the character is busy** (dq0reachstop, dq0tiring, dq0antidote's antidote at the venom's words,
  dq0engage's lines typed ahead): the gate leaves them alone by design; they land where the tasks' timing has them, as
  before. A fixed landing needs the world to wait for the keys, which cost D17 and broke tests (section 2).
- **The maker's chunks made ahead** are stocked when the maker finishes (stocks(), each pass), and a look one way's
  rest is asked three real seconds after the character's free (REST_IDLE): both the tasks' timing at the turns pace still.
- **dq0sway's "You hear something close behind you."** is followed by two bytes that move with the layout ("åN",
  "iO", "O", ",O": a pointer's bytes, $4Exx-$4Fxx): a text fault in dens.c's LF_HEARD path (inv1's to fix). It parts
  dq0sway between any two layouts.
