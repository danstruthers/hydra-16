# Closures by one code for every actor (2026-11-21; as built, for review)

The coordinator's job op2a: the one-path plan's stage 2, the closures area (ONE-PATH.md's 2.1, 2.7, 4; the act audit's
B1-B10, C3-C4, G-5, G-6, G-8, G-20). Branch op2a, from reborn-commands fbaab46c.

## What changed

1. **Portcullises and windows read the act table** (games/dq0w/acts.txt: wind, heave, raised, lower, bend, bent,
   prise, wopen, wshut). wwin.c sends every actor's noise by act_noise and takes its long tasks' times from the rows.
   wport.c's own denizen table for a portcullis is gone. The numbers are the character's, except where the character
   made no noise and a denizen did: heaving, raised and bent bars are now heard from the character too (the minds hear
   them; nothing new is played, since NZ_DOOR has no sound of its own). Every actor's winding is now 5 (a denizen's was
   7). A lowered portcullis is NZ_DOOR for the character (was NZ_WORK, which played a dig sound beside the chains).
2. **One tool rule** (wport.c's s_tool_of, which replaces s_door_key): a key that fits, or lockpicks, carried by whoever
   acts. For the character it means on it or in what it carries; for a denizen, its own things, held or in its pouch.
   A denizen's door key used to count only when held.
   - A door: a denizen's PA_UNLOCK is its key alone. Its picking is the mind's PA_PICK (a robber's kind), and it needs
     lockpicks or gets PO_NO_PICKS. B4: a robber used to pick with no tools.
   - A lid (den_pry): its key, else its lockpicks (the lidpick row and test, quiet, a minute), else forced (unless
     unawares). G-8. The lidpick row's denizen word is now DE_PICKING (it was DE_TURNS).
   - outfits.txt: lockpicks at the end of [purse bandit] and [purse banlord]. A purse line was used because [role
     bandit] is at 250 of its 255 bytes. Each line has its own die, so the other draws are unchanged.
3. **A denizen bars a door** (wport.c's den_bar): the widest bar it carries or has within reach, by the character's
   rule (things.c's bar_hand). The bar is set in the brackets on the denizen's square, IW_PORTAL. wk.link is no longer
   the picking flag. G-5.
4. **A denizen at a window** (G-6): den_portal takes T_WINDOW. It routes by s_work's choice: shutters by wdoor.c,
   bars bent and a pane worked out by wwin.c. Climbing through and breaking glass are refused for now (the climbing
   and blows branches). Shutters are told as shutters: DE_WOPENS and DE_WSHUTS (new, before DE_CLOSES), the
   wopen and wshut rows, DEN.wopens and DEN.wshuts, and dq0s plays them as a door opening and shutting.
5. **Slam** (G-20): PA_SLAM and PO_SLAMMED. "slam the door" (a new verb, VB_CLOSE_OBJECT, aux PA_SLAM; the grammar's
   close). A fleeing denizen's slam is now its mind's PA_SLAM (it was wdoor.c checking bd.doing). The words are the
   piece @slam, which DEN.slams now uses too. "slam the case" closes it. W_DIG and the codes after it moved up one.
6. **A braced door** (B8): a door that won't open though it's neither locked nor barred is tried twice. Then a hostile
   denizen forces it, and anyone else gives up. Before, it was held there for ever. A door found open resets that
   door's tries.
7. **The walk's door** (B2): a door opened on a walk is heard (the open row's E_NOISE; it was silent). A door with a
   set trap is not opened on a walk; it stops the walk, as a locked door does, and is left for the door work, which
   springs the trap.
8. **An ally's errands**: lock, bar (bar, rebar), unbar (unbar, unbolt), force and pick (OR_LOCK ... OR_PICK), at a
   door, a window or a portcullis (errand.c takes OB_WINDOW and OB_PORTC). Timed acts are posted twice (begun, then
   done), as the portcullis's raise already was. commandwords.js's order limit went from 7 to 15.
9. **The sky look on a window**: a window's sky is looked at only for the character's own work.

## Tests

New parity tests, one scene per act: the character and a denizen doing the same thing, their noise, test and effect
compared. Time is the same code (wdoor.c's 2 x step, the rows' times) and is not measured separately.
- **dq0parbar**: the character unbars and bars (the lift and bar rows at the door); an ally asked to bar sets the bar
  lying at its feet (DE_BARS by the row, played as a bar). Afterwards it lifts the bar and opens the door itself to
  follow.
- **dq0parpick**: the character's and an ally's picking of the same lock: the picking row, RS_TEST P_PICKLOCK at the
  same steps (-3, -3). With every denizen's lockpicks taken, there is no test and no picking.
- **dq0parslam**: "slam the door": PO_SLAMMED, the slam row at the door, played as a door shutting, the same row as
  the fleeing bandit's DE_SLAMS (dq0denportal).
- **dq0parlid**: a case's lock picked by the character and by a robber (den_pry): the lidpick row, P_PICKLOCK at the
  same steps (-2, -2). The robber is given the character's lockpicks as its own: the scene's bandit's purse had none
  made by then.
- **dq0parwin**: a window's shutters opened and shut by the character (the wopen and wshut rows) and by an ally
  (DE_WOPENS and DE_WSHUTS at the same window). The scene is seed 1 with dq0sky=1, the window given shutters by a
  poke. The ally's quiet opening is told or not by the UI's hearing rule, as a door's is, so only its shutting's
  words are required, and neither may be told as a door's.
- **dq0actlid re-set**: every denizen's lockpicks are taken as its first wait is typed, so its robber forces the case
  (the test's act) instead of picking it.

## Open questions

1. **The portcullis's noise numbers**: the rule was the character's numbers, but where the character made none and a
   denizen did (heaving 7, raised 8, bars bent 8), I gave both the denizen's (act1's precedent for a door burst or
   held). Winding is now the character's 5 for both (the house's said 7). Which do you want?
2. **Which robbers pick**: lockpicks went to bandits and bandit lords only. Kobolds and orcs (also robbers, kinds.txt's
   r) now can't pick and force instead; before, they picked with no tools. Should they carry picks?
3. **Lockpicks in the purse**: they are drawn as a denizen is first met, so a robber met for the first time may have
   none yet. Should they go in its role's things (the bandit's section is full: 250 of 255 bytes)?
4. **A window's climb through and its glass broken by a denizen**: left to the climbing and blows branches.
5. **A braced door forced**: wdoor.c's PA_FORCE ignores bracing, for both sides, so a hostile denizen now bursts a
   door with a chest against it. Should forcing a braced door be harder, or fail?
6. **"The bandit goes to the door."** is said for an errand at a window as well (errand.c's words).
