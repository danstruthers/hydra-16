## What was built, and why

1. **The help said whole** (5791db8b).  help.txt's lines 3, 4 and 8 were 419, 449 and 504 bytes, past help.c's
   400-byte buffer (dq0.h's lb), so each was said as two paragraphs, two of them cut mid-word ("dig d" / "own with the
   knife", "the we" / "ll", "drop d e" / "f").  help.c's o_help now reads a line a part at a time: one longer than lb
   is said to its last space, then its rest, the paragraph going on (say's gap).  games/dq0/make.js's helpParts holds
   every line to it as the build runs ("help.txt: 20 paragraphs, each printed whole (3 in parts, at a space)"): a line
   fails the build if a part has no space to end at, or "@own " is cut where two parts meet.  The first steps say "l
   is left, not look".
2. **The history's words whole** (011dd20d).  linegen.c's story put an era's first letter's place in an unsigned char:
   past 255 bytes of the story the capital went 256 bytes back ("called Gruog, aNd Wearag held it", "13 centuries ago.
   the Khulim dwarves"; 30 of 60 seeds' stories, 70000-70059, had one).  Its 240-byte buffer cut clues at 239 bytes
   ("... and their dead would not lie s"; 9 of the 60).  The place is an unsigned, the buffer 300 bytes (the bank
   program's own room, not themegen's).  themegen's tcut is set wherever generated words are cut to fit (fill's slots,
   linegen's era words and story), and "themegen: words cut short" said as it ends: dq0cut holds 24 seeds to none.

   into the light of day".  The theme format has a choice by the light outside, {day/night/dawn/dusk} or {day/night},
   chosen by the true hour as the sky shows it (the world's wtime.c's parts of the day).  theme.c's words_at gives

   endings, its way out opened and its seal's rule; the temple's endings; every generated goal's endings (goals.txt).

## The copy loop, kept aside

bank.s (said_b, said_run, and said_at shared: in place of said_b's own bank choosing; 34 bytes of main RAM):

```
_said_b:
            jsr         said_at
            lda         (ptr1),y
            stx         BANKREG
            ldx         #0
            rts

; unsigned __fastcall__ said_run (const unsigned char* p, char* d): the plain letters from byte p of the rows' banks
; (' ' to '~'), copied to d (lb), as many as there are: how many, and the byte that ended them times 256
_said_run:
            sta         ptr2
            stx         ptr2 + 1
            jsr         popax
            jsr         said_at
@l:         lda         (ptr1),y
            cmp         #$20
            bcc         @e
            cmp         #$7F
            bcs         @e
            sta         (ptr2),y
            iny
            bne         @l
@e:         stx         BANKREG
            tax
            tya
            rts

; (Byte p, .A and .X, of the rows' banks in the window: ptr1 its place there, the bank there before in .X, .Y 0)
said_at:    (said_b's own bank choosing, as it is, ending ldy #0, rts)
```
