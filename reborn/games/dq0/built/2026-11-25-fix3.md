## 1. The faults

**1. A follow's turns told twice** (dq0follow game 2, before dice2 re-aimed it). A follow's walk turns the character
without telling it. The follow then turns back to the heading it last told: "You turn to face southeast." twice in
one answer.
- The fix: a turn to the heading the last turn told, since the line was typed, is told "You turn back to face
  southeast." (said.txt's SELF.face; task.c's turned_says; main.c's face_told, cleared as a line is typed). "Back" is
  true: it turned away from that heading, untold, in between.
- The test: **dq0turnback** is dq0follow's second game as it was before dice2. At the base it fails ("You turn to face
  southeast." twice); here it tells "... You turn back to face southeast.".
- A line of several commands ("face north, turn left, face north") is several answers to the told rules (parts.c;
  ntold is cleared for each part too), so it still says "to face".

**2. "You stop waiting." three times while senseless** (dq0hoard).
- The world stops a long task at each blow that lands, even while the character lies senseless. The UI told each stop.
- The fix: a stop (TE_STOPPED, TE_HIT, TE_NEAR, TE_ENGAGED) is untold while out_told (task.c's task_words, B3). Its
  state work stays: the command's reach is still dropped.
- The world's repeated stop events are harmless, so they are left.
- The test: **dq0numb** (a frail character waiting a minute, a rat's bite rigged to fell it). At the base it reads
  "Everything goes black. You drop your torch. You stop waiting." Here the stop is untold.
- The invariant **numb**, in every test: no "You stop ...ing." after a black-out in a reply.

**3. An unseen denizen named by a social verb** (dq0cover). The denizens' sway (dq0d.c's meant) chose the nearest
denizen of the name "on a square it sees or within four of it". Within four, unseen, it answered by its name.
- The fix: the one meant must be in the senses' record (what the UI names by), or be an ally of the character's own
  within earshot (12 squares, as an order to every ally reaches; known by the name it was given).
- Every named SW_* goes through meant: look, talk, persuade, deceive, threaten, calm, befriend, wake, taunt, order,
  name and give. The market's comes by the goods found; a capital comes by its letter; the crowd's by earshot.
- The UI's own first words still say "You look for the rat." for these verbs whether the rat is seen or not (the
  resolver doesn't place a denizen for them). That is left; see question 3.

**4. Club credited for a bare-handed block** (dq0grapple). The referee's defence remembers, for each foe, the slot
that defended it this round. With the torch let fall, a foe met again got the torch's slot, so its skill (Club)
was marked, while the words ("your fists") were the bare arm's.
- The fix: a slot no longer held (T_HELD) frees that defence, and another is chosen from what's held now (gear.c's
  pc_defence).
- Every attack checked: the world puts SK_BEST in place of a named weapon as soon as it's let fall (wlimbs.c) or thrown
  (wshoot.c), and pc_side otherwise takes the held list. Nothing to change there.
- Room: dq0harn's overlay 3 had 88 bytes. w_damage moved to overlay 6 for it.

**5. Iron keys loose on two bandits** (dq0kit, dids 18 and 20). A lair's door keys (wport.c's door_keys) are made for
its first denizen before its kit, and filed on it neither worn nor held: in nothing.
- The fix: as the kit is made, those keys go into the pouch on its belt (wlimbs.c's keys_home, in part den_limbs,
  after den_kit). With no pouch, they lie at its lair's home, its faction's.
- A rats' lair's keys now lie there, so dq0nodig and dq0dulled see "an iron key" at their feet. An orc lair's lie by
  its table (dq0peoples).
- The invariant **loose**, in every test: a denizen's own thing neither worn nor held. It found another case, which is
  left (question 2).
- A first try put the keys after the kit, with a pouch's fit tested (part things). That moved the background's phase
  and failed dq0fightmid's "d, played" (791K), so it was dropped.

**6. A fourth sound repeated** (dq0time). Four sounds are kept in mind a line (main.c's heard_sum[4], heard_rep's eight
bits), not three: 2 bytes of the UI's main RAM.
- dq0time's squeak is told once, then "again", then "again and again".
- The invariant **heard**: no sound told whole twice in a reply. At the base it flags dq0time.
- A fifth distinct sound can still push one out of mind. The invariant would catch that.

**7. A look made whole overran lb.** Only a look with names given was made whole, so names_look could put the room's
name into its first sentence afterwards.
- The fix: every look now streams. Its room's name goes into the first sentence as that sentence is said (look.c's

- So lb holds a single sentence as the look is made, and nothing can run into lbe. Nothing is cut, so there is no
  "rest for the next telling" to keep.
- I tried a hard stop in each overlay's add first, then a shared add in an overlay. The first overflowed overlay 2 (76

- In dq0names, dq0lightden and dq0parwin, a look with names now tells denizens that lb's room used to drop. Its lines
  also wrap at other places.

**8. A copy's critical failure.** HOUSE.md's reading and clues: "a critical failure, a wrong reading told just as
confidently: its words with the theme's own things' names swapped about (in its own dungeon)".
- read.c swapped the names only for the writing itself (`here` excluded CLUE_COPY). So a copy read in its own dungeon
  gave the true words and the wrong place. The tablet read again afterwards gave the swapped words: two tellings of one
  kept reading.
- This doesn't match the record, so it's fixed: a copy read in its own dungeon swaps its names as well. The critical
  success's "line more" stays the writing's own.
- dq0reading's copy game is rigged to a critical failure now (it was a failure; G12 covers that grade). It checks that
  the copy is told with the names swapped and the wrong place, and that the tablet read after it says the same.

## 2. Tests and engineering

**9. One list.** tests/lib/dq0.js's DQ0_REPEATS is now the repeat invariant's list too. Its entries told as they go are
marked `goes`, and sim/lib/dq0inv.js takes those (`goes()`) in place of its own GOES.
- The told-twice check is unchanged.
- The invariant's exemptions are the ones it had, plus the fight's words ("It falls senseless.", flees, falls dead).
  "The way rises" has an entry of its own, so the passage's other beats stay held to three.

**10. dq0hidelook** reads RS_ASLEEP from the request's own flags (`e.ask.asleep`). Before, it read the reply's w2,
which a felling overwrites.

**11. The rouse as its own request: not done, and why.**
- At the turns pace, turns_run takes the clock up to the character's pc_ready, which is its next roll's moment. The
  denizens' turn runs at that moment, before the world's next pass asks RS_ROUSE.
- So a blow there must find the rolls made already. That is why rf_answer rolls them first, under whatever request
  comes.
- Keying them as RS_ROUSE inside another request means swapping the slot's request and keying twice. It also means
  saving and restoring the generator, the rig's hook and its dkey state, which is about 100 bytes of dq0harn's main RAM
  (115 over its floor). Keying twice would also spend a rig's uses twice.
- The way that's cheap in room is to roll them only under the world's RS_ROUSE, at the top of each pass before the
  denizens' turns. That needs turns_run to stop short of pc_ready. turns_run is lock2's to change (the brief), so I
  left it.
- I also tried zeroing the world's RS_ROUSE ask's stale other/a/b, so its key depends on nothing else. That moved
  dq0venom's fight dice and killed its character, so it is reverted.

**12. Cycle limits.** Every test whose `cycles` limit sat under 1.5 times its base measure now has about 1.5 times it,
rounded up: 80 tests, including dq0mine at 4,500M and dq0save at 1,300M. Five are expressions (dq0closest, dq0leftovers,
dq0start0/1, dq0scene). Budgets are unchanged. X3 is out of OPEN.md's backlog.

**Re-aimed tests, by S1** (a social verb's hearer perceived; the behaviour right):
- dq0follow, dq0sway, dq0fetch, dq0fetchlid, dq0regard coaxed the rat a second time out of sight. "You look for the
