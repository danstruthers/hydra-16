# A thing thrown out of the hand (2026-11-20; as built, for review)

Branch throw1 from ee963180 (hear1 merged). The user's playtest: "when I threw my dagger at an enemy, the game thought
I still had the dagger in hand". Their symptom, given after: the thrown dagger missed (was dodged), the denizen
attacked in the next round, and the character's defence blocked with the dagger it no longer had.

## 1. How it reproduced (dq0throwheld, fight.js)

The hunter (hmchar seed 5) at seed 3 with -k dagger (a dagger in its right hand, its outfit's at its belt), a giant
rat set hostile two squares west, "defend block", "throw the dagger at the closest rat". Before the fix:

- **The item store was right.** The dagger lay on the floor past the rat (IW_SQUARE, I_HELD off, no one's), and
  "i" listed no dagger in a hand.
- **The referee was wrong.** No RS_EQUIP was sent after the throw: dq0harn's own record of the character's tools
  (its marks, `>dagger3M`) still had the dagger in its right hand. The rat bit, the fight went on, and the world's
  in_hand (RS_WIELD, the hands empty, so SK_BEST) let the referee choose: it chose the dagger. "You strike at the
  giant rat with your dagger, and miss.", "you fend it off with your dagger", every round, the dagger lying on the
  floor. This is the user's block with the dagger.
- **The UI's copy was wrong.** The world's ix_make never ran, so uirec's ix still had the dagger CX_HELD: "hold the
  dagger" after the throw began "You lift the dagger." (the UI's first words at once, fw2-fw5's) before "You go over
  to the dagger."
- The hub's pc_strike was left SK_OF (dagger) (harmless: fighting on asks in_hand each round; reset now all the same,
  as drop_held does).
- Hit or miss, near or far, turns pace or normal: the same. The throw at a thing ("throw the dagger at the bones", a
  throw a way) was right all along: it goes by things.c's IO_THROW and the UI's after(), which tells the referee
  (to_hand) and makes ix.

The cause: wshoot.c's s_pc_shot (part 67) moved the thrown thing out of the hand by mfall (part 68) and then told the
referee only for a launcher's shot (`if (!why && !thr) equip_now ()`), and nobody made the UI's copy again. A
denizen's throw (den_shot, by the same fire) told the referee nothing either: its dwr (the referee's weapon held, gear.c)
kept the spear it had thrown.

## 2. The fix (dq0w: wshoot.c, wlimbs.c, wfetch.c)

- **fire()**, the one routine every shot and throw goes through, character's and denizen's: once the missile has
  fallen, the shooter's hands are told. The character: equip_now (RS_EQUIP 2: what's to hand, its load), pc_strike
  SK_BEST if it was the thing thrown, light_now after a throw (a torch thrown; it makes ix), ix_make after a shot (an
  arrow the fewer: the counted first words). A denizen: den_equip after a throw. The old launcher-only equip_now in
  s_pc_shot is gone (fire does it).
- **A denizen's last thing.** s_den_equip returned untold when the denizen had no things left (its kit empty), so the
  referee kept the last one in hand. A flag (den_eq_all, one byte of dq0w's main RAM) makes it tell an empty hand,
  only from the paths that take a thing out of its hands and only for a denizen with things of its own (bd.did): a
  beast with none stays the bestiary's, as before.

The same bug elsewhere (step 2 of the job: every way a held thing leaves the hands):

| path | character | denizen |
|---|---|---|
| throw at a denizen (wshoot.c fire) | **fixed** (no RS_EQUIP, no ix) | **fixed** (no RS_EQUIP) |
| shot, an arrow spent | ix not made (**fixed**); RS_EQUIP was sent | load not told (none told for denizens) |
| launcher taken up to shoot (s_pc_shot) | RS_EQUIP sent; ix not made (**fixed**) | den_draw: told |
| launcher let fall before a melee blow (den_unbow) | - | **fixed** (no RS_EQUIP) |
| throw at a thing, a throw a way (IO_THROW) | right: after() tells, light_now | - |
| drop, put, give, stow (things.c, after()) | right: to_hand and light_now | - |
| a fumble (drop_held, den_drop) | right | right, but its last thing untold (**fixed**, den_eq_all) |
| robbed (den_rob) | right (equip_now, light_now); pc_strike not reset (harmless) | - |
| an ally's errand: takes, gives, is given (wfetch.c) | right (equip_now, light_now) | **fixed**: the ally's hands never told (hold(), FE_GIVE) |
| used up, filled, shut (things.c) | ix_make; held state unchanged | - |

## 3. What the player sees differently

- After a thing thrown at a denizen, the character fights on with what it still holds (fists, if nothing):
  "You strike at the giant rat with your fists", "you fend it off with your fists", not "with your dagger".
- "hold the dagger" after it's thrown: "You go over to the dagger." without "You lift the dagger." first.
- A denizen that threw its spear blocks and strikes with what it still holds (its referee told), or draws.
- An ally that hands over or takes up a thing: its referee record follows (a weapon given away no longer struck with).

Sentences changed in the suite: see section 5.
