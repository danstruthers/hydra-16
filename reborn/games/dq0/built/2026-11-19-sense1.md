# The senses' one path: sense (looker, target) for every actor (2026-11-19; as built, for review)

The coordinator's job sense1: the one-path plan's stage 1, the senses' part (ONE-PATH.md's 2.2, 3, 4;
audit/2026-11-17-sense.md), under the user's rule that the character and every denizen sense the world by the same rules
and code. Branch sense1 began from e995c535 at 07:51. It was rebased onto e492b66a (e10, act1), then onto 60f42c66
(speed6, bankcheck, the images).

## Built

- **One routine, sense (looker, target)** (dq0p/sense.c's s_sense_actor, the hub's dsense): the target is the
  character or another actor (the hub's new `dsense_whom`: 0 the character, otherwise an actor's entry + 1). It reads
  the target's body, its position (low and hidden) and its carried light (the hub's sens).
  - **Sight:** range from the kind's sheet. Lit means the target's own light or the light layer where it stands.
    Darkvision is less while the eyes are still used to more light. A low, unlit target is seen 3/4 as far (prone 1/2).
    The line is the target's eyes' line (LN_SIGHT).
  - **A carried light seen far off:** every looker sees one by eyes.c's glow_reach (the character's look already did).
    Before this, a denizen saw the character's torch only within its kind's sight.
  - **Hearing:** one formula for every hearer, (loudness - background) x hearing x wind against twice the way's cost. With
    the character at either end the cost is the sound layer's exact way. Between two denizens the noise goes along the
    straight line the air passes (the user's, 2026-11-17). Another actor's noise is its steps while it's busy.
  - **Smell:** the same nose rule for any target. Between two denizens, along the air's straight line.
- **A senses record per actor** (the hub's `sens[1 + NACTORS]`, 7 bytes each, 63 in all, appended to the hub; owned by
  the senses). Each record holds sight, darkvision, hearing, the eyes' rate, nose, its light's reach, and flags
  (AS_LISTEN, AS_SET).
  - The character's record is filled as it looks (dq0p.c's s_sens_pc, part 7). A denizen's is filled as its mind asks.
  - The maker's chooser (dq0m/ahead.c) reads each actor's own sight, lit or dark, whichever reaches further. It used 9
    (the most any kind has) for every denizen. With no record yet, it still uses the most a kind has (22 squares).
- **unlit_at goes to the senses.** The world's s_unlit_at (dq0w.c, part 45) now asks the senses (the look request,
  LK_LIT: dq0p's s_lit_ask and s_lit_sq). A square is lit by the light layer, or by a carried light within its reach
  with a clear line. Before, it was lit only if the character had seen it lit (T_VIS) or it was in a room's own light.
  - The look's wx, wy, wd and ch are saved and put back.
  - While the world's own look is posted, its waiting clock can move a body that asks. The senses may then be waiting
    on the world, and the game may be ending. In both cases the old rule answers (T_VIS, or a room's light).
- **The character's look keeps its grid, by the same rule per square.**
  - Its darkvision is cut by its eyes' adaptation, as a denizen's is (view.c, part 1).
  - A low, unlit denizen past 3/4 of its darkvision (prone, 1/2) isn't seen, as a denizen's rule has it (dens.c with
    part 6's s_low_far).

## Left out, and why

- **Facing (the audit's S1).** Facing alone failed 8 tests: dq0fight, dq0grapple, dq0fightverbs, dq0disarm, dq0morale,
  dq0flee, dq0follow and dq0hidelook. The tests didn't fail on words; the denizens behaved differently. The minds never
  turn to look (the audit's S14), so a watcher stays blind to whatever its last step left behind it, and a foe beside
  it loses the character. Facing needs the minds to turn, and to sense again when they do (dq0d, the gw1 work).
  sense.c's header says so.
- **The minds hearing another actor's noise by the one rule.** act1's act_noise sends an actor's noise as an E_DEN. The
  minds still hear it by `apart > K.hear * loud / 2`, with no background, walls or line.
  - Built and tried: dq0d's hear() asked the senses with `dsense_whom` = the source, keeping the old reach as the cheap
    test first.
  - It failed 6 tests, each for its own reason:
    - dq0letsight hung: inventory's first letter never came.
    - dq0disarm stopped short of its end.
    - dq0denportal: the door was left open.
    - dq0grille: a rat stopped the walk, so the script lost its way.
    - dq0scent: its check thinks every dsense's target is the character.
    - dq0looks: 48 more replies.
  - I took it out of the branch. A hang needs finding first, and dq0d is gw1's. The patch is kept for whoever does it
    (a dozen lines in hear()). The senses' side is ready for it.
- **The UI's hearing of an E_DEN** (caps.c's o_den_heard) is still its own copy of the formula. It uses the wall count
  carried in the event instead of the sound layer's cost, and has no wind. Moving it onto the senses means a request
  from the UI. That's left.
- **A grille both ways (S9)** is line.inc's ln_portal and the world's. Not done.
- **"sniff" and "track" for anyone** are the world's verbs (dq0w). Not done.
