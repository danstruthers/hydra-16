## Where the pause went

The look across's longest pause runs from "You peer ahead." to its one sentence, "Down the passage ahead you see 20 feet
of it so far." I traced the menacing look across at seed 1 on 8d74ef6a with every task's cycles in 100,000-cycle
buckets from the Enter.

- The world tells "You peer ahead." at about 300,000 cycles. The senses begin the look at 473,000 and reply at
  1,911,000. Through that span they have 80-97% of the CPU. The maker had 4% in two buckets and the minds 7-8% in two.
- The look: the character was put where it stands as it listened, so its last look doesn't stand. The whole look one way
  is done at once (LC_WHOLE) and nothing is told until it's done.
- The senses' 1.26M cycles in it, by phase:
  - view: 594K;
  - perceive: 209K;
  - kept: 141K;
  - things_lamps: 120K;
  - way_seen: 94K;
  - glows: 43K;
  - sync_strata, with sens_pc: 42K.
- Of the 1.26M, 233K is the swapped parts being copied in: part 7, then 1, 4, 2 and 3, one each.
- kept's 141K before the reply was runs() (view.c's passage ways' runs) and its part 4 copied in for them (39K). The
  runs are for a glance's words about a passage's ways (sight.c's S.way shapes, words.c's). A look one way tells its own
  record's words (way.c's WAY_SEEN), not the glance's. Its memory of the squares was already kept after its reply
  (kept_owed, 2026-11-18).
- The spread is bimodal. In the high mode, the chunk on the line ahead was made ahead during the player's pause, so view
  casts into it (cast_row, grid_fill: view 591K). In the low mode it wasn't made, so view stops at its edge (300K) and
  the rest is told later. Which mode a run gets depends on the timing, so on seed and layout.
