## 1. dq0scene: the same sound told again and again while idle (feaefbc5)

**The fault.** In s_noise_told (main.c, part positions), the repeat rule tells a sound once, then "..., again.", then
"..., again and again.", then nothing more. It worked only `if (busy ...)`, and heard_rep was cleared at E_READY. So
the sounds heard while the character stood idle between commands were each told in full. In dq0scene, a rat squeaked
seven times in one reply.

**The fix.** The count now runs from one command typed to the next. It is no longer cleared at E_READY, and the
`busy` condition is gone. A command's busy stretch and the idle time after it, up to the next command, are one count.
That is what the invariant counts: one reply runs from one line's echo to the next.
- **When it's cleared.** heard_rep is cleared when a command is first taken. A line held for the busy character clears
  it as it's typed, not again when the character comes to it.
- **A soft question's answer** ("yes" to "Keep waiting?") doesn't clear it (a87752c9). The task goes on, so its
  sounds go on with "again". The first full run cleared it there too: dq0tasks's scurrying, already told, was told in
  full again after the "yes". Now dq0tasks reads as it did at 89aea28b.

**Checked.**
- dq0scene's idle squeaks: "You hear a squeak to your left." and "... ahead to your left." each told once, then
  ", again.", then ", again and again.", then no more till "look" is typed. Before: 13 and 7 in full.
- dq0time's 240 squeaks of a two hours' sleep are still its three sentences (the same three as at 89aea28b).
- The UI's main RAM: 3,213 -> 3,200, the command's own clearing (200 over its floor).

The invariant is right as it stands. It is not loosened.

## 2. dq0sleepsenses: a scene posted during a turn waited thirty seconds (cc250b98)

**The failure.** "At rest, awake, a denizen seen not asked." The test puts a bandit beside the character as it
settles to rest. The bandit came into place only after the hour's rest was over, so the rest was never broken off
to ask.

**The cause.** A deadlock between scene1's mechanism and the turns pace, broken only by a 30-second timeout:
- At the turns pace, the world's `turn()` rings the minds and waits up to TURN_WAIT (30 s: 107M cycles) for their
  turn (den_done).
- While a scene is pending, the minds are held (`held()`: scene != scene_d). They go round to their loop's top, where
  they can do their part only after the world has done its part (scene_m).
- The world does its part only at the top of its own main loop. While it is inside `turn()`, it never gets there.

So a scene posted while the world is in a turn waits the full 30 s. After that the world counts the minds late and
skips their turns till the next command.

With DQ0SCENE=1, at 89aea28b each of the test's scenes posted in a turn took about 107.5M cycles to be in place
(403.6M -> 511.0M as the character slept, 610.3M -> 718.1M as it rested).

**Why only together.** The stall is there in scene1 alone too: its "beside" scenes took 107.7M and 107.9M there. But
there the bandit's world ops were in the scene's first (and only) part, so the world put the bandit in place at once,
and only the minds' bookkeeping waited.

In the merge, beside()'s scene also has a minds' op (SO_REC, the bandit's record). So scene1's own commit 8f907473
("the minds' facts first") splits the scene in two:
- the minds' part first, which stalled 30 s;
- then the world's part, the bandit's place, which came after the rest had ended.

inv1's changes didn't change the mechanism. They only changed the record bytes the scene finds and the moment it
lands. None of inv1's text or food changes is involved.

**The fix.** `turn()`'s wait now does the world's part of a pending scene, as the main loop's top does. Like the main
loop's top, the wait holds no intent's body (it already carries out den_intents and the UI's things there). The world then rings the minds, which do their part and take their turn. With the fix,
dq0sleepsenses's scenes are in place within about 100,000 cycles, and the rest is broken off: "Something stirs. (C) A
bandit, armed and armoured, is to your left, 5 feet away, watching you. Keep waiting?"

No test's scene is re-set. dq0sleepsenses stays on tests/noise.txt: that entry is a different race (the UI's naps,
start1). This failure wasn't that race.

The world's main RAM: 2,231 -> 2,214.

## 3. dq0harn's room: 4-byte overlay stubs (6e34cc4f)

dq0harn had 59 overlay stubs (OVFN). Each was `jsr ovcall`, the overlay's number and the function's address: 6 bytes
of main RAM. Now each is `jsr ovcall` and a stub number: 4 bytes.
- **The tables.** The overlay and the function's address go in three tables of a byte a stub: OVSTO, OVSTL and
  OVSTH, segments in dq0harn.cfg, in overlay 9's bank.
- **ovcall** keeps the caller's bank first, as before. Then it puts overlay 9's bank in the window, reads the three
  bytes, and puts in the function's overlay. It is the same scheme as the swapped parts' SWSTUBOV (room7, room8).
- **The cost.** ovcall is 7 bytes longer and about 14 cycles a call slower.
- **The guard.** The macro stops the build at more than 256 stubs.
- **parts.js** counts the tables in their bank for the packer, as it counts SWSTP's. It writes ovfn.inc from the same
  list.

OPEN.md's R7 named "4-byte stubs in dq0harn" for the swapped parts' 20 stubs, about 24 bytes net. The overlay stubs
were the bigger win, so the swapped parts' stubs are left as they are.

## 4. dq0sway: a look ran past lb's end (8cae8d75)

The second full run failed dq0sway on inv1's stop invariant: "hear something close behind you.O".

**The cause.** A look made whole (not streamed, because a name was given: Grub) ran past the end of lb (400 bytes).
o_den_words added " You hear something close behind you." with no room check, so the text's end met lbe, which lies
just past lb. lbe's own bytes (a pointer, 0x4Fxx: "\x14O" here, "\x07O" at 89aea28b) were then said after the full
stop. 89aea28b's transcript has the same stray bytes. The BEL byte between "." and "O" kept the invariant from seeing
them; my changes moved the UI's statics, so the BEL became 0x14 and the invariant saw it. I found it with a dump of
the UI's lb as the stray byte went out.

**The fix.** The sentence is added only while 40 bytes of lb are free. Otherwise it isn't kept as told, so the next
stir tells it. The denizens' own sentences in a look were already told "as there's room in it". In dq0sway's look it no
longer fits, so that one look says one sentence fewer, and no stray bytes. The UI's overlay 9 goes from 904 to 877
bytes free.

**Not fixed.** The rest of a look made whole has no room check either. A long enough place could still run past lb's
end. A room check on every add of a look made whole is engineering for a later round (section 12).

## 11. Assumptions (each a rule's)

- **A sound is told at most three times from one command typed to the next, busy or idle:** once, "again", "again
  and again", then not again till a command is typed. A soft question's answer goes on with its task's count
  (WORLD-MODEL.md section 7: nothing told twice; P3, inv1's rule for a busy stretch holds for the idle one too).
- **A heard sentence that doesn't fit in a look waits for the next stir,** as the look's denizens are told only as
  there's room (section 7; S1).
- **A test's scene is put in place whenever the world waits on the minds** (section 8).
- **A line held behind a fight's round that takes it over is a command in hand,** as a line typed with the character
  free is. The give-ways (P7, budgets) hold for it until its news says something. P2: no actor acts other than by the
  same rules. The denizens' intents wait for it at most GW_CMD, as for any command.
- **The world takes the next intent only once the UI has seen the news,** while a command is in hand at the real
  paces (P7). The news is told first; no game time passes for it.
- **The quit is taken whatever,** the character senseless and busy too (the user's, 2026-11-13).
