## 1. L1: a test's scene and the look at the bodies it moved

even1's lead was a mind's intent carried out at a pass of the world's, outside a turn. The dumps (DQ0DUMP, even1's
part2.js and tl.js) of dq0denerrands, dq0dentakein and dq0thingact in the as-built and 91-byte layouts put the parting
at one moment in each: a **test's scene** (tests/lib/dq0.js's dq0Scene) that moves bodies and posts an intent "as its
mind would". A scene is done by its owners in turn: the world's part (SO_HUB: the bodies placed, den_seq), the maker's,
then the minds' (SO_INTENT posted, scene_d). The world went on with its pass while the minds were at their part, so its
look at the moved bodies (dseq_job's E_VIEW V_STIR) came before the scene's intent was carried out in one layout and
after it in the other:
- dq0dentakein game 0: the view first, the bandit seen as it took the crowns ("The bandit takes the crowns from the
  sack.", then a turn at what was told), or the take first, the bandit not yet seen, untold;
- dq0denerrands game 0: the don seen ("The bandit puts on their mail hauberk." told once more) or not;
- dq0thingact: "The bandit puts their dagger in the sack." once (even1's run at b9e9565a).

Outside a scene the minds post nothing outside their turn at the turns pace (dq0d.c's main: act only in a turn) but a
sway's answer (the UI's request, hub_call), which is posted before the minds reply, so before the UI's own intent in
every timing. The scene was the one path.

**The fix** (1b31d6e5, wscene.c's whole; dq0w.c's sc_look): at the turns pace a scene with a part of the minds' is put
in place whole before the world goes on: the world, its part done, waits for scene_d (thirty seconds at most, a turn's
wait; the intents' ring carried out only if it's full, the minds waiting on it), and holds the intents posted for the
scene through that pass (den_held's, as main's job alone; cleared after the late jobs): the look at the moved bodies
first, then what they do, as a mind's intent follows the look at the bodies it saw. That was the as-built layout's order
in dq0dentakein (the take told, as the test's own description says) and the 91-byte layout's in dq0denerrands.

**The check**: dq0lockerrand (tests/suites/dq0/denizens.js), dq0dentakein's first game played six times with the same
lines, a task slowed through the scene's line in five of them (each of its instructions 3 or 20 cycles the longer in the
minds, 3, 8 or 20 in the world, IRQs on: the cpu's step hook): the six stories the same sentence for sentence, and the
world's events, the denizens' doings and the referee's answers in the same order; the crowns taken in each, told in the
first. At db6be65d games 5 and 6 (the world slowed 8 and 20) part from game 1, the take untold; with the fix all six are
the same, as built and in both fillers. (Line lags, dq0lockwake's way, didn't part them: every task is idle as the
scene's posted, so a lag moves them all together.)
