## 3. Not done: the felled foe's band, robustly

The coordinator's failing path (729,662, 101%): a foe felled whose shock rolls can still succeed, but hardly ever
(UP within three of its Endurance). The giant rat's Endurance is 7 (Strength 3, Stamina 8, Will 9), so UP 5 to 7.
wake rolls its rounds one by one, up to 359 × UP generator steps, each some 250 cycles with its die: up to 630,000
cycles of the referee's on the blow, before its words.

Exact (the same dice, the same stream after) and robust can't both be had:

- **The floor of an exact method:** any exact method must find every round's first number. Rounds are UP numbers apart,
  so at best one matrix (T^UP) applied a round:
  - nibble tables: some 400 cycles a round;
  - byte tables (4KB): some 250;
  - T^UP's columns built first: some 60,000.
  - The best is some 150,000-200,000 cycles for the worst band (UP 7).
- **The ordinary path:** a line typed mid-round already costs 500,000-550,000 to its first letter (the referee's blow
  250,000, the UI's words 100,000, the parser's and the console's). So the band's worst still can't be held under
  90% whichever way the race falls.
- **Taking the rounds out off the blow's answer:** the world needs RS_ATTACK_OUT at once (the denizen's ready time,
  bd.ready; the character's pc_ready; the words' out). Answering first and sending the out time later is a protocol
  change, and the world's acts between the two would differ.

This wants the user's decision. Options:

1. A shock roll for a senseless foe as its time comes (each round, or each minute as the book may have it), the world
   asking, not an hour's rolled at once. The dice change: later rolls, interleaved with the rest.
2. The out time sent after the blow's answer (the world's down denizen's ready patched), if its acts between can be
   shown the same.
3. Keep the dice and accept the band's worst over 90%, or the band rolled by T^UP tables (some 200,000 at worst).
