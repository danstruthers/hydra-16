## 1. dq0letdens' played game (tests/lib/dq0.js, denizens.js)

"attack A, played" failed D17 at 1,538,245 cycles, the same number on every build since the throw1 layout:
deterministic, not a race. Traced (a profile of each attack line, the UI's busy flag and the hub's pc_task and
pc_going at each Enter):

- The line wasn't typed while the character was busy. Its prompt only comes once the character is free, and at its
  Enter the UI's busy, pc_task and pc_going were all 0.
- **The cause: the played game's scene was never set.** dq0PlayLetters types the test's lines in the played game's own
  letters ("attack C" typed as "attack B" there, the rats met in another order), and dq0Poke matched its scene by the
  line as typed. So the scene, keyed to "attack C", never fired: no rats put beside the character senseless. "attack
  B" walked over to a live rat, the rats mauled the character, it was knocked out and came round in shock. The next
  attack ("attack A", typed as "attack B") was refused ("You're in shock: you can do no more than rest, or walk slowly
  away."). Its first letter waited 1.5M cycles because the world and the referee were busy with the character coming
  round (in the window: dq0harn 552,000 cycles, the world 409,000, part swaps 296,000 of that).
- **The fix:**
  - dq0Poke now matches a played game's line by the test's own words (m.dq0Orig, dq0PlayLetters' record of each
    rewritten line), and so does its hearts record. So the played game's scene is the same as the other two games'.
    Only dq0letdens uses dq0Poke together with dq0PlayLetters (gear.js's five letter tests use no pokes).
  - As briefed, **dq0WhenFree** (tests/lib/dq0.js, new) holds each line at its prompt while the character is busy (the
    UI's busy, or a long task: fighting on, walking, pc_task, pc_going), the rest of the serial port's tick going on.
    It changed nothing in this run (the prompt already waits), but it is the guarantee the brief asked for.
- After: "attack A, played" 530,585 (the scene's rat finished where it lies). No check was narrowed.
