## 1. The mechanism (games/dq0/hub.h's scene)

- **The request.** The tests' harness (tests/lib/dq0.js) writes ops at SCENE_AT ($1800) in CMD_BANK, then the scene's
  number in hub.scene. hub.scene's bit 7, SCENE_LINE, marks a scene posted at a line's Enter.
  - An op is its kind, its payload's length, then the payload. SO_END comes after the last.
  - CMD_BANK past the command's reply was unused room.
  - Bank 0 gains 10 bytes: scene, scene_w, scene_d, scene_m, sc_said and the five sc_* numbers. The header is now
    6,013 bytes, and 2,048 stay free.
- **Each owner applies its own facts, at the top of its own loop**, where no copy of the facts is in hand:
  1. **The world** (dq0w/wscene.c), before anything else in its pass:
     - SO_HUB: the hub bytes the hub.h writers' table gives the world;
     - SO_WRAM: its own main RAM;
     - SO_DUE: a job made due (DU_BURN sets burn_ask; DU_TRACE and DU_HOLD set tr_n);
     - SO_RS: forwarded to the referee as rs.h's RS_SCENE in its slot (SCN_OUT, WELL, HURT, LAME, ATT, TIRE, RAM, HUB).
       dq0harn answers all of them (hscene.c, a part); dq0null answers SCN_OUT for the character, plus RAM and HUB.

     Then the world sets scene_w. It also sets scene_m, and scene_d too, when the scene has no part for the maker or
     the minds.
  2. **The maker** (dq0m.c's part scene): SO_MHUB, a chunk's own bytes in the cache. Then it sets scene_m.
  3. **The minds** (dq0d.c's part scene):
     - SO_DHUB: an actor's mind's fields in the hub;
     - SO_REC: their records' bank (den.h's struct pop);
     - SO_DRAM: their own main RAM;
     - SO_INTENT: an intent posted by post(), as a mind posts one.

     Then they set scene_d. Until it's set, held() is true, so a pass is broken off at the next actor.
- **A line typed waits for its own scene** (main.c's s_scene_wait, a part).
  - It waits before the line is posted to the parser, and before ui_cmd is set.
  - While it waits it rings the world, the maker and the minds, and lets the keys' hold go.
  - The world's den_held lets the minds' posted intents through, so their settle ends.
  - The world's clock stands still (clock_run), as it did for a poke.
  - A scene that isn't a line's (dq0jobs' and dq0hold's SO_DUE) isn't waited for.
- **Swapped parts, not main RAM.** Each task's code is in a swapped part named scene (5 new parts), so it's in main RAM
  only during a scene. The main code adds only the check and the stub call.
- **A test's start: dq0 -S FILE.** The file is a line per fact: hour, sky, air, well, furn. The UI's game_args part
  reads it into hub.sc_*.
  - The world (wtime.c's begun, wwell.c's scene), the minds (s_hour) and the maker read the hub, not the environment.
    The maker reads a main-RAM copy taken at its start, since the readers are overlays that can't map the hub.
  - The environment's dq0hour, dq0sky, dq0air, dq0well and dq0furn are gone, along with their ENV_GET code.
  - dq0auto stays. It's read by dq0acct, which runs between dungeons with no hub and is on hold.
- **Offsets from the build.** The minds' record structs moved from dq0d.c to dq0d/den.h, which hubgen.js now reads
  (HEADERS, and evaluate's includes), so dq0Rec's offsets are the build's. SAVED.source points there; the declaration
  text is the same, so SAVE_VER is unchanged at 22961. HUB_VERSION is the build's.
  - dq0.js's CHUNK_SIZE, CK_TILE/ROOM, WORLD_BANK_N, CSLOT_AT, the cache's first bank, the stratum record's size and
    the portal offsets (218, 219, 14) all come from hubgen. The task table's offset 12 is gone as well.

## 2. The tests' side (tests/lib/dq0.js)

- **`dq0Scene(m, specs)`** takes dq0Poke's specs unchanged.
  - The spec code and the tests' own `call(h, L, K, at, named, sc)` run on COPIES of the hub's banks
    (`m.dq0.bank`, `m.sharedBanks`), the minds' records (`dq0Rec`), and the world's, the minds' and the referee's main
    RAM (`m.taskRam`).
  - Each byte changed becomes its owner's op, routed by hub.h's writers' table (sim/lib/hubown.js's map): world,
    maker, minds, or the referee (SCN_HUB, SCN_RAM). A byte of a region none of them owns throws, naming the region.
  - `sc` holds the referee's facts by name: `sc.out(k, till)`, `well`, `hurt`, `lame`, `att`, `tire`; and
    `sc.due('burn' | 'trace' | 'hold')`.
  - The minds' own facts are posted first, in a scene of their own, then the rest. That way the senses' look, which
    den_seq bids, sees the new names and attitudes (dq0fightverbs).
- **When it's in place.** The harness sees it as the recorder's hook watches the minds' write of scene_d. Then:
  - m.hearts and the line's snapshot (dq0Turns' `at`) are taken again;
  - dq0Timer measures that line from the scene in place, as a poke cost no time;
  - dq0Check (every test) fails on a scene never put in place.
- **`dq0SceneNow(m, fn, after, why, enter)`** posts a scene from any hook. dq0Intent and dq0PcShock now work only inside
  a scene.
- **Removed:** dq0Poke and dq0Sheet. dq0Aware and dq0BodyPoke (fight.js) now post scenes.
- **`dq0Game({ char, pre, theme, scene, game, lines, end })`** writes the prelude once. Where mechanical, 321 test
  inputs now use it, each one's typed input byte-identical, checked by script. dq0Typed adds `-S /ram/s.sc` to every
  dq0 line after a scene file's echo line.
- **dq0Turns' bare** also strips a test's own `-R N` that comes before `-S` (27 lines; the coordinator's fix).

The conversion of the suites was split among four subagents (conv-den, conv-fight, conv-gear, conv-misc), one per
group of files. Their logs and transcripts are in

## 7. Assumptions

- **A scene takes no game time** (the world's clock stands still while a line waits for its scene). A poke took none,
  and a test's setup isn't play (WORLD-MODEL.md section 8: tests are the engineer's; Ti1 is untouched, since no actor
  acts meanwhile).
- **RS_SCENE in rs.h.** It's an appended, test-only request, like RS_DICE. A ruleset answers what it has; the rest gets
  0xFF, as rs.h's unknown-request rule already says. The skeleton itself isn't changed. The facts are named in each
  ruleset's own terms (P6). SCN_RAM and SCN_HUB are raw, test-only escapes.
- **A line's scene may let the keys' hold go during its wait.** Nothing of the player's is in hand then: the line is in,
  and its command isn't yet.

## 8. Open questions

1. **Senses' scene part.** The senses have no scene part, so dq0heading's lgen bump stays a raw write. Is a fifth
   owner's part worth its room (the senses' main RAM is 2,290, 242 over its floor)? The rule I'd add: "every task that
   owns state a test sets has a scene part".
2. **Overrides of a derived summary.** dq0blind and dq0darklook hold C_BLINDED in the referee's summary. The clean way
   is a ruleset state, blindness, that a scene sets (SCN_* "blinded") and the referee then reports. That would make
   blindness a referee fact rather than a summary override. Since it touches the ruleset contract, the coordinator
   should decide.
3. **OPEN.md's G18 and X1** can be closed (dq0scene). G18's first half, the UI's 4-byte stubs, is a separate matter
   and is untouched here.
