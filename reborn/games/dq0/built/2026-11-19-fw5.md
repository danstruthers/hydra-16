## The played games' own letters

- **tests/lib/dq0.js's dq0PlayLetters(m, own, played)**: as each line of the test's own game is entered, each letter
  in it (a word of one letter, a count before it, "drop 1g"; not the new one of "letter h as t") is noted as the thing
  it names then, from the UI bank: a carried thing (ix) by its kind's name, where it is (held, worn, at the belt, in
  what) and its state (lit, spent), its place among those alike; a capital (cap) by its thing's or denizen's kind and
  its place among those alike.  As the same line comes to be typed in the game as played, at its prompt, each letter
  becomes that game's letter for the same thing (alike as near as it can be: kind, place and state; kind and place;
  kind), kept if there's none.  The game's own screen isn't read: its first line ("drop j") comes before any list of
  that game's, and the UI bank has what the lists show.
- **dq0Timer** counts a line so changed as the test's own (its measure's echo the typed one): its "b, played" is
  still "b, played" in the notes, typed "c".
- Applied to dq0letstow, dq0letwhich, dq0letseveral, dq0letcmd, dq0letsight and dq0letdens (dq0letters names its
  things; dq0doff and dq0torchwords type names).  sim/lib/dq0meaning.js's layout has the UI bank's ix, ixks, low and
  the ucap fields for it.
- The played games now type, against the test's own: dq0letstow "drop j" -> "drop u" (the torch), "f" -> "e" (the
  pouch), "hang l" -> "hang w", "drop 1g" -> "drop 1s", "hold i"/"stow i" -> "l" (the waterskin), "b" -> "c" (the
