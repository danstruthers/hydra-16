## How it was measured: eight phases, not one

A single suite run says one phase of each race. `SIM_PROMPT_LAG=N` (acia.js) types every line N cycles later after
its prompt, so the same test lands its lines at other phases of the background's work; scenes.js keys a scene by it (a
scene saved at one lag was being restored at another: the first sweeps mixed). Each test was run at eight lags (0,
7,000, 13,000, 23,000, 31,000, 41,000, 53,000, 67,000: `lineprof.js TEST --list`, in parallel) and every timed line's
first letter collected. That is my stress run that forces moves and fellings into windows: dq0felled's and the fight
tests' windows at those lags hold a rat's move, a blow, a felling in many of them.
