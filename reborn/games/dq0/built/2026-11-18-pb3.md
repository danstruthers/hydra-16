## What was built

### P4: "empty X", "empty X onto Y"

- **The grammar** (commands.ebnf): `<empty> ::= <EMPTY> <object> [ "out" ] [ <put-prep:other> <object:other> ]`,
  `<EMPTY> ::= "empty" | "empty out" | "tip out" | "pour out" | "dump" | "dump out"`; the action VB_EMPTY_OBJECT
  (commands.actions, object and container), its verb row (commands.verbs: aux IO_EMPTY, unsaid the last thing named)
  and its words; three corpus lines.
- **The UI**: a dispatch row (actions.c: H_HANDLE, the things' IO_EMPTY), so the one path for things carries it: out
  of reach, walked to first; its words the world's; its time waited.
- **The world** (dq0w/wempty.c, a new swapped part, 111): the thing named, carried or within reach; its inside's
  things (its first compartment's: a chest's inside, not its lid's top) put down at the character's feet, or with Y
  named, into Y's first compartment (onto it, a surface); a liquid in it gone, poured away; a flask's oil poured away
  (its fuel to 0).  A take's moment (two seconds) a thing put out, and one for the pouring.  Refused, said: not a thing
  that holds things ("The torch isn't a thing to empty."), closed or locked ("The sack is closed.": sealed, the user's
  word), nothing in it ("The sack is empty."); Y not one that holds things ("The torch won't hold them."), closed, no
  room in it for them all ("There's no room for them on the table.").  Said: "You empty the sack out at your feet.",
  "You empty the sack onto the table.", "... into the chest.", " The water runs away." after, or "You pour the oil out
  of the flask of oil." when only a liquid was in it.

### P5: a thing put on a ledge, in a corner, the centre, a side or a passage

- **The UI** (sway.c's place_said, overlay 14): a put or a drop whose other is a corner, the centre, a side (or "the
  room" with one of those words), a ledge or a passage finds that square: a corner, the centre, a side by place_sq

  ledge here.").  The command is then a put by the world's IO_PLACE: its square in cmd.other's x, y, the place's words'

- **The world** (wempty.c): the thing carried, got out (its reach owed, worn taken off first), put down on that

  it."); "You put the iron key in the corner.", "... in the middle.", "... on the far side.", "... on the ledge.", "...
  in the passage."  A shaft's head takes it as a drop does (sky_ask).

### One path for every actor

Empty and place are world acts any actor does by the same code: a denizen's I_ROB with errand FE_EMPTY (its own
thing t's things put out at its feet, a liquid poured away, a take's moment each, told as it's seen: E_BEAT WB_DROP of
the first) or FE_PLACE (its own thing t put down on square w1, w2 within its reach, a moment, WB_DROP), wbody.c's,
through wempty.c's same functions (iq.to's TO_DEN: body bd's square, no words in the things' text).  No mind posts them
yet: a denizen emptying a sack it's robbed, or putting a thing down somewhere, wants its mind's choosing (dq0d), and
the empty's FE_EMPTY borrows the things' request copy (iq) for its moment: safe while no character request is part way
through a reach's steps, which is so as long as the denizens' turn comes between requests.
