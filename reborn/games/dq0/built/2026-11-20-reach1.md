# A reach step's words told as it's done, never before (2026-11-20; as built, for review)

Branch reach1, from reborn-commands 22056f3f. One commit, 69510486.

## 1. The fault

hear2's dq0packsteps showed it: a "stop" typed while the torch was got out of the pack, and the game said "You stop
there. You put on the backpack. Your backpack is in your hand.", the pack in hand. The cause is general:

- The reach told each step's words as the step began (the world's own steps at RO_GO; the UI's at step_go, the
  IO_STEP reply), and made its change only at the step's end (part 86's RO_END, rs_did).
- A stop landing between the two (any time in the step's wait, or as the next step was begun) cut the sequence
  (rs_cut) with the change never made.

A sweep of stop moments (the new test, below) on the base found three such untruths in one reach alone:
- a stop in the pack's step: "You take off the backpack, and set it down at your feet." and the pack still worn;
- in the get-out's: "You get out the flask of oil." and the flask still in the pack;
- in the pack's return: "You put on the backpack." then "Your backpack lies at your feet.", the pack left off.

## 2. The fix

HOUSE.md's rule is kept: "A reach ends between its steps, never part way through one: a step not finished hasn't
happened" ("You stop taking off the backpack.", the pack still on). What changed is when a step's words come:
- **As it's done.** A step's words are told at RO_END, with its change made (rs_owed, set as the step begins, told
  there; a cut owes none). The world's own steps (RO_GO) and the UI's (step_go) no longer tell them as they begin.
- **Words that begin a work, or ask, keep their place at the start** (hub.h's SV_AT_START): "You set to dipping
  the torch.", sharpening, "You strike a spark.", "You go to put out the torch: that will leave you in the dark."
  (its question). They claim nothing done.
- **The stop's words.** The UI notes the step under way as it begins (step_k), so a stop part way names it ("You
  stop taking off the backpack.", nothing done, nothing told); once a step's words are told (done) it names none,
  and a stop after it says "You stop there.".
- **Room.** Part 86 (the reach's) had 66 bytes free and would have gone under 64. rs_spill and rs_queue (small, self
  contained) are moved to part 85 (s_rs_spill, s_rs_queue, stubs): part 86 now about 184 free. The world's main RAM
  2,184 to 2,170 (the stubs, rs_owed). The UI's main RAM 3,143 unchanged (step_go and reach_said are parts).

The stop races stay races in timing only: a stop lands in a step (undone, untold, named) or between two (done and
told before it, "You stop there."). Every word told is true either way.
