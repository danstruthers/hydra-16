## What was built

**Flights, every actor's (wflight.c, part 114).**
- One rule for a flight's crossing (a stairway, a ladder, a long slope): `flight_t` in the world's main RAM (acts.h's
  FLIGHT_T), the drop between the floors at the actor's own step (a step's time a foot on a stairway, twice on a ladder,
  halved down; a long slope 13 or 8 tenths a foot). The character's s_climb reads it; so does an actor's crossing.
- An actor's I_CLIMB (its mind's) now crosses the flight joining its stratum to the one it means, the nearest within 3
  squares: its walk to the foot a step a square, then the flight, on its ready (after what was left of its last act);
  it comes out at the flight's other end, or beside it if someone's there; told there as a step (the act table's new
  flight row: E_DEN DE_MOVED, loudness 2, seen or heard). Down, prone, its legs first (den_legs, as its step's).
- Before, an ally coming along was put beside the character in a second, whatever the way. Now that's only when no
  flight joins the strata within its reach (a shaft, a rope, a fall: not yet any actor's), an ally's alone.
- The minds' choice: a foe that was coming at the character or fighting it, near it (anear) as it went up or down, goes
  after it by the same intent as an ally coming along (dq0d's tend). An actor crossing isn't roused from it (the
  minds' aflight: the character's change otherwise cut its long crossing short to a second).
- ally fallback and the crossing moved out of wport.c (part 80's room).

**Lights, every actor's (wlink.c, part 115; wlimbs.c's den_lsum, part 60).**
- The hub's `alight[NACTORS]` (the world's): each actor's carried light's reach (the brightest lit one held, worn or
  hung from its belt), AL_KIT a light of its own it could light, AL_NOFIRE none lit for want of a flame. Made as its
  things are told the referee (s_den_equip), cleared as it enters.
- Seen by others (the senses): a lamp at each lit actor's square in the light layer (things_lamps: the character sees
  by it, and every looker's light_at); s_lit_sq (unlit_at's) counts any actor's carried light within its reach with a
  line from its eyes, as the character's; sense_actor takes a target actor's own light from alight (its glow seen far
  off) and keeps the looker's in its sens record. A lit actor that moves: E_LIGHT at its square, the character's look
  made again if within 16 squares (the main loop's dseq_job, as bodies change).
- Lit by the character's rules (I_LIGHT:den, a new form; its mind's): a light of its own not lit nor spent, one in
  hand first, else got out of its pack into its hand (10 tenths); a flame: a lit light of its own, or one lying lit
  within reach (a sconce's), at once; else its own fire-maker (kind tool "tinder", not found empty): got out (10), a try
  of the act table's new kindle row (5 seconds; the referee's P_GROPE by its sheet, 4 harder in the dark: the
  character's rs_roll), one try an ask, a lighting off it on success; empty, IS_SPENT. Told if seen (E_BEAT WB_LIGHTS:
  "The bandit lights its torch."). Out of hiding as it lights.
- The minds' choice: a kind that wants light (kinds.txt's l: folk), awake, with AL_KIT and no light lit, asks once in
  25 seconds at most (md.alit); the world lights it only where its square is dark (unlit_at).
- Put out: I_LIGHT 0, each it carries, the new douse row's time each; and lain down to sleep (I_RISE POS_PRONE) its
  burning lights are put out first, told "The bandit puts out its torch." (the user's: a rest puts a burning light out
  first, every actor).
- Burning down: burns_now goes through IW_DEN too; an actor's light burns by the same minute, and going out it's told
  as one seen ("The torch gutters and goes out."), its alight made again, the light layer near it out of date.
- A denizen with a light lit can't hide (the character's HW_LIGHT rule, part 94).
- The pool hook (step1's H15): wpool.c's flame (who) already finds a naked flame an actor carries (IW_DEN): a denizen
  carrying a lit torch that falls in lamp oil now sets it alight. Nothing of the pool's burning changed here.

## Not built (open questions)

1. **Shafts, ropes, ledges, a pit's sides, wells, a window climbed through, jumping down**: a denizen's climb by rounds
   (P_CLIMB by who, the character's climb_round_on) needs an actor's fall (s_fall is the character's alone; the
   referee's harn_fall is the character's). Step1's. Until then an ally still comes along by a shaft or a rope put
   beside the character in a second (the asymmetry left), and a foe doesn't follow that way.
2. **Rope acts by who** (tie, untie, cut, drive a spike, throw a grapple: G-12).
3. **A denizen leaving the character's stratum of its own accord** (its day's patrols between strata, fleeing up a
   stair): the minds keep only actors on the character's stratum; only following is built.
4. **The walk to a flight's foot** is a step a square straight (three at most), not the way-finder's path as the
   character's `way` is.
5. **Fatigue** of a long slope climbed: the character's GA_WORK; an actor's waits for the one body clock (A6).
6. **Folk's outfits**: bandits, soldiers, cultists want light (kinds.txt's l) but carry none, nor any fire-maker; dwarf
   miners carry a lantern but no tinderbox. Should their outfits give them one? (A change to every bandit test's kit.)
7. **Refilling a lantern** from a flask of oil, for a denizen (the character's s_fill): not built.
8. **A rest putting out lights within reach** (L6) for denizens: only those it carries; a keepers' sconce (D1:
   keepers relighting their torches as an errand) not built.
9. **Hands**: a light got out goes into a hand without the hands rule (C8, things1's).
10. **The referee's quiet dice**: a creature's RS_TEST without RS_ASKER (act_test's) uses dice mixed from the game's and
    put back, so the same test at the same moment comes out alike; twelve tries in one call all failed alike. The
    kindle tries now ask with RS_ASKER, one an ask. Other actor tests in a loop (search's) may share this.
11. **Telling**: an actor's light going out is told with the lying light's words ("The torch gutters and goes out."),
    not "The bandit's torch ..."; its lighting in the dark isn't told (not seen till its light shows). A flight's
    crossing is told as a step at its arrival (no "comes down the stairs" row).
12. **Part numbers 114, 115** may clash with other branches' new parts (renumber at merge).
