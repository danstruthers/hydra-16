# A senseless one's shock rolls a round each, as time passes (2026-11-21; as built, for review)

Branch felled1 from reborn-commands (9296e680). The user's option 1 (DECISIONS.md's last entry, 2026-11-21): a foe
knocked senseless rolls its shock test as its time comes, not an hour of rolls at once as it falls. The same rule and
the same odds; the dice fall in another order, so outcomes may change. Every actor by the same code, the character too.
Then dq0felled's felling check back to every game, held to D17 at every pace.

## 1. A round, not a minute

The rolls come a round each (10 seconds, 100 tenths: RS_HELLO_ROUND).

- **HarnMaster's rule:** the book has a senseless character make a shock roll each round to come round (harn_wake's,
  "a fainted character's turn"). The game already rolled exactly that, a round each, up to an hour (360 rounds); it
  only rolled them all at once as the blow fell.
- **The game's clock:** the round is the clock's own unit for fighting (a blow, a struggle up, a round of strenuous
  work). A minute would have changed the odds (six times fewer rolls in the hour) and so the rule.

So the change is only when each roll is made, which is what the user chose.

## 2. As built

### The referee (dq0harn)

- **o_wake_out is gone.** knocked_out (overlay 10) now only marks the fall: `ko_at[i]` (the clock) and `ko_n[i]` (the
  rolls made since), by index (0 the character, an actor's entry plus 1). Its first roll is a round on; the time out
  it returns is that round (100 tenths), or more if it was out already (AF_AGAIN, as before).
- **o_rouse** (overlay 10) rolls the rounds due by now, harn_wake's rule each round: a roll not over Endurance wakes
  it, one more says whether it's in shock (H5). The 360th round comes round anyway (the character's last shock roll
  the book's; a creature as it is), as before. Rolls sure to fail (UP over Endurance, no test dice) are passed over by
  xs_jump, as before. A choke's minute is kept: no roll before the time out.
- **When the rolls are made:** before any request while the character's senseless (rf_answer, RS_DICE apart), in the
  summary (status), as a creature is unpacked for any request, and for RS_ROUSE.
- **RS_ROUSE** (new, rs.h's enum end, msgs.def): actor 0 or an actor's entry plus 1. Reply w2: the tenths it's
  senseless still (its next roll's), 0 come round, 0xFFFF dead as it lay (it never comes round). For the character,
  the tenths till its time out (pc_out) whatever its state, as the old black-out's end was: a state set otherwise
  (dq0helpless's poke, out a minute and in shock after) keeps it out till then. A referee with none (dq0null) leaves
  the world's 0: it comes round at its time out, as before.
- **The summary:** C_UNCONSCIOUS kept for a character that died as it lay (its fall's time still kept), so it's
  senseless still to the world: no round of its own after (dq0disengage counted one, a fight round charged after its
  death). The status word as it was, "DEAD".
- **A loaded file** forgets the rolls (o_unrouse, overlay 10, load_pc's): its fall unknown, it rolls from its next.

### The world (dq0w)

- **One ask for every actor:** out_still (part 52, beside bearings_off). A denizen's as its time out comes (den_intents:
  DO_DOWN, its ready reached); the character's from main's senseless branch (the one that turned its bearings off),
  as its time comes (pc_out, pc_ready), before the denizens' turns so none finds it free between its rolls. Its busy
  time is then its next roll's; 0, it comes round as before.
- **A sleeping character is struck as a sleeper** (RS_ASLEEP, the book's Ignore), as a creature asleep already was. One
  path: dq0killrest asked for exactly this, but its check had passed by accident (it read the 0x80 bit of a reply's
  time out, the old rounds-out). With the shorter outs the accident stopped and the gap showed.

## 5. Open questions

- **A denizen unattended catches up.** A creature's rolls are made as it's next asked about (its intent, a blow, an
  ask). One left alone a long while rolls its due rounds all at once then; the outcome is the same rule, but the moment
  it comes round is only seen as the world next looks.
- **The character's state while out is now "senseless"** (HARN_UNCONSCIOUS) all through, not its coming-round state set
  at once. Anything that read the sheet's state during a black-out (the status word, the conditions) now says senseless.
- **A harm's knockout** (a fall, a trap) now goes through knocked_out too, so it sets pc_out's time and its rolls the
  same way as a blow's.
- **Fatigue or a new wound during the out** changes UP between rolls (each round's roll uses the UP of the moment); the
  old code fixed it as the blow fell.
- **The rolls aren't kept with the game:** a game saved while one lies senseless begins its rolls again from its next
  when loaded.
- **A sleeping character's defence** (RS_ASLEEP) is a behaviour change the old check claimed was built; it was not.
