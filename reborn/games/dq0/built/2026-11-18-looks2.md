## What was built, and why

### The far part makes no chunk (M5)

- **None made for a look one way** (dq0p.c's v_nom, v_may; view.c's chunks).  A look one way's far part (LK_WAY), or
  the whole of it when its last look doesn't stand, makes no chunk the cache hasn't: not the glance's own reach, not
  the quarter ahead's.  The first such chunk the eyes reach is kept (dcx, dcy) for the look's rest.  The view is made
  from the ground there is; the squares in a chunk not made are as the grid always had them (the cache's chunks alone).
- **Its walks stop at the edge** (dq0p.c's way_stop, for the far part too; view.c's runs, kept).  An opening's way on
  (way.c's seen_on) stops at a chunk not made, as a near part's did, and is held (WB_HOLD); a passage's runs (sight.c's
  ways) stop there; the memory kept passes over chunks not made.  Each of these had made the chunk through at () before
  (the runs did, found by a stack trace: a run along a lit passage to the chunk's edge asked for the next).
- **The record says the rest is to come** (level.h's WM_REST, way_more).  The look's record says its eyes reached
  ground not made: its held openings are told now "so far", and "You see nothing more that way." is not said.
- **The words, "so far"** (said.txt's AHEAD.sofar, o_focus_words): a held opening's way is told as far as the made
  ground shows it, with "so far": "Down the passage ahead you see 20 feet of it so far."; "Through the doorway ahead you
  see only darkness so far."  Nothing told is taken back: the rest tells only what's more (the passage's further feet,
  the room it comes to, a thing, a denizen, a light), and an opening as told before, "so far" or not, isn't told again
  (near_said, the hold bit aside).
- **The rest, as the maker's made it** (hub.h's way_want; dq0m.c's way_next; dq0w.c's look_due, s_look_rest; dq0p.c's
  look_rest).  The far part puts the chunk it wanted in the hub (way_want, WW_WANTED); the maker, as it next makes a
  chunk ahead (in the background, giving way to a command and to keys as it always does), makes that one first
  (WW_MADE at once if it's made ahead already, or in the cache; let go if there's no room, the next look one way to tell
  it); the world, nothing in hand and the character free (its E_READY told, no long task), asks the senses the look's
  rest (LK_REST): the far part's look again from the same square, the same way, its own chunk made now (the finish of
  one made ahead, its things stocked), told the UI (E_VIEW V_LOOKED) if it differs, only what's new.  Any other look
  (but the denizens' and the eyes') lets the rest go.  A line begun as the rest is asked, or as its chunk would be
  finished, puts it off till nothing's in hand again (no answer kept waiting on a finish and a stock).  A rest's own
  ground not all made asks the next chunk's rest the same way.
- **A request the less** (hub.h's LC_WHOLE; dq0w.c's s_look_way).  A look one way's near part asked when its last look
  doesn't stand did nothing, and the world then asked the far part: now the senses do the whole look one way in that
  request and say so, and the world asks no far part (the near part's request now goes by see () too: the things round
  a moved character in the item store first, as any look's).
- **The far part's words sooner.**  A look one way's memory (view.c's kept, its chunks' freshness and drawing) is kept
  after its reply, before the next look (dq0p.c's kept_owed); its passage's runs still before what it perceives.  The
  lamps (things.c's things_lamps, light.c's) moved from part 3 to part 7, so part 3 (way.c) is copied in once a look,
  not twice.

### A stir held through the far part (L7)

- **The hub's ui_cmd set as a near part's told** (way.c's cmd_hold, overlay 13's own code through hub_mem): the UI's
  command counts as in hand again from the near part's words to the far part's (or its "all the same", WM_SAME), so a
  denizen's stir arriving between them is held (main.c's events, stir_held) and told after the far part's words, the
  minds' asks held with it (the senses' sense_held), the maker's making ahead and the world's long jobs giving way.
  Let go by the far part only if the near part set it: a rest told after the prompt never clears a command in hand.
