# The referee's rounds out made cheap (2026-11-20; as built, for review)

Branch ref1 from 34d64e25 (throw3 and reach1 merged). reach1's find, failing the head: dq0fightmid's "go left" at
6,509,604 cycles (904%). The character's blow knocked the rat senseless as the line waited, and the referee spent
4.52M on its own work (`_random` 1.57M and `next` 0.89M in harn_shock_roll, harn_get_up and harn_wake).

## 1. The cause

sheet.c's wake: a creature knocked senseless lies "till it comes round, by the book's shock rolls, a round each,
within an hour". So up to 359 rounds, each harn_wake: a shock roll, dice(UP, 6) over Endurance, a die a point of
universal penalty. Each die went through dice, dice_plus and die, then random: xorshift32's step, and a 16-step
shift-and-add multiply. A felled rat whose rolls nearly all fail cost some 5,400 dice: 4.5M cycles before the blow's
reply, the line's first letter behind it.

## 2. The fix: the same dice, cheaper

Three changes, each giving the same numbers as before.

- **The SDK's generator** (sdk/c/lib/xorshift.s):
  - random (n) for n under 256 (a die's sides, the most asked) multiplies by n's 8 bits, not x's 16: the same high
    16 bits of rand16 () * n, in half the steps.
  - next's x << 13 is now 3 shifts of (x << 16), not 5 of (x << 8).
- **dice_plus** (sdk/c/lib/dice.c): each die rolled in the loop, not through die ()'s call; dice_hook still rolls
  every die when it's set.
- **wake** (games/dq0harn/sheet.c):
  - Its penalty and Endurance are found once, not per roll (they can't change while it lies senseless).
  - Each roll stops multiplying once the sum is over Endurance: the rest of its dice only move the generator on
    (rand16), so the sum can't come back under and the stream moves exactly as before.
  - **A foe whose every roll must fail** (its least, UP, over Endurance) has the hour's 359 rolls' numbers passed over
    at once: xsjump.c's xs_jump moves the generator on 359 × UP numbers exactly. xorshift32 is linear over GF(2), so n
    steps are T^n, the product of the T^(2^i) of n's bits: 17 matrices, 2,176 bytes, in overlay 10. The table was
    made by a script and checked against stepping one at a time, for n = 1, 2,872, 2,873, 5,385 and 91,545 from
    several states.
  - With dice_hook set (dq0 -F's fixed dice) the rolls are made as before.
  - wake (now o_wake_out, with its stub wake_out for o_harm) and o_knocked_out moved to overlay 10, beside fight.c's
    strike (overlay 1 was full).

**The dice are unchanged.** Every roll's numbers and the stream after them are what they were: the same draws, a jump
exactly equal to the draws it replaces. The suite agrees: no test whose words come from the dice differs, nor
dq0armour, dq0ghoul or the turns-pace fights. The null referee builds and its tests run the same.

## 3. The test: dq0felled (fight.js)

dq0fightmid's way and its lines typed as the character's own blow is being worked. Its rat is hurt as it's engaged:
a minor wound of level 8, so every one of its shock rolls fails and the referee works the most rounds out, an hour's.
Looks, a wait and a step are typed mid-round, a blow fells the rat as one of them waits (checked in each game), and
every first letter is held to D17, at the normal pace, the menacing and as played.

## 5. Open questions

1. **A foe that can still wake, but hardly ever** (UP within three of its Endurance): its rounds are rolled one by
   one, up to 359 × UP numbers, each step of the generator some 170 cycles. A rat with UP 6 against Endurance 6 or 7
   costs some 370,000 cycles more on its blow. dq0felled made with a level-6 wound ran its played "go left" at 728,620
   (101%). That profile was the blow's ordinary cost, its rounds few (next 31,000 cycles): the referee's blow
   250,000, the UI's words 107,000, the console's and the parser's the rest. So a line waiting on a near-certain
   foe's felling can still miss D17.
   - Options for the user: an exact jump per round for a big UP (T^UP, built once, applied each round: worth it for UP
     of 7 or more); or, if the book allows it, a shock roll less often than every round. That would change the dice.
2. dq0stopsweep's stops at fixed cycle offsets: list it in noise.txt beside dq0reachstop?
