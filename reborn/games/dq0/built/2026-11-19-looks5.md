comes after the prompt, its first sentence led in:

    > look ahead
    You peer ahead. Down the passage ahead you see 20 feet of it so far.

The rest's first sentence takes the lead-in, whatever it is:

| The rest's first sentence | Its row (said.txt) | As told |

## How

- **The senses mark the rest's record.** level.h's WM_RESTED, set in dq0p.c's look_rest on WAY_SEEN after the rest's
  look.
- **The UI tells the lead-in from that mark** (overlay 13, way.c's o_focus_words). The rest's first opening's sentence
  uses AHEAD.onthrough in place of AHEAD.through, and its first light far off AHEAD.onglow. With neither, AHEAD.onmore
  comes before its things and denizens.
- **Why a mark rather than the UI's own count.** The UI had kept its own "a rest to come" (nrest). In dq0lookfar's look
  at once, the test's poke moves the character as the line is typed, and the look's own words came from the place before
  it. The UI's count then missed the rest that followed, so the mark is the record's own.
