## 1. What was built

1. **Each faction knows its own traps.**
   - **The knowers.** world.h's struct trap has a new byte, `know`: a bit for each faction (TRAP_KB(f) = 1 << (f & 7);
     bit 0 is the denizens of no faction). It is kept with the trap's state in the TRAP_STATE record's elev. Knowers
     are only ever added: store.c's s_change ORs in the bits the chunk already has. The maker replays them
     (dq0m/changes.c) and makes every trap with none (fixtures.c).
   - **Its own, from the start.** When a lair of folk is placed, the minds' s_place (part 3) posts I_KIT with a new
     draw, KT_TRAPS (hub.h), the lair's faction and its home square. wlimbs.c's den_other hands it to wstep.c's
     own_traps (part 122), which marks every floor trap in the lair's chunk as that faction's (a TRAP_STATE record
     each). The maker can't do this itself: lairs are the minds' records, decided after the chunk is grown. "Where the
     dungeon says so" is therefore a lair of folk (kinds.txt's nature f) in the chunk. A beast's lair, or the dead's,
     owns no trap, and a trap in a chunk with no such lair is no one's.
   - **Another's, once found.** A denizen's notice in time (wstep.c's DT_NOTICE) or its search (now wtsrch.c, see
     below) marks its faction's bit. It no longer clears TS_HIDDEN, so the character doesn't learn of it. The
     character's own find clears TS_HIDDEN as before, and the denizens don't learn of that. A trap in plain sight
     (hide 0) is known to all. A door's trap is unchanged: found for everyone, as before (open question 1).
   - **The walks.** dq0d's wet_ok goes round a set floor trap that the walker's faction knows (ktrap.s, a new assembly
     helper, TRAP_KNOWN's rule). The walker's knowers' bit is the minds' new md.akb[] (set as it's enlisted) in the
     high byte of wet_ok's argument (WALKER). The ways' fields are keyed by it too: rfield.who is now 16 bits, so one
     faction's field is never used by another faction's walker.
2. **A winged body passes over floor traps, pits and spilt oil.** In hz.s's den_hz, a body of BD_WINGS skips the pit
   climb, the trap ahead, the trap underfoot and the oil pool. The minds' WA_WINGS (enlist's) lets its walks go over
   the floor traps it knows of. A door's trap still takes it (workpart.h's front is unchanged). A wire counts as a
   floor trap, set off underfoot as the code had it.
3. **A wound from a trap or a fall tests courage as a blow's does.** RS_HARM at an actor now carries its heart (x[0])
   and its shyness (x[1]), as RS_ATTACK does (msgs.def). Both referees answer with the blow's rule:
   - HarnMaster: fight.c's RS_HARM case calls left(), the same courage() as a blow's wound.
   - The null referee: its blow's flat test against the heart.
   The world's den_harm treats the answer as wfight.c treats a blow's: FF_FLEES makes it DO_FLEE, and AF_CAUTIOUS
   holds it back a round. The E_FIGHT FO_HURT carries FF_FLEES, so @after's "They flee." is told when it's seen. The
   minds' fought() marks it fled, but for FO_HURT doesn't turn its attitude to the character to unfriendly (a trap
   isn't the character's doing).
4. **"You hear a cry of pain" only where it's heard.** The wound's unseen words are now empty (said.txt's FIGHT.hurt).
   The cry is an E_DEN of its own, DE_CRY or DE_CRYCUT (dead of it), sent by wport.c's den_told, the one sender of an
   actor's noises. The UI weighs it as any denizen's noise (o_den_heard: the distance, what's between, the
   background, the character's hearing, and the same way not again within 30 s). It is told in the noise's words, its
   heading added: "You hear a cry of pain ahead to your right.", "... a muffled cry of pain cut short ...". The minds
   don't hear it, as they don't hear a blow (open question 4). Its loudness is now a fight's din, 5 (it was 3, unused
   before; open question 3).
5. **The climb out of a pit ends with a step.** The character's last round of a pit climb (dq0w.c's climb_round_on,
   cl.up 2 or 3) now spends the step onto the edge: step_cost at its pace, from the pit's floor to the edge, diagonal
   or onto steps as any step. A denizen already paid it, its I_STEP's own time after DT_PIT.

**Room work.** wtrap.c's part 8 had 78 bytes free and the change didn't fit. Its search (the character's and an
actor's) moved to wtsrch.c, a new part 123 with its own copy of workpart.h's helpers. dq0w.c's work() and wsearch.c call
tsearch directly, so no extra part copy is made. Part 8 went from 78 to 1,391 free. dq0d's wet_ok trap loop is now
ktrap.s (cc65's loop was some 200 bytes), so the faction check costs dq0d 11 bytes in all.

## 6. Open questions

1. **A faction's own door traps.** Door traps are unchanged: found by anyone means found for everyone, and a lair's
   folk don't know their own. A known door trap is left shut for every actor, so knowing their own would shut them
   out of their lair. Should a faction know its own door traps and pass them safely (a catch only they know)?
2. **What "the dungeon says" a trap's faction is.** As built, the traps underfoot in the chunk of a lair of folk are
   that faction's, marked as the lair is placed. Should it be only the lair's home room? Should the dead (a tomb's
   guardians) or a theme's piece guardians (theme.h's pguard, goal_guard) own traps too? The factions are taken & 7,
   as dq0d's REL is, so with more than 7 factions two share a bit (the stand-in's gargun share the crawlers' bit).
3. **A cry's loudness.** It is 5 as built, a fight's din. It was 3, a word's, which the background almost never let
   through (dq0trapheart's 2-square cry wasn't heard even at 5). Is 5 right, or as loud as a shout (6)? A cry also
   follows the hearing rule's "not again from the same way within 30 s".
4. **The minds and a cry.** The denizens don't hear a cry (as they don't hear a blow). Should they turn to it, as to
   any noise?
5. **A denizen stepping onto a trap it knows.** Its walks go round one, but a step onto it still sets it off with no
   notice test, as it did for a known trap before (a flight or a step not by its ways). Should a known trap stop the
   step instead?
6. **A factionless creature's find.** A beast's find marks bit 0, shared by every creature of no faction. Should it be
   known to that creature alone, or to no one?
7. **The character no longer learns of a denizen's trap find** (it did under op2b's "found for every one"; ONE-PATH.md's
   S1). This follows "known only once found" by knower. Is that right for the character too?
8. **Open pits for a winged body.** It passes over a covered pit, a trap, as built. An open pit (T_PIT) is still no
   square for anyone's step (T_PASS, step8, the ways' rules). Should a flier cross one? That reaches the shared walk
   library.
9. **The base worktree.** N:/.claude/worktrees/step2base (detached at b44bd6fb) was made to run dq0reachflee at base.
   It can be removed.
