## What was built

- **A new kind, appended** (items.h's KI_LAMP_OIL, after KI_SHELVES; kinds.c's row "oil", and its throws' entry): a
  liquid counted in pints (KF_LIQUID | KF_COUNT), a pound a pint, a penny a pint.  Its record's fuel is the minutes of
  burning it holds, its qty the pints (OIL_PINT, 360: a lantern's fill, six hours), so a part-used pint is kept exact.
  Nothing else renumbered: every kind before it where it was.
- **Poured** (dq0w/wpour.c and wpour2.c, every actor's, H11's code):
  - a flask's oil into a vessel that holds liquids: a pint of lamp oil there (joined to oil already there, or a record
    of its own), the flask empty after ("You pour the oil into the vial.");
  - a vessel's lamp oil poured on, as water is: whole pints, the last one's part going with it all; joined, moved, or
    split off, its minutes with it; poured away ("You pour the oil out of the vial.");
  - into a flask of oil, by its measure, as far as it takes ("You pour the oil into the flask of oil.", or "You pour
    some of the oil into the flask of oil: it's full, and the rest stays in the vial."); a flask holds only lamp oil
    ("The flask of oil won't hold water.");
  - "pour the oil into the flask of oil": the oil, not the flask named twice (the world's chooser and the UI's first
    words alike: sway.c's ix_pick, the very name, when the thing it'd take is the other too);
  - two liquids don't mix (H13): "The vial has oil in it already."; filling a waterskin that holds lamp oil: "You'd
    have to empty the waterskin first." (wwater.c);
  - an empty flask emptied: "The flask of oil is empty." (was "The flask of oil isn't a thing to empty.").
- **A lantern filled from any vessel holding lamp oil** (things.c's s_fill): "fill the lantern from the vial" (the oil
  in it; its vessel reached for and named: "You fill the lantern from the vial.", "..., and it's empty now."), or, no
  fuel named, the first lamp oil carried; its pints as it's used, gone when none's left.  A torch dipped the same.
- **Carried**: a vessel's lamp oil is listed as water is ("3 pints of oil"), its weight and bulk its pints'.
- **One path**: the pour is wpour.c's for every actor (a denizen's I_ROB FE_EMPTY, its other in w1); the lantern's
  fill is the character's (s_fill, as before: a denizen fills no light yet).
- **First words** at once, held to D17 in dq0oil: each pour, empty and fill.
- **Not built** (the coordinator's: a new behaviour the user hasn't asked for): oil spilt on the floor burning or
  slippery.  It's gone, as water poured away is.  OPEN.md's H15.
