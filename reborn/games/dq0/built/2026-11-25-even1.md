## 1. The turns pace's last way in: the world didn't wait for the UI's answer

Two layouts (as built, and the 37-byte filler) parted in dq0digact (game 2) and dq0wakeblow (game 1). The tool: DQ0DUMP=DIR
(sim/test.js) dumps each test's m.dq0 games, every record stamped with its cycle; the two layouts' dumps compared record by
record. Both tests parted at the same kind of moment: a long task's question (E_TASK TE_ASK_HEARD, a sound heard as it
waited or slept) and its answer (dq0 -a's "go on", I_GO_ON, the UI's at once). Around them the world's own work came
before the answer in one layout and after it in the other:
- dq0wakeblow: the time believed reckoned (RS_EXERT's rest, then RS_TEST P_BEARING) before the "go on" in one layout,
  after it in the other; the bearing's roll then fell another way, the clock's skew -246 against +246, and the death's
  wounds had "bloodloss 2" or not.
- dq0digact: the rest's RS_EXERT before the "go on" (200 tenths) in one layout, merged with the next (300) in the other;
  then the miners met "watching" or "fighting you".

Three causes, all the world's wait for the UI to see to its events (turns_run's, lock1's 2026-11-04 rule):
1. **Its timer let it by on an even tick.** `ui_t = hy_ticks () | 1`, then `(unsigned) (hy_ticks () - ui_t) <
   TURN_WAIT`: on an even tick the difference is 0xFFFF, so the first look at it said "waited thirty seconds already"
   and the world went on. Whether it waited at all was the tick's parity: the tasks' timing. HUBTRACE (with
   HUBTRACE_SW at the timer's address) showed a turn given 13,000 cycles after a question was told, the UI not round
   to it, ui_t freshly set.
2. **The wait ran a whole pass each tick.** turns_run's "wait" returned 1, the main loop went round again: its early
   jobs, the intents taken, the clock's step, the late jobs (the lights' minute, the time believed) all ran while the UI
   was behind, as many times as the UI took. A job due then ran before the UI's answer or after it.
3. **The intents loop took the question and its answer in one pass or in two.** The UI's I_ASK told TE_ASK_HEARD
   inside the loop's intent(); a quick UI's I_GO_ON was in the ring before the loop looked again and was taken in the
   same pass, a slow one's in the next (wait_pace saw the asking or not: the lights' minute at once, burn_ask, or not).

The fix (9f19b08e, b9e9565a):
- ui_state (jobs.s, in assembly, the main RAM): at the turns pace (tpr 0, not paused or held, no command being given,
  not dead), 1 while the UI hasn't seen to the events told (ui_eq, ui_dq); 2 no turn; 0 quitting or none.
- The world waits on it at the top of its pass (ui_behind: a tick at a time, the UI's things and its save or close look
  served, nothing else), takes no intent while it's 1 (one that told the UI something ends the loop), and goes on with
  none of the pass after the intents while it's 1. turns_run waits on it too, whatever's in the ring.
- The timer's look "+ 1": `(unsigned) (hy_ticks () + 1 - ui_t) < TURN_WAIT`.
- A UI stuck past TURN_WAIT is let by, the pass and the intents as before (ui_stuck), till it's seen to them.
- The UI's quit (stop_world) says it has seen to the events before it sends I_QUIT: it then waits on the tasks' ends,
  and the world would have waited on it (dq0pcfelled's quit as it lay senseless hung, the first version).
- ui_behind and turns_run moved into part lockstep (with jobs.s's quiet): dq0w's main RAM 2,144 -> 2,300.

The check that catches it again: **dq0lockwake** (tests/suites/dq0/time.js), dq0lockstep's way on dq0wakeblow's sleeper
among the cultists (put to sleep in the dark, asked about a sound, struck awake, fighting on): four games, each line's
first key 0, 9,000, 37,000 and 211,000 cycles after its prompt; the four stories the same sentence for sentence, and
the world's events, the denizens' doings and the referee's answers in the same order. At 444699f7 it fails in both
layouts (game 2 to 4 part at the referee's 23rd or 48th answer: the RS_EXERT and RS_TEST orders above, and an RS_TIME
taken a turn earlier); with the fix it passes in both.


A third commit (54defbb8) took back what the first had cost: turns_run waited on the UI's pass before taking an intent
already in the ring, even with the UI round to every event, so the look at once's answer waited on the UI's own pass
(dq0lookrest's longest pause 1,589,097 -> 1,739,510, 97% of D17's 1.79M, in both layouts). Now an intent with the UI
round to the events is taken first, as before even1: 1,617,147. A UI behind still has the world wait for it.
