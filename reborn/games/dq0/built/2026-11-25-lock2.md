## 2. The three failures, and their causes

- **dq0saveout** (both layouts): **a hang, not a moved poke.**
  - At the turns pace lock2 left the start's E_READY to the world's main loop, which sends it at its free point.
  - A game carried on with the character senseless (pc_out) doesn't reach that point until it comes round. The UI,
    still starting, waits for that E_READY, so the carried-on game stopped after its first look with no prompt.
  - The fix (dq0w.c's s_start): the deferred E_READY applies only to a character that is free (not out, pc_ready not
    ahead of now). Otherwise E_READY is sent at once, as before lock2.
  - The test passed with that fix alone, raw poke and all. Its raw write to the referee's fb bank (KOS) is now gone
    as well. Its one job was to make the first reading match. The expected KOS entry is now the referee's ko_at[1]
    and ko_n[1] as the scene set them (RS_SCENE's SCN_RAM). The save's KOSAVE, read back into KOS, is what's proved.
- **dq0fightmid** (as built only): **not the lockstep.**
  - Its games run at the normal pace, the menacing pace and as played. None is at the turns pace.
  - In the normal-pace game the character's first blow felled the rat, so the next three lines met no round.
  - A real-pace blow falls at the tasks' timing, and its dice are keyed by its moment (dice2). So the layout decided
    whether the rat fell.
  - Re-aim: the menacing game's rig (the character's blows marginal failures) now covers all three games. The rat
    fights on in every layout and every line meets a round. The check itself is unchanged.
- **dq0tasks** (as built only): **a stir told after the answer's echo, then the look listed the rat again.** It was
  not a stir held for E_READY.
  - "Keep waiting?" was answered with "look". The stir came after the question but before the world's quiet. The
    character was busy, so it was told as it came.
  - The test's keys landed before the stir in one layout and after it in the other. Told after the echo, the stir
    sat inside the look's answer. Taking the line then reset the answer's told sentences, so the look told the rat
    again.
  - The fix (main.c): at the turns pace a stir is held while the character asks, as it already is while the
    character's free. It is told when the answer is taken, as part of that answer: after the echo and before "You
    stop waiting.". The look then doesn't repeat it (lbsaid's told list). A yes or a no tells it in the same place,
    whatever the keys' timing.

## 8. Assumptions

- At the turns pace a stir that comes while the character asks belongs to the answer: it is told when the answer is
  taken, and a look in that answer doesn't repeat it. Section 7 ("Nothing told twice") and lock2's rule for a stir
  while free (told at a fixed point, not at the keys' timing). The world is stopped while it asks at the turns pace,
  so nothing is told late.
- A game carried on with the character senseless or busy tells E_READY at once at the turns pace, as before lock2.
  Only a free character's E_READY waits for the world's first turn. B3 (the senseless sense nothing and do nothing):
  there's no free point to wait for.
- The quiet a line waits for, the E_READY deferral and the stir holds are engineering (section 8). So are dq0fightmid's
  rig and dq0saveout's scene-only setup, both test re-aims with the behaviour checked unchanged.
