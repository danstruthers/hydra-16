# The console's stall, every answer's longest pause, and three races (2026-11-18; as built, for review)

The coordinator's job after fw3: an answer once begun must not stall mid-word (fw3's trace: "You lift the dag", 3.8M,
"ger."), the console kept in the kernel's foreground while the UI's output is queued, the right owner said; a survey of
every answer's longest pause between letters across the timed games, before and after; then dq0tend's sleep's wants
after its prompt again on the merged code, dq0letstow's "b, played" at 986,326, and a denizen's news in a long task
leaving a line no first letter.  Branch fw4, from 93c3c60f, rebased onto 6dceefbb (room3).

## The stall: what it was

- The console (reborn/modules/cons) scrolls jump: a write to the shown window is taken whole into its screen, the
  serial port's send ring filled as it has room (256 bytes, some 80,000 cycles at 115,200), and the rest painted from
  the screen by the console's own pump as the ring empties.  That painting is the console's work, not the UI's call.
- The kernel kept a driver in the foreground FORE_SRV (2) ticks after a foreground task's call to it (K0_FSRV); and
  every KCALL is an SCALL to task 0, which took K0_FSRV too: the UI's next KCALL (its ticks, its nap) ended the
  console's time at once.  So once the ring held the answer's first part, the rest waited in the console's screen for
  the background's turn, behind the world's foreground work: "You lift the dag" ... "ger.".
- **The owner is the kernel's.**  Only the kernel can put a driver in the foreground (TASKFORE refuses a driver,
  E_PERM: main.c's o_fore_set can't), and "the work a call left the driver" is already the kernel's rule (FORE_SRV); it
  was too short (2 ticks against a ring's 4.4) and every KCALL ended it.  The UI can't see the console's queue.
- The denizens' words drain no differently: a stir, a squeak, a blow at the character are the UI's words from the
  world's events, written by the same t_flush to the same console window, and painted by the same pump.

## What was built

- **Kernel** (base/kernel/call.s, sched.s; base/include/layout.inc): K0_FSRV is the task a foreground task last
  SCALLed, a KCALL not recorded; it's in the foreground whenever it can run till a foreground task calls another.
  FORE_SRV and K0_FSRVT are gone (base/spec/api.def, so hydra.inc and hydracalls.h and both call references made
  again; docs/03-base/kernel.md; t_fore.s's comment).  The background keeps its turns (FORE_RUN): base's fore test
  passes.  A foreground caller's SCALL is two cycles shorter (IRQs off), a KCALL shorter still (it no longer
  touches K0_FSRV).
- **The body's time as a wait or a sleep ends** (dq0w.c's main loop): hurt (bleeding, poisoned, not fine), it's run
  before E_READY whatever the referee's watch, as the lights' minute would; any other long task's end as at fw3 (its
  watch come).  dq0tend's character is hurt, so the lights' minute ran the body's time every minute; looks2 moved
  burn_job into part 27, and it now ran just after E_READY at the same moment: the world's events were E_READY then
  E_TASK TE_BODY, both at 296647, and the sleep's last "You're getting hungry. You're getting thirsty." came after the
  prompt.  pc_waited marks the wait's end (2) for that round only (a knockout's 1 kept).  First built for every long
  task's end (56f0e1a0): in dq0handy's fight that told the referee's news ("You've begun to learn Club by doing it")
  between the reach's steps, the pack never put down, the character dead; narrowed to the wait's end (770460aa).
