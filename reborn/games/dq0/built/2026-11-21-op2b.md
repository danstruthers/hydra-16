## 2. One search (wsearch.c, part 94)

A denizen's search (wtake.c's TW_SEARCH) is the character's own, in place of wtake.c's one-square loop:

- **Its minute and its noise:** the search row by act_noise (E_DEN DE_SEARCHES at its loudness, told seen or heard:
  "The bandit starts searching about." / "rummaging"; said.txt's DEN.searches; no sound played, as the character's
  search plays none).
- **Doors and traps:** wtrap.c's search, wk.who the actor (as wport.c's door work): the referee's P_SEARCH by its
  sheet (ask_w's actor), its whole minute's reach (three squares), the squares it sees its own (WK_SIGHT: the line from
  its eyes, ln_seen94, a byte a row in wk.m, which an actor's work tells nothing by), by touch in the dark beyond its
  darkvision (its reach the eight squares round it, 2 steps harder: the character's own rule). Told nothing (the
  character's E_TRAP and E_PORTAL words its own); a find found for every one, as the character's finds are (PS_HIDDEN,
  TS_HIDDEN cleared). workpart.h's door_state is one for every part now: PP_KNOWN (the character's handling) only for
  the character's work.
- **Things:** each hidden thing lying within that reach (not by magic; its own home's stash left), the character's
  test (P_SEARCH, its hiding the steps worse, 2 more by touch); found, it's uncovered (no longer hidden, as the
  character's own finds are), and its take takes it as anything lying (what it wants).
- **One hiding:** the character hiding where it sees it, by the character's own search's test of a hidden denizen
  (P_SEARCH at FIND_STEPS): found, its hiding ended (part 94's HD_FOUND, "found", its long task stopped); a denizen of
  another faction hiding there, out of hiding.
- **Who searches:** robbers where they lost the character, as before, and now any hostile folk denizen drawn on to
  where it lost the character (dq0d.c's s_draw_on and s_take_way: "searching is anyone's", tailing the character).

## 3. A thing hidden

The character's "hide X" (things.c's s_hide) reads the conceal row (its minute, the long task's kind) and tests by
act_test (0, ...). A robber whose kind hides its things (RB_HIDE: brown orcs) hides its stash by its own Concealment

## 4. Sniff for anyone

A hunter by smell's sniff of the traces' store (wtrscent.c's tr_scent: its ten-second sniff, wbody.c's STEP_SCENT) is
the character's sniff: the sniff row's three seconds on its ready, the referee's P_NOTICE_SMELL by its sheet, its steps
TRACE_STEPS by the strength of the scent it follows (its points, less one each doubling of its age: wtrace.c's found's
rule); a critical failure loses the trail (as the character's sniff notices nothing). The character's sniff and track
times are the rows' (wtrace.c's took[] gone).

## 7. Open questions

1. **A find's knowers.** A door or a trap a denizen's search finds is found for every one (the character sees the
   door; its walk goes round the trap), as the character's finds are for every denizen today. Keep that, or keep
   knowers (a lair's faction, the finder: the act audit's B10) before denizens spring traps?
2. **Who searches.** Robbers and now any hostile folk denizen search where they lost the character (tailing it). The
   user's other natures (exploring, patrolling) aren't built: when should they search, and how often?
3. **Fellows hiding.** A denizen's search doesn't bring its own faction's hiders out of hiding (only another's and the
   character). Right?
4. **A hunter's sniff.** Three seconds a store sniff (every ten seconds at most), and a critical failure loses the
   trail. Should a hunter's following of the journal's fresh trail (dq0d.c's, untested) be tested too?
5. **A stash concealed badly.** A robber's critical failure hides its stash at 0 (dust and corners), still hidden.
