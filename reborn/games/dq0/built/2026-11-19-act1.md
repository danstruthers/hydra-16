## The act table

- **games/dq0w/acts.txt**: one row an act, its numbers the same for every actor, the character's (HOUSE.md's) the
  reference: its time (tenths, or "N step", so many of the actor's own step), its test (the referee's P_*), the steps
  its test is the easier, its noise (how loud, 0-9; the character's E_NOISE's NZ_*; an actor's E_DEN's DE_*), its
  fatigue (GA_WORK or none), whether it's a long task (LT_*: timed, broken off by a command or a blow, its test at its
  end), the tool it wants (KI_*) and the hands.  22 rows: open, shut, slam, lift, bar, unbar, unlock, lock, picking,
  picked, forcing, burst, held (a door's); lid, lidkey, lidpick, lidforce (a lid's); take; dig, dug, digbegin, roof.
- **games/dq0w/acts.js** makes it as the build begins (tools/dq0build.js's generate, after the hub's numbers): every
  name checked against the hub's headers (a wrong one fails the build), obj/gen/dq0acts.inc (the rows, 12 bytes each)
  and obj/gen/dq0acts.h (AC_* by name, struct act, its size checked at compile time).  `node games/dq0w/acts.js`
  prints the table.
- **modules/dq0lib/table.s**: the rows in the paged ROM's library, and their one reader, AC_ROW (the library's jump
  table's $A066, after WK_SEARCH): a row copied into the caller's block.  No main RAM.  (table.s, not act.s: the
  library's sources link in their names' order, and its entry table must come first.)
- **games/dq0w/acts.h**: act_get (q, n), a row read into a part's own block on its C stack (rp_x's XCALL), and the
  senders' declarations.

## What changes, and why

Every changed sentence and timing is the one rule's (the character and every actor alike):

1. **The character's opening and shutting of a door is heard** (E_NOISE NZ_DOOR, loud 2 and 3, at the door), as a
   denizen's DE_OPENS and DE_SHUTS always were.  NZ_DOOR has no sound of its own (E_PORTAL plays the door); the minds
   hear it by the one hearing rule (dq0d's hear draws a denizen on only for a voice or a knock).  The character's path
   was the odd one: its door made no noise at all.
2. **The character's door burst or held is heard** (E_NOISE NZ_DOOR, 7, at the door), as a denizen's DE_BURSTS and
   DE_HOLDS were; before, the character's forcing was heard only as it began.
3. **The character's door noises come from the door's square**, not its own (an actor's always did: den_told's
   DT_DOOR).  One square nearer the far side for the minds.
4. **The character's door forcing begun is NZ_WORK** (it was whatever the work's message last held).
5. **A spell gone badly in poor rock is 7** for the character, as a tunneller's DE_ROOF is (it was 6, the spell's).
6. **A denizen turning its key in a box's lock, or forcing a lid, is told and heard** (DE_TURNS: "The bandit turns a
   key in a lock." or a key in a lock; DE_PRIES: "The bandit strains at a locked lid." or wood creaking), as the
   character's lidkey and lidforce noises are (quiet, 1; loud, 6).  Its time the same rows': 30, 100, 20.
7. **A denizen's door noises are told from inside the door work** (wdoor.c, as the character's), not after it in
   wport.c: the same events, at the same moment of the clock.
