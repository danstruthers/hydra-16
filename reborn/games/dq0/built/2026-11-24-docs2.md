### The new rule-level questions (OPEN.md)

- **Q1. What gets a letter** (P1, P2).
  - Proposed: everything the player can name in a look gets a letter, in the look and in "Which one?". That includes
    features (doors, windows, stairs, a portcullis), and a part of the floor with things on it.
  - Against it: letters for things and denizens alone, as built.
- **Q2. The look and a change of light** (L4).
  - Proposed: a look is told when what the character can see changes, and never otherwise (the user's rule of
    2026-11-14, generalized). A moment's dark inside one act is told in a sentence as it falls, and the light's return
    in a sentence too, with no look if what's seen is then the same.
  - Against it: a whole look whenever a light is lit after none was carried; or, as built, the dip not told.
- **Q3. The plug-ins' boundary** (W1, W5, W9, W11, W12).
  - Proposed: the world owns every name, people, tongue and belief, and the ruleset every number. A creature's kind,
    name and tongue are the world's, its stats the ruleset's. A coin's name is the world's, its worth the ruleset's.
    Any ruleset runs in any world. A life keeps its world and ruleset. The world is asked at the start, as the pace is.
  - Against it: rulesets and worlds paired, and the world chosen by a switch, as now.
- **Q4. Sight's detail by distance** (U1). Proposed: what a looker makes out falls with distance and light, by its
  sight: movement, a shape, a kind, then who. The look and the minds name only what is made out, for every looker.
  Is that the rule, and is it built now or later?
- **Q5. Tuning by play** (U2, U3).
  - Proposed: numbers the book doesn't give are realistic house figures, marked tunable, which the engineer sets now.
    The user tunes them later by play: each occupation's worth (U2), and sound's reach and hearing, by ear (U3).
  - The question: is that the rule? And should occupations be evened out in play, or left as the book makes them?
