## 1. What was built

- **The referee's harm for a creature** (the act audit's G3: "the world can't hurt a denizen but by a blow").
  RS_HARM with its actor (r->actor, an actor's entry plus 1) is now answered for a creature:
  - HarnMaster (dq0harn): routed to the fights' overlay (dq0harn.c: `goto fights`); fight.c's `o_hbeast` (overlay
    10) unpacks its sheet and applies the character's own rule (sheet.c's harm): a fall by the book's harn_fall, a
    strike or a burn by its dice and its impact more, the armour off it its hide or what it wears (fight.c's guard,
    as a blow's); the wound by the same code a blow's wound takes (o_strike's tail factored out as `wounded`: its
    shock, a stumble, a fumble, knocked senseless by knocked_out with the creature's own fall and rolls). So **K14 holds
    for every actor**: a creature knocked out by a fall or a trap rolls its shock a round each as its time comes,
    exactly as one knocked out by a blow (the world's DO_DOWN and out_still/RS_ROUSE path). The reply: its effects, the
    worst band, the tenths out, AF_BLEEDING in c.
  - The null referee: the creature's own wounds count (sheet[]), by the null's own harm numbers.
- **The world's harm to a body** (wstep.c, part 115's `den_harm`): workpart.h's harm, for an actor's work (wk.who),
  hands the request it made to den_harm, which asks it at the actor and applies it to body bd as a blow's reply is
  applied (wfight.c): its legs (C_MOVE), fallen prone, senseless (DO_DOWN, ready at its time out), bleeding, its
  weapon let fall, dead (its remains and things where it lies). It's told as a blow is: E_FIGHT with a new outcome
  FO_HURT (hub.h), the referee's words in the fights' ring, so the minds take it to heart (a kin's fall dismays,
  killed records it dead) with no change in dq0d.
- **Traps underfoot, every actor's** (G1). A denizen's I_STEP (wbody.c) asks dq0w.c's `den_hz` (main RAM, the cheap
  test: a pit it's in, a set trap on the square ahead or underfoot, a pool) and only then part 114's `den_trap`:
  - before the step, a hidden trap ahead: the referee's P_NOTICE_SIGHT, a passing glance's (b 1), its hiding the steps
    worse: the character's own test (wtrap.c's step), asked of its actor. Noticed: it stops short (a refused step's
    second), the trap known (TS_HIDDEN cleared), as a denizen's search find is (op2b: untold);
  - on it, the trap set off by wtrap.c's own step and spring (wk.who its actor; the character's work kept round it):
    a pit's cover gives way (T_PIT) and it falls in (HC_FALL, its depth) or onto spikes; a deadfall, blade or darts
    dodged (P_DODGE_HAZARD, its actor's) or struck by; gas resisted or choked on (senseless half a minute a point of
    its power, prone, the character's choke's time); an alarm's bell rung at its square (E_NOISE 9, the minds hear it).
- **A door's trap, every actor's** (B9): workpart.h's front arms a door's trap for any opener, so a denizen (or an ally
  on an errand) opening, forcing or breaking in a trapped door sets it off by the same parts; a known one is left shut
  (PO_TRAPPED) for every actor, as for the character.
- **A known trap gone round** by the denizens' walks (dq0d's wet_ok: a set, found floor trap is no square for their
  ways, as the character's walk goes round a trap it knows of). "Known" is found by anyone (op2b's rule); no faction
  knowledge.
- **A denizen in a pit climbs out** a round at a time (part 114's DT_PIT, the character's pit climb's numbers: the
  referee's P_CLIMB, a critical success 20 feet, a success 10; a slip off the wall back to its floor; a critical
  failure off it a fall from as high as it got, its harm den_harm's). Its progress kept in wbody.c's bclimb[] (8 bytes).
- **H15, every actor's:** already so (ans1: a denizen's step asks the same stumble roll); kept, now asked from den_hz
  after any trap. Burning pools still burn only the character (H19, open: now that a creature can be harmed, it is a
  small step, not taken).
- **The packer** (games/dq0/parts.js's banks): the parts' records (SWTAB, swap.inc's SWPART, 5 bytes a part) live in
  overlay 2's bank but come from bank.s, whose object isn't in the sizes the packer reads: overlay 2's room was
  overcounted by 580 bytes. Three more parts made it overfill unseen (the link failed). The packer now takes 5 bytes a
  part off that bank's room.

**Not changed for the character.** Its trap step, its door traps, its harm, its K14 and its pools are the same code and
the same calls in the same order: no sentence of the character's own changes but where a denizen's trap now happens
in front of it.

## 6. Open questions

1. **Fliers and traps**: a winged body (BD_WINGS) sets off a floor trap (a plate, a wire, a pit's cover) and slips in
   oil as any body (as built: no exception made). Should a flier pass over them?
2. **A known trap**: denizens go round any trap found by anyone (op2b's "found for every one"). Keep, or knowers per
   faction (with A13)?
3. **A creature's courage when hurt by a trap**: a blow's wound tests its courage (A14); a trap's doesn't (as built).
4. **An unseen harm**: "You hear a cry of pain." at any distance an actor is (actors are within 24 squares), through
   walls. Tell it only within hearing (the sound layer), or not at all?
5. **Fighting from a pit**: a creature fallen into a pit beside the character fights it from there (as built, as the
   world allows the character); and the senses don't show a creature in a pit beside the character ("Something").
   Rules for both?
6. **The pit climb's out step**: a denizen climbed out pays its step's time too (the character's climb out ends on
   the edge with no step more). Small; same rule wanted?
7. **The trap words' timing**: a denizen's trap beat (E_BEAT) is told only if the senses' last look saw its new square;
   the hurt row (by name) is told regardless, so a seen harm may come without "Darts hiss out of the wall ...". The
   hurt row now names what hurt it, so it reads alone. Fold the trap's words into the hurt row instead?
8. **The world's main RAM**: den_hz and trap_sq cost some 370 bytes (cc65's code for the at() calls, the heading's
   offsets and the trap records' loop). In assembly they'd be some 120. A first version tested the chunk alone and
   swapped part 114 in for every step in a trapped chunk (165 bytes less), but that held the body longer at each step
   and dq0fightmid's played game lost its poke to it, 3 runs of 3. Worth the assembly now?
9. **The packer's SWTAB fix** (games/dq0/parts.js) is a shared build file: other branches adding parts meet the same
   failure without it.
