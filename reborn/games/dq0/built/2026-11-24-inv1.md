## 1. The invariants (sim/lib/dq0inv.js)

sim/test.js's dq0 instrument calls them in every dq0 test (both HUBOWN branches), after dq0Check. `INV=0` turns
them off. The recorder (sim/lib/dq0meaning.js) keeps what they need:
- each actor's body (BD_*) and the tile under it, and the character's;
- each item's kind's keeps and the weight it bears;
- each denizen intent's actor (its state, doing and name) as the intent is posted;
- each denizen event's actor as it's told, and an E_DEN's square's tile;
- the denizens the senses' record shows, a line at a time (game.seen). These are the look about and the looks one
  way, read again whenever the sight bank is rewritten, and those the world tells seen (E_DEN's seen).

**The state's**, at every line's snapshot and at the test's end, for each game:

| Name | Rule | What it checks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| place | T1 | Each thing in one place: its holder exists, it isn't in itself, its holders don't loop, no two records share an id, it's never held and worn at once, and IW_ACTOR is the character's only |
| hands | B5 | The character's left and right hand hold one thing each, by their marks (IH_BOTH counts in both). A denizen with two hands (BD_TWO) holds two things at most, a beast's mouth one. No held thing has did 0 (the code review's finding 1) |
| hoard | T2 | A faction's hoard (owner OW_HOARD) holds no more weight in its first compartment than it bears (by things.c's s_weight_of) |
| handed | P2 | No E_BEAT WB_HANDS_TO from an actor to itself (finding 4) |
| posts | B3 | A senseless actor posts no intent. This is on the known list (section 3) |
| acts | B3 | A senseless body does nothing. It strikes no blow (an E_FIGHT that isn't a rise, a rearm or a bleeding out), takes no step and works no door or dig (E_DEN) as it lies |
| rock | B2 | The character never stands in rock or a wall, and a denizen never steps into one (its E_DEN DE_MOVED's square) |

The rock check counts only the world's own steps. A test's pokes put denizens anywhere: dq0Alone parks them in the
rock, and dq0Poke puts a foe on whatever square is in front of the character.

**The words'**, over every reply to a game line (the story after a "> " prompt, as dq0Answers reads it):

| Name | What it checks |
| :--- | :--- |
| repeat | No sentence comes more than three times in one reply, but those told as they go by the user's decisions (GOES): a fight's blows, falls, risings, turns and tries to rise; a grapple's rounds; a fire-maker's tries (2026-11-02); a dig's and a climb's spells; a battering's beats; each stir's "Something stirs."; an incline's "The way rises." (dq0incline asks for two or more) |
| feet | No "1 feet" |
| twin | No "a X and a X" |
| stop | No sentence runs into the next with no space after its full stop |
| unseen | No denizen is named ("The bandit ...", "Grub ...") in a reply when neither its line nor the line before saw one of that name |

"Nothing told twice in one answer" (WORLD-MODEL.md section 7) was every dq0 test's already (tests/lib/dq0.js's
dq0Check), and it stays as it was.

## 7. Assumptions (each a rule's)

- **The same sound, merged.** A sound heard again while the character is busy at one command is told "again", then
  "again and again", then not again till the command ends (section 7: nothing told twice; P1, as one hears a squeak
  come and go). Three sounds are remembered at once.
- **Food given is eaten as it's taken,** one of a lot, the receiver's meal's time (T3: food eaten is used up; the
  mind counted it eaten). This holds for any actor given food.
- **A gift is taken by the take rule** (a hand free, else put away, else set down at its feet: B5, P2).
- **A hoard's lid is lifted for the takings and let down again;** what doesn't fit lies by it (T2: a thing is put
  only where it can rest). The time to open and close it is not charged yet.
- **A beast owns what it takes up,** by a did of its own (P2). It still has no outfit, and its lair's home isn't read
  in for its things, as before. So a beast's thing can be paged out with its home chunk: a one-path rewrite matter.
- **A drawn or retaken weapon ends a creature's fists** (B5, P2: the character uses what it holds).
- **A dodge marks Dodge; a block marks the thing that blocked; rising marks nothing** (Ac3). Blocking with a torch
  marks Club by the book (a torch is a club swung), so Club still opens in 46 transcript lines, each with a torch, a
  lantern or a club in hand. The message is told some lines after the block that marked it, so it can read as if it
  came from the fists (dq0flee).
- **"don" and "put on" are wear** (P4: wear takes only what's worn; DECISIONS.md:2683-2684 left "don" as one line).
- **A foe told seen as it closes is named in its blows** till the senses look again (S1: what the world tells seen is
  seen).
- **The refusal names the way asked:** "There's no way up here." (WORLD-MODEL.md section 7: one wording per action;
  the look shows the way down).

## 8. Open questions

- **Merge an incline's repeated "The way rises."?** dq0incline asks for two or more, a 4-foot rise at a time (slice
  4.10). The rule I'd add: "a walk's beat that's the same as the last one told isn't told again until something else
  is told". It settles six "The way rises." to one, and the turns and bends that repeat on a winding passage.
- **The tests' kit (dq0 -k) "held all the same".** Putting the overflow at the character's feet is the rule (B5).
  It's left as it is here because it re-sets some 60 tests' scenes. It's for scene1's scene builder, or for a rule:
  "a scene's things go only where the take rule would put them".
- **"Something stirs." more than three times in one reply** (dq0denpace: 5 in one wait). Each stir is excused as its
  own telling. The rule I'd add: "an answer says 'Something stirs.' once, and later stirs in it give only what
  changed".
