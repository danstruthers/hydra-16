## What's built, and why

### 1. A drawn weapon keeps its hand in a fight

"A drawn weapon keeps its hand in a fight; outside one, the home-first order stands."  The hands' rule (dq0w's
things.c, part 90: `s_room`) skips a weapon in hand (its kind's craft, `AP_WEAPON`) while the character is engaged: a
foe near (`near_one`) and its own square in an enemy's zone (`zone_step`, the reach's own test of a fight).  The light,
last of the ranks, is laid down instead.  dq0reachflee's fight (seed 7's ghoul; a sword and a lit torch in hand; "take
the rations from the backpack"): "You lay the torch on the ground, still burning.", the sword kept in hand (before, the
sword went first: its home the pack, the first rank).  Out of a fight the order is as it was: a sword to its scabbard,
the light last.

### 2. A rest puts a burning light out first

"A rest puts a burning light out first (for every actor)."  As a wait begins asleep (a sleep or a rest: I_WAIT's
WA_SLEEP, dq0w.c's `s_intent_few`), `rest_dark` puts out each light the character carries burning, each told by
E_BEAT's limbs' row (WB_DOUSE, its kind: "You put out your torch.", said.txt's BEAT.douse, its {Actor} and {its} every
actor's), and the light it carries is made again (`light_now`).  The UI's own words for the rest ("You settle down to
rest for eight hours.") wait for the world's E_TIME when a light was burning (task.c's `o_wait_go` reads the hub's
pc_light, on the C stack: the overlay's statics are its bank's), so they come after the light's: "You put out your
torch. You settle down to rest for eight hours."  A wait awake keeps its light.
