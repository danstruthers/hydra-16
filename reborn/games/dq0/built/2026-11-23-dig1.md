## What changed

1. **One spell of digging** (dq0w.c's `s_dig_spell`, part 1, beside the rock's code; its stub `dig_spell`). The
   character's spell (`s_dig_on`) and a tunneller's (wdig.c's `s_den_dig`) both call it. It works out the rock the dig
   is into, asks the referee's test by who (RS_TEST P_DIG, actor 0 or the actor's entry plus 1), takes the referee's
   rate and wear, applies the rock's hardness and poor rock coming down, adds the feet made, and finds a hidden door
   dug into (found for every one; the character's own find also handled, PP_KNOWN, as before). `struct dig` gained the
   digger's stratum and stand (`sn`, `sx`, `sy`), `found`, `grade`, and an actor's `wear` and `key`.
   - `who | DIG_ASK` asks only whether anything bites (RS_DIG_QUERY): the character's own question as its dig begins,
     now asked for a denizen too as its dig begins.
   - `DIG_RECORD` is a record's spell away from the character: the referee's rate with no dice (RS_DIG_QUERY), where
     wdig.c used its own copy of the rates.
   - `DIG_LOOK` is the wall looked at (`wall_look`, the rock's code's).
   - Its locals are static in part 1's image (no other part is called until its last step), to fit the part.
2. **The world's copy of the rates is gone** (wdig.c's `pick[]`, `spade[]`, `claws[]` and its own RS_TEST). A
   denizen's tools are now its own things, by their kinds' keys (a pick, a shovel, a spade, a mattock, a knife, a
   dagger, a blade; a chisel only with a hammer), plus a burrower's claws. They're told to the referee in the referee's
   own tools form (eight bytes a key). A record's tools stay its DG_* bits, since its things aren't looked at away from
   the character.
3. **The referee** (rf/dig.c, rshouse.h). `house_dig_tool` takes an actor's list. `house_dig` (new) gives the tool, its
   rate and its wear for every actor, in both referees. The table has a new `claws` row (the house's numbers that wdig.c
   had). dq0harn's `dig_of` and dq0null's P_DIG branch use it. A creature's grade is still by its own sheet on its own
   dice (`s_quiet_test`; the null's QUIET flat). To make room in dq0harn's part 3, `speaks` moved to part 4.
4. **A denizen's tool wears** as the character's does. things.c's `wear` takes `wear_did` (an actor's did); for an
   actor it leaves the UI's request alone.
5. **The act table** (acts.txt). The `dig` and `digbegin` rows carry the spell's 6000 tenths; before, both sides had
   their own SPELL. New rows: `digfound` (loud 6, DE_DIGFOUND), `hang` (6000, loud 4, DE_HANGS) and `wallup` (6000 a
   spell, six of them, loud 4, DE_WALLS). The builder's work reads these through `act_noise` (it had its own numbers).
6. **The minds**. dq0d.c's `dig_way` takes a hidden door for the wall it seems (`dug_as`), so a tunneller digs at it.
   The two `>= DE_CLOSES` checks (caps.c, dq0d.c) are bounded to DE_CLOSES..DE_AIMS, so the new DE_DIGFOUND is heard
   and told like any work.
7. **Words**. said.txt's `DEN.digfound`: "The dwarf miner strikes a door behind the facing." (heard: "a scraping in
   rock").
8. **Parts**. The character's `s_dig_on`, `roof_harm`, `cut_corner` and `spell` moved to a new part 114 (bank 2):
   part 1 had no room for the one spell beside the rock's code.
