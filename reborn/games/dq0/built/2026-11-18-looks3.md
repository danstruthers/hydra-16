## What was built

### The senses' look one way cheaper

- **The things seen from the near list** (things.c's things_lamps, things_seen).  The look's lamps' pass of the item
  store lists the records lying near (24 at most); a look about used it, a look one way went through the store again.
  The list now reaches as far as the look (WAY_R for a look one way), so a look one way uses it too: some 75,000 cycles
  of what it perceives.  Too many near, the store's gone through as before.
- **The eyes' reach asked only till the first wanted** (view.c's chunks).  A far part makes no chunk; it asked of every
  chunk not made whether its eyes reach it, to keep the first for the rest.  It stops asking once it has one.
- **The light layer's grids only where a look one way may see** (view.c's way_in, way_box: the half ahead, a diagonal's
  quarter, a glance's reach).  The rest of the view's chunks the look can't see; their grids are made as a look about
  wants them.

### The senses given the CPU while an answer is in hand

- **The UI's quick naps** (main.c).  After a command was taken, the UI looked at the keys every tick for a quarter
  second: each look a console call, some 9,000 cycles with the console's own.  The world's events ring the UI anyway.
  At the real paces the quick naps are now taken only on a pass where the UI wrote something (its answer's words
  streaming out).  At the turns pace they're as before.  This was the largest gain: some 200,000 cycles of a look one
  way.
- **The world asleep sixteen ticks at a time while a look's worked out** (dq0w.c's looked; at the real paces; four
  before).  Its waiting wakes on a ring (the senses' stock, the UI's things), the clock running on from the ticks gone.
- **A look one way in part 37** (dq0w.c's s_look_way, s_look_rest), with the intent that asks it (intent_more).  Part 22
  is no longer copied in for it, and part 37 is no longer copied back after: some 50,000 cycles at its start and before
  its words.
- **A look one way asked before its first words** (the UI's way.c s_look_way).  The intent goes before "You peer
  ahead." is said, so the world and the senses start on it as the words go out.  The first letter comes some 50,000
  cycles later (still well within the 720,000 target), and the far part some 150,000 sooner.
- **The look's record summed after its words, in the main RAM** (look.c's sight_sum_of).  Each look copied the UI's part
  8 in (27,000 cycles) to keep the sum a light's change is compared with.
- **The denizens' task and the sound task** nap 40 ticks, not 10, while a command's answer is in hand at the real paces
  (dq0d.c, dq0s.c).  The sound task does so only when nothing's playing.  The minds are held meanwhile anyway (the
  senses' sense_held), and the world's events ring both.  The same rule for every actor's look.
- **A look one way's rest** is asked only after three seconds free (dq0w.c's look_due, REST_IDLE).  A player typing on
  (dq0lookfar's look at once, 0-4M cycles after the look across) isn't kept waiting behind the rest's finish and stock.
  The menacing look at once had 4.6M once.
