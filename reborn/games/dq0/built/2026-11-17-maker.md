# The maker ahead of the eyes, and the map a row at a time (2026-11-17; as built, for review)

The D17 misses where the maker made the player wait (built/2026-11-17-d17.md's table): the look across a cavern, its
longest pause 17.1M cycles (a chunk made in the foreground as the eyes reached it, 14M-21M), and the map, its longest
pause 5.0M (the whole map drawn before its first row) and its first letter 684,961 (one typing 854,650).  The user's
decisions (2026-11-17): the maker builds the map's pieces ahead of the character's eyes, in the background (the
chunks the character could see or reach next; the background giving way to commands as the tasks' give-way does; the
cost the cache's room, "made way for round, as the other stores"; generation deterministic: the same seed makes the
same chunk whenever it's made); and the map drawn row by row.  Branch maker, from f1cdc9ec, rebased on
reborn-commands' e31523a4 (polish, loadguard and looks merged: looks' near part first had brought the look across to
15.0M there) and then on 43832bd7 (pb merged); the baselines are the coordinator's runs of those heads, mB3 and mB4.

## What was built, and why

### The map a row at a time

- **Its first words at once.**  The UI says "The map (you face ...):" and posts the map's request (look.c's
  `s_map_begin`) before it asks the referee for the panel's lines (main.c's `s_sheet_map`), so the first letter isn't
  behind the referee, the panel or the drawing.
- **Each row as it's drawn.**  The maker writes each row into the UI bank as it finishes it, the row's letters first
  and its layers (the colours) after, their last byte last (map.c).  The UI marks each row's last layer byte 0xFF
  before it asks; it shows a row once that byte has changed, sending what it has said so far (`t_flush`) while it
  waits a few ticks for the next (look.c's `s_map`).  A row with a passage's squares in it waits for the two rows
  after it, since a passage's slant (`slants`, 2026-11-01's) is worked out from the rows round it; the slants are
  worked out a row at a time now, into the row's copy (the same marks as before: proved by the transcripts).
- **The rows as before.**  The first row shown is the first seen (north up: the top row else); a row with nothing on
  it after the first is held back till a row with something comes, so the map's end is where it was; the panel's lines
  beside the rows, or under them, as before.
- The maker's map (map.c, its part 1) already wrote each row as it drew it; it's unchanged but for its room, 35
  bytes to 69 (the north-up window's far edges worked out once: the headroom rule's flag on it lifted).

### The chunks made ahead

- **Which** (ahead.c's `ahead_next`): by one rule for every actor (ONE-PATH.md; every body in the hub by one loop, the
  character's, actor 0's, first), the chunks round each that it could see or reach next: its own, and those whose
  nearest square is within its eyes' reach and eight steps more, or, in the quarter it faces, within twice its eyes'
  reach (a look one way's, 31 squares at most) and the steps; the nearest first.  The eyes' reach is the senses'
  look's (4 squares and 2 for each point of sight, to 9); a denizen's sight isn't in the hub, so the most a kind's (an
  asymmetry noted below).  Looked at again only as an actor crosses into another sixteen squares' block, turns, or the
  cache changes.
- **Where** (ahead.c's `ahead_room`): a slot the readers see as free (its `used` 0: the world, the senses and the
  minds find nothing there, so nothing is placed, stocked or seen in it): the last free one, `pick`'s taking the first;
  else one whose chunk made ahead no actor wants now.  A chunk a reader has is never made way for by one made ahead
  (see the open question).
- **How** (make.c's `make_ahead`): the chunk is grown, the part of its making that's the seed's alone (its squares,
  rooms, uses, fixtures, sky, water and air), and kept in the free slot with what its plan of uses left for its stock
  (`struct ustate`, in the chunk's `kept`, idle till it's finished).  It's finished only when a reader first asks for
  it (`make_chunk`): taken into the slot `pick` gives, as it would have been made, and finished there and then: the
  change records laid over it, its things stocked, its sconces, the sky's marks and what's been seen of it, the world as
  it is at that moment, the moment a chunk not made ahead is made.  So the chunk, its things and the cache are the same
  byte for byte, and only the time differs.
- **When** (dq0m.c): in the background, when nothing's asked of the maker, 60 ticks after the last request (a reader's
  requests come in bursts: a new stratum's chunks one after another).  It gives way, as the senses' look does to a
  command (dq0p's `sense_held`: from a line's first key 600 ticks at most, from its Enter 100; at the turns pace while
  it's in hand); and while the game's starting, while the character's long task is under way (a walk, a wait, a rest,
  a dig: the hub's `pc_task`), once it's dead, and at the turns pace while the world's turn goes on (the character not
  ready again, its intents not all taken: the denizens catching up).  Held where it is at the growers' checkpoints
  (`ahead_check`), asleep 25 ticks at a time for a command or keys, 40 for the game's own time; at each checkpoint the
  other tasks go first (`hy_yield`), so it has the time no one else wants.  A request for anything else (another
  chunk, the map, the game's end) gives it up at the next checkpoint, the stacks it was called with put back
  (`setjmp`, bank.s's `ahead_mark`, `ahead_back`); a request for the very chunk it's making makes it on, in the
  kernel's foreground if the asker is.  With nothing to make it wakes every 40 ticks to look again (the actors alone,
  a few thousand cycles; the strata's records copied only when the choice is made again).
- **The checkpoints**: each throw of the dice (place.c's `dr`), the growers' loops (built.c's tries, rooms, joins,
  each row of a zone's floor, its flood fill every four squares; caves.c's; pass.c's joins and crossings;
  fixtures.c's; plan.c's detail every 32 steps; uses.c's floors per room), and between a chunk's parts (its ways, each
  corner, its grower, fixtures, sky, water and air).  Abandoning at any of them leaves nothing behind but the chunk
  being grown: the plan's detail is marked whole only once it's worked out, and every other store the growers touch is
  either the chunk's own or keyed again before it's next used (the dice by `dkey`, the library's by `rseed`).  The
  gaps between them: below, in "What's made ahead, and its cost".
- **The maker's own foreground.**  The UI no longer puts the maker in the kernel's foreground (main.c's `FS_MAKE`
  gone).  The maker puts itself there as the UI did (for the map, and while no command's in hand), but only while one
  in the foreground waits on its request (the world, the senses, the UI: dq0m.c's `fore`), never as it makes ahead.
  The UI's rule would have given the background's work the foreground's time between commands.
- **A chunk asked for twice at once** (two readers missing it in the cache before the maker made it) is found made
  by the second request (make.c's `ahead_found`): before, it was made again into a second slot.
- **Room.**  The main RAM's room (3,852 bytes free before) paid for most of it: `grow` and plan.c's `detail` went to a
  new overlay 10 with ahead.c, caves.c's slants to overlay 2; the chooser's tables lie in the stock list's room (idle
  but as a chunk's finished).  Part 2's image went to bank 10 (it had been put in 5, 9 full).
- **Quicker.**  built.c's flood fill (`connected`) by offsets among the squares (some three times quicker), and the
  dice's remainder (`dr`) a byte at a time, not by a 32-bit division (that division was a seventh of the growers'
  time): the same numbers, proved byte for byte below.
- **The chooser kept cheap.**  Each actor's place is worked out once a look (`stands`: its chunk and its distances
  to the chunks round it), a denizen on another's square looked at once, and the choice looked at again only as an
  actor crosses into another sixteen squares' block, turns, or the cache changes: looked at for every denizen's step,
  it took a twentieth of the game's time at the normal pace.

## Open questions

1. **"Made way for round": a reader's chunk is never made way for by one made ahead.**  The user's cost was "the
   cache's room, made way for round, as the other stores".  Built: a chunk made ahead takes a slot the readers have
   free, or one of its own that no actor wants now; it never takes a reader's.  Why: what's in the cache is read by
   more than the readers' finds.  Travel's `of_kind` and the far glows look only at the chunks in the cache, and a
   chunk's seen map is written to the save as it's made way for (`seen_keep`).  So a chunk made ahead that made way
   for a reader's would change what the game says and saves.  Measured: with that eviction, the -C games (twelve
   slots) said other things.  The consequence: once the readers' chunks fill the cache (24 slots, 12 with -C), nothing
   more is made ahead until a slot comes free.  The scratch proofs' games, 2 to 24 chunks each, made ahead all
   through; a long game's wanderings fill the cache and stop it.  The choices:
   (a) as built;
   (b) made-ahead chunks make way for the reader's chunk furthest from every actor, with the game's words and saves
       changing as the cache does;
   (c) a room of the maker's own for chunks made ahead (2,707 bytes each, three to an 8K bank), the cache's readers
       untouched.
   The user to choose.
2. **The look across still pauses longer than 1.79M** (4,612,121, the median).  The maker's share of it is the
   finishing of its chunk made ahead, 0.8M; the rest, some 4M, is the senses' whole look one way, made at once
   because the test's poke leaves no last look standing (the looks branch's open question 3).  And the gain needs
   the player's time: dq0lookfar now has the character listen at the cavern's edge and pause some nine seconds
   (sixteen waits of 2M cycles) before the look across; typed at once on arriving, the chunk would be made as the eyes
   reach it, as before (some 14M of the maker's).  Is that pause the scene the user wants held?  And should the test
   bring the character there by walking, so its last look stands and its near part comes first?
3. **A denizen's sight isn't in the hub**, so the chooser gives every denizen the most a kind's (9).  It makes ahead
   for a denizen as far as the keenest could see.
4. **The headroom rule's flag in dq0answer**: turns step forward's first letter is 668,511 (93% of the 720,000 target;
   666,058, 92.5%, on 43832bd7: flagged there already).  The making ahead's cost there: the maker runs to its next
   checkpoint as the line comes (most under 20,000 cycles), and the background's share at the turns pace is a full
   turn.  By the rule its time is to be won back before the next feature: finer checkpoints in the built strata's
   growers (the library's dice, random, have none of their own), or the senses' and the world's own time.
5. **The background gives way to more than commands.**  The user's rule was "giving way to commands as the tasks'
   give-way does".  Built so, it took the game's own time where nobody types (a long wait 52% slower, a descent at the
   turns pace 25M cycles more, dq0hard out of its cycles), so it also holds while the game starts, while the
   character's long task runs, once it's dead, and at the turns pace while the world's turn goes on.  The chunks are
