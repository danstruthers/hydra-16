## What was built, and why

### The grammar records roles (dq0c)

- **Role tags.**  A rule used in a role is tagged where it's used: `<object:other>`, `<object:tool>`,
  `<hand-phrase:hand>`, `<worn-phrase:worn>`, `<locative:loc>`, `<object-one:list>`, `:skip` (words that are the verb's
  own, or name nothing: "of the room", "look under", "search around for"), and `:mine` OR'd ("my left arm").  The
  grammar table carries a name's role in its leaf (T_NAME $40 | role << 1 | rule's top bit; T_OPEN $60 | role, grammar
  version 9); the index marks the rules that hold a role (khi bit 6), so the walk enters no other.
- **The walk** (cmdmatch.s's cm_roles; commandcompile.js's rolesOf is the host's): once ends() says the line parses,
  the derivation is walked from "line": an alternation's first option that can end there (an indexed one by its
  options' first words), a sequence's items each begun as late as they can, an option taken if it can, a repeat's
  fewest rounds; each token gets the innermost role (tokrole).  A line the grammar doesn't take has none (cm_ok) and
  keeps the old rule's other thing (after the thing said and its preposition).
- **The meaning** (cmdmean.s's cm_mean; commandwords.js's scan, its host twin) fills the record by role, not by word
  order.  The noun-phrase rule: a phrase's head is its last content word; the word before it is kept, "qualifier head",
  when it makes one kind another (49 words, generated from the catalogue's names, the compartments' nouns and the
  bestiary's kinds) or it's a side ("left arm"); a room part's kind is its head's.  The UI's `named` matches each word
  of it (nameis.s).  Deleted with it: resolve.c's side-pocket rule, nameis.s's qualifier list, errand.c's given_to.
- **Room made for it.**  The meaning records 255 of 255 went to 110: a record no longer holds its word's verb (a byte
  an entry, gwv), so records that differed only by their verb merged.  The aliases moved from dq0c's RAM into the
  words' bank (TABLES_MAX 14,592 -> 12,800), which paid for the walk's BSS.  No two-byte record index was needed.

### One noun phrase in the grammar, and the missing forms (2.8 step 3)

- `<thing>` merged into `<object>`: every slot takes where a thing lies ("give the dagger to the dwarf ahead", "throw
  the dagger at the rat to my left", "take the key from the chest ahead" each parse now; they didn't).
- The forms: "remove X from Y" (taken from it); "climb on", "sit in"; "get in", "get out", "get on", "get off", "get
  out of"; "in", "out"; "look under" and "look behind" (a search of that thing); "the north wall" anywhere; "drop X on
  (onto) Y".
- The 46 floor, wall, tracks and search phrase verbs are gone.  commands.verbs' new [turns] makes a verb another by its
  thing's name ("look at the floor": the floor's look) or by its own words, the grammar's skip, each in turn and
  anywhere in the line ("search around for": a grope, then a seek; "put the goblin in a headlock": a hold).  A thing's
  name doesn't turn a verb whose own preposition makes another action ("look in the wall" stays a look in it, refused;
  "look for the tracks" is the tracks' look, as it was).
- "empty X (onto Y)" isn't built: no verb in the game empties a thing (an open question, below).

### The user's parser answers

- **"pick" always picks**: PA_PICK, a lock picked with no key tried (a door's in wdoor.c, every actor's: the denizens'
  gate takes it too; a thing's lock in things.c).  "unlock" uses its key if one fits, as built; "unlock ... with the
  lockpicks" picks.
- **"take all from" names what's taken in one list**: "You take a chisel and an iron key from the case." (part 105's
  took_kind and took_list; more than five kinds, "everything").  A hidden compartment found is emptied with the rest;
  one not found isn't.
- **A thing put or dropped on a part of the place**: on the floor or the ground, put down; on the stairs, placed there
  when its stairway is the character's own square, else "The stairway is out of reach: go up to it first."; refused
  where nothing can rest (a wall, a door, a window, a portcullis, a pillar, a ladder, the way out), dropped as put.
- **"say" alone shows the quoted form**: 'Put the words in quotes: say "hello".'
- **The desk's hidden compartment is "the secret drawer"**; "the drawer" is still the one that locks.

### The corpus held to the role record

commandcorpus.js (the report tier's check) holds each case's action to the game's meaning, and the things its roles
name (object, instrument, source, container, target) to the record's thing or other.  It doesn't hold an answer to a
question, a line of the player's own shortcut, several commands in a line (parts.c's), or a role's thing the line
doesn't name.  The aliased actions (activate, negotiate, sense a lie) map to their verbs' (use, persuade, examine).
Cases put as the record has them: the wall's look, a look under a thing a search, a flight, a denizen held, thrown or
grabbed the UI's grapple.  phraseSem's cases the record lacked are ported to it: "knock out the rat" and "knock the
daylights out of the rat" a punch, "put the goblin in a headlock" a hold.  phraseSem itself stays only behind
commandtables.js --check, which no test runs (an open question).

### The line's raw reads

All the UI's reads of the typed line by strstr are gone (26 at f7468c55): hdens.c's fast and "and", hmove.c's dive,
hplaces.c's out (two), hself.c's break, hthings.c's wash, sharpen, hone, whet and " on ", main.c's hide locatives (six)
and kneel, minds.c's five, parts.c's "and", task.c's rest and short, tend.c's change, left and right, time.c's until,
watch and star, and worn.c's " my ".  Four readers went with them: the hand's (s_hand_said), given_to, worn.c's and the
hide's locatives.  Each now reads the record (a role, a verb row's aux, a turn).

Still reading the line, and why:
- pace.c: the pace and watchfulness settings ("set pace to sprint", "autotarget at medium range"), a sub-language
  read before the parse;
- alias.c and names.c: the shortcut and naming commands, and a name's letters made its "#id", before the parse;
- task.c's wait_of: a wait's length ("3 hours and 20 minutes", "until dawn"), which the record has no field for;
- main.c's place_go: "far", "near", "other", "left", "right", "corner", "centre" of a place in the room;
- blows.c: a grapple's move by the line's first letter (pin, choke, tackle); hself.c: "heal"; errand.c: entertain's
  verb (sing, play, dance).  Each could be a verb row's aux (commands.verbs) in step 4's one vocabulary;
- parts.c: a list's verb carried to its later parts; minds.c: the quoted words, read at the record's quote place;
  refuse.c: the refusals that say the line back.

## Open questions

1. *"empty X (onto Y)"* (2.8 step 3) isn't built: no world operation pours or tips things out.  What should "empty the
   sack" do: its things put down where the character stands (or on Y), a liquid poured away, its time?
2. *A thing put on a ledge, in a corner, at the centre or side of a room, in a passage*: still refused as a put (a drop
   puts it down at the character's feet).  The record names the place; the world has no square for "on the ledge" (a
   zone's edge, above or below) or "in the corner" without a walk there.  Placed after a walk there?
3. *phraseSem* (commandcompile.js's old semantics) now holds nothing the suite checks; commandtables.js --check still
   runs the corpus through it, through the encoded tables.  Delete it, and that check with it?
4. *A line the grammar doesn't take* is refused ("I don't understand that."), as before; its record keeps the old
   rule's reading for the UI's other uses (a choice's answer).  Keep it so?
5. *The line's remaining raw reads* (above): a grapple's move, "heal", the entertainments' verbs and a place's words
   could be verb rows and words of the record (step 4's one vocabulary); a wait's length would need a field.  Do them
   with step 4?
