# Every command's first words at once; the timing tests at every pace (2026-11-17; as built, for review)

The user's two decisions on the D17 targets (DECISIONS.md's "The D17 targets rethought (the user's, 2026-11-17)"):
every command says its first words at once, in the story, by said.txt's rows (a status line's change isn't the
answer), its first letter within 720,000 cycles; and the timing tests run at the menacing pace too, and once as
played (no test switches), held to the same targets.  Branch fw, from f7468c55, rebased onto reborn-commands at
e31523a4 (polish, loadguard and looks merged), then at 43832bd7 (pb on it) for its last build and suite.

## What was built, and why

### First words at once

Each is said by the UI before the world is asked (or, for a world-side beginning, by the world as the action
begins), by a said.txt row; the world's later words of the same action are not said again (`worded`, once a line).

- **An attack at a denizen beside the character**: "You close with the giant rat." (said.txt's `FIRST.close`, its
  piece `@closes`), said as its target is found (blows.c's `fight_target`, `found`), before the wield's requests and
  the fight.  Out of reach: "You go over to the giant rat." (`FIRST.over`), by the name it's seen by (the hub's actor
  name, as `close_word` reads it), not the word typed ("You go over to the orc." was the first build's; the old
  "You go over to the small orc." came from the word after the resolver).  In the dark: "You close with something."
  (as its blows are told).  `engage` uses the same row.
- **Its hands empty**, a question may come first ("You can fight it with your fists, but would you rather draw
  your sword?": its first words): the closing words are held while the weapon's chosen (choose.c's `wielded` marks
  `worded` 0x80; blows.c's `found` holds them unless something's in a hand, the UI bank's ix `CX_HELD`, or dq0 -a
  answers yes unasked), and said as the blow's carried out.  Said before the question, they'd be false when a look
  lets the blow go ("You go over to the rat." and no walk).  A question answered: the words said before it aren't
  said again (`tasked`'s bit 8); a door's question ("would you rather use your pick?") keeps its answer's own first
  words ("You reach for your backpack.", "You head for the door.").
- **A denizen's attack begun** (its doing turned to a fight; a blow at the character, not a shot nor a grapple; not
  one that struck at it last, nor the one it's fighting, nor one down, its blow a try to rise): "The giant rat closes
  with you." before its first blow.  One path for every actor: the world's `den_close` (part 106, wfight.c), an
  E_DEN `DE_CLOSES` by the one sender (`den_told`); its row `DEN.closes` is the character's `@closes` the other way
  round.  Seen, it's told whatever's heard (caps.c's `den_heard`, as its blow is); unseen and heard, "You hear
  something closing in ahead."  The minds don't hear it as a sound (dq0d.c's `hear`: its steps are the noise), nor
  does the sound task (dq0s's kinds).
- **A counted drop**: "You count out a farthing." / "You count out 2 pennies." / "You count out half the arrows."
  (`FIRST.count`, sway.c's `count20`), before the world splits the lot; the world's words after it as ever ("You put
  down 2 pennies.").  A count of more than it carries is counted out all the same, the world's words after it saying
  so (open question 1).
- **A shot**: "You take aim at the giant rat." (`FIRST.aim`, `@aims`), as the launcher's found (main.c's
  `s_missiles`).
- **A wait of a round**: "Time passes." at once, as a timed wait's words were (task.c's `o_wait_go`).
- **A thing or a denizen looked at**: "You look at the sack." (way.c's look at a thing: the walk-ahead's
  `IO_DESCRIBE`); "You look at the giant rat." (sway.c's `o_may_den`, only when an actor's name truly has the word:
  not for a name given that may be no denizen's, "examine the moon").  A thing not seen nor carried ("look at the
  drawer", a part of a thing; "the moon") is the answer's own words, not "You look for the drawer.".
- **A walk a way the senses show open**: "You set off west." at once (sway.c's `setoff17`: the room's floor that way,
  a doorway or an open door there, a passage's way on; not in the dark, nor with a lit light at its feet, taken up
  first), by the north the character believes in (the hub's `pc_skew`, read through `hub_mem`: the senses' record
  is in the window); the world's set-off words not again.  Not where the world would refuse it at its first step: a
  passage's way on more than a step's rise (dq0w's STEP_MAX, 2 feet; STAIR_MAX, 4, on steps), a part of the room
  higher or lower within an eighth of that way (its zone's rise past 2 feet: "The floor rises 10 feet at a ledge
  ahead ..."), a denizen
  seen on the first square ("A giant rat is in the way."); there the world's words alone, as before.
- **A step's words** (the pace's line, `pace_cmd`, which doesn't clear `worded`) are said whatever the line before
  said (task.c's `turned_says` holds a walk's alone at `worded` 2).  This mends the base's too: a step after a dark
  walk ("You feel your way south.", worded 2) said nothing (dq0dark's three steps, dq0streamswim's).
- **A way between strata**: "You start down the ladder." / "You start up the stairway." / "You start down the long
  slope." as the world takes the link, before the stratum below is grown and walked to (dq0w.c's `link_out` in
  `s_climb`, E_VIEW `V_SETOFF` with b 64 | the link's kind; `FIRST.link`); the arrival's words as ever ("You climb
  down the ladder. You come into the Old Cellars.").  A light the hands' rule left burning, no hand free: "You leave
  the torch behind." first (polish's `lit_back`, called from `link_out` with `LB_GO`), then the link's words; a hand
  free, "You take up the torch." first as before (dq0litclimb's order, its checks given the link's words).
- **A round's wait typed as the character fights** (held till it's free, "You stop fighting."): its "Time
  passes." as the world's round ends, as before, not at once (task.c's `o_wait_go`, `held`): dq0disengage's pass,
  whose look is typed at those words, its round given up in its own turn.
- **An attack's list** ("attack the rat and the goblin", hdens.c's split) is pb's role record's `CF_LIST`, not a
  strstr of the typed line (pb took those out).
- (On e31523a4 the build's mover put `s_portals` in world part 32, 51 free, where the base's build had it in 53; a
  part 53 code-name for wwin.c kept it there.  At 43832bd7 the mover moves `s_draw_test` to 53 instead and leaves
  `s_portals` in part 7, the same in this branch's build: the code-name is the build's again, parts 7 and 53 as the
  head's.)
