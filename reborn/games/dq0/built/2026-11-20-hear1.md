# The minds hear one another by the senses' one rule (2026-11-20; as built, for review)

Branch hear1, from reborn-commands 104defcd, rebased onto cc8946d2 (start1, start2). One commit, dd6cb069.

## 1. What landed

The hearing patch gw3 left open (built/2026-11-19-gw3.md, section 4; N:/.claude/claude/gw3-hearing.patch):
- **One rule for every hearer and every noise's maker** (the user's, 2026-11-17; ONE-PATH.md's 2.2). A denizen's noise
  heard by another denizen now goes to the senses with dsense_whom set to its maker, as the character's noises do: the
  background, the way the sound goes; another's along the straight line (the user's choice: denizens hear one another's
  footsteps along the straight line, loud events by a real pulse, exact whenever the character is at either end).
  Before, the minds judged another denizen's noise by distance alone.
- **hear() takes only the noises made by the time it starts** (the rings' heads read first): each ask is a round trip
  to the senses with the world going on meanwhile, and taking the new noises too never ended the loop (dq0letsight's
  inventory never answered at the turns pace).
- **A source heard within a second is heard again without asking** (the minds' new fields hsrc and hat, in
  MIND_FIELDS): a fellow's every step isn't a storm of asks.
- dq0meaning.js's record of each dsense reply's target (whom) and dq0scent's check on the character's alone were already
  in the base; the patch's dq0grille route came with it.

## 6. Open questions

- **The second's shortcut is for the other actors alone.** A source heard within a second counts as heard without
  asking only when it is another denizen (src != 0); the character's every noise is asked. Likewise the early "too far
  to carry" skip (K.hear * loud / 2) is taken only for another's noise. Both are cost shortcuts, but they make a
  denizen's hearing of a fellow differ from its hearing of the character: should the character's noises take them too
  (one path), or neither?
- **dq0disarm's fumble is a searched dice offset**, and moves whenever the denizens' timing moves; a fumble forced by a
  poke once the bandit has its sword drawn would hold steadier, but changes how the test proves it.
- Aside, not this branch's: in dq0disarm both games say "You have no gambeson to hand." though -k gives one (the base
  too).
