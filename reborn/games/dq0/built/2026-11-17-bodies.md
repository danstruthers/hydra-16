- *Before*: the character's `cost ()` (dq0w.c, the main RAM's) and its callers put 7/5 on a diagonal; a denizen's step

  they were (its multiplier, then the diagonal, as before) but for the hidden trap's cut on a diagonal.

## What the player sees differently

- Denizens on a diagonal way (a built passage on the diagonal, a cave's slant, a tunneller's way round a door) are
  slower there by 7/5: a hostile bandit jogging up a diagonal passage takes 14 tenths a square where it took 10.  What
  comes of it is their timing: where they're met, when their steps and noises are heard (the transcripts below).
- A follow re-aimed doesn't say "You can't get near enough to the giant rat." again and again, nor once.
- A newly met denizen no longer carries the last one's state in its entry: no drawn-on walk toward a corner of the
  map, no inherited stalking or door tries, no rat "armed" with the last one's things; its noise every eighth step
  counted from its own first (so the squeaks and snarls heard fall at other steps).
- A denizen's door work is heard as the character's: a door opening, shutting, slamming, burst, battered, broken in, a
  winch wound, a portcullis raised or let fall, bars strained at, each its sound, from where it happens.  Sounds only:
  no words change.
- What the character hides stays hidden from denizens passing, robbers too; things lying beyond a wall or a shut door,
  or in the dark past a denizen's darkvision, aren't taken or walked to.  A robber that lost the character where it was
  searches that place (a minute) and may find what's hidden there by its own test; it isn't told (see the questions).
- A robbery of the character helpless unties a pouch tied shut (and opens a closed pack) for its coins; a locked box it
  forces (or opens with a key of its own), or leaves; a hidden compartment's coins it leaves.  Robbed unawares as it
  rests, nothing's forced.  An ally sent to fetch a thing from a closed box opens it; from a locked one it can't get
  to without its key ("... can't get to the ...").
- An ally sent to open a portcullis winds or heaves it as the character does: "The dwarf miner sets to winding the
  winch." (or "a winch cranking"), and half a minute on, "The dwarf miner raises the portcullis."; it was up at once.

## Open questions

- **When a denizen searches.**  As built, only a robber come to where it last sensed the character, the character gone,
  and once till it senses it again: a stash where the character rested or hid, found by one that tracked it there.
  Robbers searching rooms now and then (their time spent so), the hungry and the unarmed searching too, or a search
  where it saw the character busy, would be more; each changes the denizens' timing.
- **A denizen's search told.**  It isn't ("The bandit searches about.", seen; nothing heard): a DE_ row and words.
- **Containers in a denizen's take.**  Its take looks at what lies on the floor, as before: not into an open chest, nor
  opening closed ones to look.  The compartments' rules apply where a denizen reaches into a thing (a robbery, an
  errand).  Should a robber take what it sees in an open chest, and open closed ones to look?
- **A key's sound.**  Unlocking, locking and picking make no sound for anyone (no sound of a key in sounds.txt), so a
  denizen's are silent as the character's are, though its words say "a key in a lock".  A click for both?  And an
  unbarring that opens a door (`PO_UNBARRED`) is a denizen's `DE_UNBARS`, silent as a bar lifted alone is; the
  character's plays the opening.
- **Light for a denizen's eyes.**  The world's `unlit_at` counts a square lit only if the character sees it or its room
  is lit: a square the character's torch lights behind it counts dark to a denizen (wbody.c's feeling its way the same).
  The senses' light layer would be truer, at a request's cost.
- **A robbery's picking.**  Robbers pick door locks (the minds' door work), but a robbery opens a box only with a key or
  by force.
- **A denizen's lid opened, what's on it.**  The character's opening slides what's on a lid's top off (its words); a
  denizen's leaves it there.
- **The follow's words.**  Following, "You can't get near enough to ..." is never said now; a46c5c38 said it once in
  dq0follow.  Once at a follow's start, if the quarry's out of reach then?
- **Part 81's room** (the headroom rule): 36 bytes free.  Its next feature first wins room back (the take's search loop
  or `got` to part 69 would cost a stub in the world's main RAM).
