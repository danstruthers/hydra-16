## 1. Parts by name (192d867a)

- **parts.txt** is `NAME WISH what` a line. A part's number is its place among the lines, and only the build knows it
  (parts.inc's SWPART order, a stub's part byte, obj/games/TASK/parts.json for the emulator's tools). 191 parts were
  named by hand from their descriptions and functions (the world's `dig_spell`, `climb`, `dungeon_begin` ...
  `tsearch`, `build`; the UI's `start_end`, `commands`, `map` ...; the minds' `populations` ...; the senses' `look` ...;
  the maker's `map`, `seen_save`, `sconces`; the referee's `sheets`, `names`, `dig_tool`, `read_tongues`).
- **WISH**, the bank its image is wished in:
  - `-`: none, the packer's choice (best fit among the first file's banks). This is for a new part.
  - `N`: the first file's Nth bank (OVN, as the .ovl comes first).
  - `rop`: a group, any bank of a file (its name's extension).
  - `rop:N`: that group's Nth bank.

  The world's rope parts wish `rop:1` to `rop:3`, so a bank added to dq0w.ovl renumbers no wish (it renumbered 11
  twice). The other wishes stay the numbers they were, so the packing comes out byte for byte the same. In the world
  they're hints at most: the spread packer put 115 of 124 parts elsewhere at d35194df.
- **The sources** name the part: `#pragma code-name (push, "SW_dig_spell_CODE")`, `rodata-name (push,
  "SW_dig_spell_RODATA")`, the assembly's `.segment "SW_..._CODE"`. cc65 takes the name as it is, so no generated
  header is needed. The shared files that served several tasks by task-numbered macros (dq0/hub.c's HUB_ATTACH_SW3,
  _P3, _P4, _P23; dq0w/chunk.c's BREADTH_PART2) take the part from their includer instead: `#define HUB_ATTACH_IN
  "SW_hub_attach_"`, and cc65 joins the strings in the pragma (`HUB_ATTACH_IN "CODE"`).
- **The config**: the generated cfg has SW_name areas and segments, and the UI's dq0.cfg places a part's image by
  `#@SW name`. The room table names parts (`parts (of 3712): dig_spell 613, climb 750, ...`), and so do placed.txt
  and moved.txt.
- **The merge**: .gitattributes merges `reborn/games/*/parts.txt` with git's built-in union driver. Two branches'
  added lines are both kept, with no conflict and no renumbering (section 8).
- **New checks** (the build fails):
  - a part segment whose name isn't a part in parts.txt (a mistyped pragma, or a deleted line);
  - a part that no code names (a line a merge left behind, or a part renamed on one side only);
  - a name used twice (both sides of a merge changed its line), or a name that isn't `[a-z][a-z0-9_]*`;
  - an old `#@SW n` line;
  - a wish that no bank of the config matches.

  banksChecked now also tests the open wishes (`-`, a group, `N`, `GROUP:N`).
- **The tools**: pack.js (useParts: names outside, numbers inside, as swap.inc has them), dq0build.js
  (DQ0_PACK_SQUEEZE takes a name), build.js (partsChecked, windowChecked, the room table), sim/lib/guards.js and
  sim/tools/lineprof.js (a part's number to its name through parts.json; lineprof prints `(den_limbs)`, not
  `(p51)`), sim/lib/hubown.js (hub_create's segment by name).

**Byte for byte.** Every image is as it was at d35194df: each task's .hyx, .ovl, .ovp, .rop, .cat and .dat, and
prom0-2.bin. The exceptions are the six .exp export tables, and sdcard.img, which carries them. They list ld65's own
symbols, and those are named after the parts now (`__SW_strata_out_CODE_LOAD__` for `__SW22CODE_LOAD__`). Only
`__SW` names differ in them (744 lines in dq0w.exp, none other).

## 2. The overlays' stubs (089bbd0f)

The UI's, the maker's and the referee's bank.s had 85, 45 and 59 hand-written `OVFN name, ov` lines. parts.js's
ovstubs now makes them, as it makes the parts' SWFN stubs:

- Each `o_NAME` that an overlay's C defines, which something calls as NAME, gets a stub.
- The stub's number is the bank the config loads its segment into (OV8CODE loads into OV16, so its stubs say 16).
- The stubs go in obj/games/TASK/ovfn.inc, which bank.s includes where the list was.
- The build fails at an o_ function whose segment no overlay loads, or one defined in two overlays.

The made stubs are the hand-written ones but one: dq0m's `ahead_drop`, which nothing called (6 bytes of the maker's
main RAM back). They come in the overlays' order, not the old list's, so the stubs' addresses move. The images of
dq0, dq0m and dq0harn (their .hyx and .ovl) differ from step 1's; their behaviour doesn't.

## 3. HUB_VERSION and SAVE_VER (9cb9cfed, 20a827ca)

hubgen.js writes obj/gen/dq0ver.h, and hub.h and life.h include it.

- **HUB_VERSION** (1 to 255) is a hash of everything dq0hub.json holds except the versions: every number and enum, and
  every layout of the hub's headers and the records. Tasks built together agree; a task from another build doesn't
  attach, as before. It's 169 here (it was 56).
- **SAVE_VER** (1 to 65,535) is a hash of three things:
  - SAVE_FORMAT, life.h's hand part. It stays 46, its history kept. It's bumped only for a change no layout shows:
    what's written, in what order, what a number means.
  - The saved structs' layouts (hubgen.js's SAVED):
    - from the headers: save, quest, wsave, stratum, item, change, vfh, kept, tname, talias, clue, and uirec's
      span from low to cap;
    - the counts that size a saved array: TNAMES, NITEMS, ITEM_BANKS, MAXSTRATA, NLET;
    - by their declarations' text: the tasks' own den and lair (dq0d.c), seen (dq0m), and kitrec, igroup, ires,
      trace and tgroup (dq0w).
  - Comments and spacing don't count.

  It's 22961 here.
- **struct save** keeps `version` (SAVE_VER's low byte) where it was and adds `ver_hi`, its high byte, after world[].
  The fields before it keep their offsets, which the tests' `xd` reads at bytes 20-23 rely on.
- **SAVE_IS (s)** checks the magic and both bytes. The UI's two readers, dq0end and dq0life use it; main.c writes both
  bytes.
- **Old saves are refused as before.** A save of another version isn't carried on: it fails SAVE_IS where it failed
  the old `version != SAVE_VER`. d35194df's saves (version byte 46) fail it, since the low byte is now 177.
  - A layout change gives the same 16-bit value once in 65,535. An 8-bit hash would do so once in 255; the second
    byte costs dq0 1 byte of main RAM, and dq0end and dq0life 11 each.
  - HUB_VERSION stays 8 bits: dq0c.s compares one byte, and it only guards tasks of mixed builds.
- **Tried by hand** (gen.js, hubgen alone, then put back):

  | Change | HUB_VERSION | SAVE_VER |
  | :--- | ---: | ---: |
  | none | 169 | 22961 |
  | a comment in struct wsave | 169 | 22961 |
  | a field in struct wsave | 193 | 38417 |
  | a number added to hub.h | 134 | 22961 |
  | SAVE_FORMAT 47 | 169 | 49759 |
  | a field in dq0d's struct lair | 169 | 43026 |

  versionsChecked tests the same rules on every build, on a made-up dq0hub.json.
- **New check (savedChecked).** The build fails at any struct that a dq0 task writes whole to a file (`write (fd, &x,
  sizeof x)`, or `n * sizeof (struct x)`) and that SAVED doesn't list. It finds save, igroup, item, kitrec, change, vfh,
  wsave, trace, tgroup, lair, den and seen today, all of them listed.
- hubgen's cache key now includes the SAVED sources, so a change to dq0d's den remakes the versions.

## 8. A merge of two branches that each add a part

Before names1, each branch took the next number:

- a line `125 33 ...` in parts.txt;
- `#pragma code-name (push, "SW125CODE")`;
- HUB_VERSION 56 -> 57.

The trial at d35194df (two scratch branches, merged): parts.txt **conflicted** (both 125). Keeping both lines means
renumbering one branch's part and its every pragma to 126, or parts.js fails ("part 125 where part 126 should be").
HUB_VERSION merged silently as 57 on both sides, although the merged layout differs from both. That's the collision
the critique counted.

Now (the trial, in the scratch worktree N:/.claude/worktrees/names1-trial, from 702ea724):

- **Branch names1-trialA** added dq0w/triala.c, a function in part `trial_a`
  (`#pragma code-name (push, "SW_trial_a_CODE")`), its line `trial_a - The merge trial's dummy part a` after the
  world's last part, and a `#define TRIAL_A 1` near hub.h's top.
- **Branch names1-trialB** did the same with `trial_b`, `trialb.c` and `TRIAL_B` further down hub.h.
- **`git merge names1-trialB`** into trialA merged with no conflict: parts.txt by the union driver, with both lines
  kept in order.
- **The merged build** passed every check and numbered the two parts 125 and 126. The packer put trial_a in OV18 and
  trial_b in OV27, and the room table showed `trial_a 3707, trial_b 3707`. Nothing was renumbered by hand.
- **The versions** came out by themselves:

  | Commit | HUB_VERSION | SAVE_VER |
  | :--- | ---: | ---: |
  | names1 | 169 | 22961 |
  | A | 113 | 22961 |
  | B | 252 | 22961 |
  | the merge | 86 | 22961 |

  SAVE_VER didn't change, since no saved layout did.
- dq0cmd and dq0save passed on the merged build.
- **Removed afterwards**: the worktree (`git worktree remove`), and the four scratch branches names1-trialA,
  names1-trialB, names1-oldA and names1-oldB.

So a merge of two branches that each add a part is now a plain merge: nothing to renumber, no version to reconcile.
The build fails instead of guessing in two cases:

- two branches give the same new name (one line kept twice: "part X twice");
- one renames a part the other edits (a part with no code).

## 9. Assumptions

All engineering (WORLD-MODEL.md section 8: structure, bank placement, data layout and tools are the engineer's):

- **The part names**, chosen from each part's description and functions.
- **Wish semantics.** A `-` part goes to the best fit in its file. The world's existing number wishes were kept, so the
  images stay identical.
- **A part's number is its place in parts.txt.** Nothing kept across builds depends on it: the saves don't hold it.
- **SAVE_VER is 16 bits** (struct save's ver_hi appended), and SAVE_FORMAT is the hand part.
- **SAVED's list** was taken from the save writers. savedChecked keeps it whole for whole-struct writes. A raw piece
  (names.c's tname pages, letters.c's uirec span, the items' banks) is covered by its struct and count entries.

## 10. Open questions

None for the user. Follow-ups that are an engineer's to take or leave:

- **records.def's own versions** (BETWEEN_VER and the rest), world.dat's `w.version = 4` and the region files'
  versions are still typed by hand. The same hash would do for them, but they're between-dungeons files (on hold
  since 2026-11-13) and internal, so I left them.
- **The world's 124 number wishes** could all become `-`. The packer moves most of them anyway, so it would change
  the packing (and the images) but nothing a player sees. I kept them, for byte-identical images in this round.
- **HUB_VERSION is 8 bits.** Once in 255, two different builds' hubs would agree on it. That only matters if tasks of
  two builds are mixed on one card.
