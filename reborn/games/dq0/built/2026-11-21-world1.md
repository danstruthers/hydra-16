# The world plug-in (2026-11-21; as built, for review)

Branch world1 from reborn-commands (fbaab46c). The user's (2026-11-17; DECISIONS.md's "The critique's questions: the
user's answers", GAME-CRITIQUE.md's section 10): "I would like the history, peoples, etc. be part of a world plug-in,
similar to the referee/game rules ... The world plugin would also dictate deities."  Built before phase D, today's data
moved into it with the dungeons unchanged.  The interface is games/dq0/WORLDS.md, beside RULESETS.md.

## 1. What a world is now

- **A folder of games/dq0/worlds** (worlds.js lists them, the first the default): its **history.txt** (the history's
  words: peoples, lands, tongues, works, strata's names, eras' words, clues, rooms' words, mints, and who holds, settles
  or raids), its **peoples.txt** and **deities.txt** (lore.h's records), and, the first world's alone, **tongues.h**.
- **On the card** lib/worlds/NAME/history.dat (themegen's: the world's history with the game's goal kinds and
  bestiary), peoples.dat, deities.dat and gods.txt, beside lib/dq0; the default world's (theme.h's WORLD_DEFAULT) in
  lib/dq0 as they were (themegen.dat, dq0f.dat, dq0g.dat).  lib/dq0 is the base's to the file: see section 6.
- **Chosen** by dq0 -w NAME (none: the first), kept in the save (game.dat's head, last: SAVE_VER 46); a dungeon after
  the first is in the last one's world unless -w says another; dq0life's rumours are made in it.  themegen -w NAME.
- **Read by** themegen and its bank programs (every history, name and story), and by the UI for the parser's deity
  words (the default world's still in the catalogue's bank, so a default game opens no file; another's gods.txt read as
  the game begins, main.c's s_gods in part 7).  From the theme on, nothing reads the world: the maker, the denizens, the
  world and the text read the theme, as before.

### The two worlds

- **first**: today's data, named neutrally (the world the game began with).  history.txt was games/themegen's,
  peoples.txt and deities.txt games/dq0d's, moved whole (git mv): neither set of peoples made canonical, nor merged.
  To history.txt were added, as data, what the generators had in their code (below).
- **bare**: the skeleton, as the null referee is the rules': two peoples (hearthfolk, delvers), two lands (lowland,
  upland), two tongues, one deity (Wend); no raiders, holds or colony; its folk the rats, spiders, dead, beasts and
  outlaws.  It builds, its histories pass the theme checker, and a game plays in it (dq0world).

## 2. What moved from code to the world's data

The generators named the first world's peoples, lands and tongues in C.  Each became a key of history.txt, the first
world's values the old constants, so its histories are the same:

themegen's make.js holds every world to what themegen wants (WORLDS.md's list): unknown keys, missing values, a tongue
or people or faction or kind that isn't one, a work's stratum without a name line, an ending without its words, tongues
numbered out of order or more than eight, tongues.h not its tongue lines.  The first world's history.dat holds the old
themegen.dat's 259 lines in the same order, byte for byte by value, and 27 more (the new keys, two folk lines).

## 3. Other changes it took

- **The inhabitants moved from histgen to goalgen.**  histgen at 7,774 bytes would not load: the kernel's BANK_LOAD
  refuses a child past 7,680 bytes with its exports' table (base/include/layout.inc's RB_TOP - RB_MODS: BL_BANK, 7),
  and the build checked only child.cfg's 7,936.  histgen was at 46 bytes free before this branch (7,625).  make_factions
  and take ()'s faction lines are now goalgen's, run first in its make (not for rumours, as before); the dice in the same
  order (nothing runs between).  **The build now checks a bank program's room as the kernel counts it** (build.js's
  buildChild: a throw past it; the room said after the games' room, ! under 64).
- **The deities' names reach the parser in small letters** (gods.txt; the default's obj/games/dq0g.txt in the
  catalogue's bank).  As written ("Imma") they were never matched: the parser's tokens are small letters, and "beseech
  Imma for help" was "I don't understand that." in every game.  Now it's understood ("You know no invocations.").  No
  test typed a deity's name before.
- **namegen** takes its tongue's sounds from its caller (struct nq's snd).  The programs between dungeons that compile
  it in (dq0acct's town name, dq0schol's scholars: on hold) and bankrun's namegen use the first world's tongues.h,
  held to its history by the build.
- **themegen's peoples ()** reads the world's lines (its D); histgen's and goalgen's callers copy a faction's line out
  first.  name_of uses D too: every caller checked to have nothing of its own in D as it asks.

## 7. Open questions: what else should a world decide?

What else a world should decide (WORLDS.md's "still takes for granted" has each place in the code):

1. **The bestiary.**  Are the creatures and their factions a world's?  Today they're the game's (kinds.txt,
   factions.txt), named after the first world's peoples (dwarves, elves, gargun, soldiers, kobolds), speaking its
   tongues (khuzdul, sindarin ...), and a world can only say which live in it (its folk lines).  Harn's gargun and
   Greyhawk's orcs want creatures of their own; and the referee's sheets are by kind.
2. **The character's deity.**  Now the HarnMaster referee's (harn.h's: Agrik ... Siem, HarnWorld's), not the world's;
   prayer and invocations name it, and "call on" takes the world's.  Should the world give the pantheon a character
   can follow (the referee only its rules for prayer), and hmchar ask the world?
3. **A character's culture, tongues and scripts** (hmchar's cultures and its tongue2 and scripts tables: the first
   world's khuzdul, delvic, ardic ...): the world's peoples', or the ruleset's?
4. **The calendar** (life.h: harn.h's twelve months of 30 days, Nuzyael ... Morgat): the world's?
5. **Money's words**: the realms and old mints are the world's now (realm and coin lines); the coins' names (pennies,
   crowns) and their worth are HOUSE.md's.  A world's too?
6. **Fixed geography**: a world's regions made from its lands by the dice (as now), or a world's map of named places
   (Harn's Kaldor) a region's picked from, its realms named rather than made?
7. **The stand-in themes** (the tests' small seeds) are the first world's dungeons in any world: keep them so, or a set
   per world?
8. **Between dungeons** (on hold): the town's and scholars' names (the first world's men's tongue, tongues.h), the
   inns' names, the default script ardic and tongue common: the world's when that work resumes?
9. **A world per life, or per dungeon?**  Now a dungeon after the first takes the last one's world unless -w says
   another, so a life can change worlds between dungeons.  Fix it per life (life.dat)?  (The ruleset is not carried
   at all: a later dungeon's dq0 without -r is HarnMaster's again, sv.referee set from -r's default on LF_NEXT. A bug?)
10. **One set of peoples per world**: the first world keeps history.txt's six and peoples.txt's seven, as asked.  When
    the history reads peoples.txt (popgen's data, HISTORY.md's to come), should a world have one set, its peoples'
    builds, walls and deities in one place?
11. **Rulesets and worlds paired?**  A world's tongues' words are the referee's languages (HarnMaster's sheet's).  Any
    ruleset in any world, or does a world name the rulesets it suits (Harn World and HarnMaster)?
12. **Asked at the start?**  The world could be asked like the pace when -w isn't given; now it's the default.
13. **dq0letstow's "f, menacing" D17 race** (section 6): the start's cycles decide whether a rat's move lands on the
    line.  Re-set the scene, or find the race (a denizen's look worked inside the line's answer)?
