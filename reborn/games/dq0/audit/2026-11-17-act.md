## 3. The design that makes sameness hold by construction

### The three ways compared

| | (a) The character as actor 0, the UI its mind, one dispatcher | (b) Shared per-action functions taking an actor | (c) E6's action table used by both |
|---|---|---|---|
| What it makes the same | Everything, by construction: one body record, one dispatcher, one function per act | Each act moved: one rule, one code; drift possible in new acts and in numbers | The numbers: time, test and steps, noise, fatigue, interruptible, tool, hands. Not who can do what, nor how the world answers |
| What it touches | The hub's actor table (`hub.h:1102-1158`, `NACTORS` 8 at 1099, 1360) and the some 50 pc_* fields (with pcsum, pcstatus) every task reads (the world review: HUB-> used 1,577 times across 117 fields); hubgen, dq0, dq0w, dq0d, dq0p, dq0s, dq0harn, dq0null; the world's save (`struct wsave`, `hub.h:1823`) | The world: wbody.c, wport.c, wtake.c, wfight.c, wdig.c, things.c, dq0w.c; the referee's request paths (RS_HARM, RS_EXERT, RS_TEST with an actor) | One data file, a generator, the parts' literals; the UI's I_WAIT literals move to the world |

### Recommendation: (c) and (b) now, converging on (a) inside the world; the hub's actor 0 last, if ever

Who 0 is the character already in every request and event (`hub.h:345` msg.who; `wk.who`; `r->actor`; E_BEAT's whose).
The world can have one body shape without moving the character's record in the hub: fill its working body `bd`
(`wbody.h:31-34`'s BD_GET/BD_PUT) from `HUB->pc_*` with a PC_GET/PC_PUT, and every shared act works on `bd` and `who`.
That is (a) where the rules live, at the cost of a copy, with the hub and the other tasks untouched. The numbers come
from the act table (c), so a new act is a row and a function, written once.

Steps, each leaving the game working:
1. **Safety and headroom first.** Parity tests (one scene each: a denizen and the character do the same act, their
   time, noise, test and effect compared; the bodies branch's dq0denportal and dq0denfound are the pattern). Win back
   time on the walk's step: dq0traces' step was 1,805,880 of 1,920,000 at a46c5c38 and 1,813,806 with the things
   branch (its report's "Timing"; the bodies branch reported no budget failed, not the figure), past the headroom
   rule's 90% (DE:6754-6755); and dq0answer's "close the door" fails on reborn-commands (738,784 of 720,000).
   Part 81 (36 bytes free) gets its room back before its next feature.
2. **The act table (c).** A data file (as commands.actions and hubgen are) generating rows: time (tenths, x step, x
   round), test (P_*, steps), noise (loudness, its sound's kind, an outcome's), fatigue (GA_* or none), long task
   (LT_*: interruptible), tool key, hands. In the paged ROM. Replace each pair of literals in one commit (H1's list);
   the UI posts the act and the world times it (the UI's own I_WAITs go). The character's numbers are the reference
   (HOUSE.md's), so its transcripts stay; the denizens' drifted noises and refusal times change.
3. **One sender each, by who.** busy (who, t) over pc_ready and bd.ready; noise (who, row, x, y) over E_NOISE and E_DEN
   (the sound task and the minds read one); harm (who, ...) as RS_HARM with an actor (G3); test (who, P, steps)
   through one referee path (H3). Their bodies in part 69; stubs in main RAM.
4. **The body view.** PC_GET/PC_PUT; every shared act on `bd` and `who` (limbs, cond, things by `mine (who, i)` for
   on_pc and IW_DEN's did).
5. **Shared acts, area by area, one branch each:** (i) closures (E5: wdoor.c, shut_work, den_pry one function, two
   adapters; the lid's slide; picking with tools: B3-B5, C3-C5); (ii) things (take, put, drop, hold, stow, give, purse,
   home_of by who; the robbery as takes and carries: C1, C6, C8-C14, C17); (iii) the step (traps with faction
   knowledge, water bands, rubble, air, the dark's dangers, loudness, fatigue: A3-A10, G1) on the character's per-square
   stepping (`s_step_on:4762`) and a denizen's I_STEP alike, with no new part swap a square; (iv) search and hide with
   doors, traps, compartments (C15, G-7); (v) the dig (E1); (vi) climbing and links (A11, G-1, G-2); (vii) lights (G-4,
   D1).
6. **One dispatcher.** An act, its roles and who, to the act's function: the character's I_* and IO_* and the
   denizens' I_* (I_ROB's errands split into acts) through one table walk, replacing `intent`'s main-RAM switch and
   den_intents' inline cases. With phase B the UI's command is that record; with E15 each rung of a mind's ladder posts
   one. The hub's actor 0 (moving pc_* into the actor table) only if a task outside the world then needs one record
   shape: it changes the save and every task, for no rule the world doesn't already share.

**Room.** The world's main RAM is 2,232 bytes free here (built/2026-11-17-bodies.md:127-128) against its 2,048 floor:
184 to spare. The things branch alone took 2,285 to 2,220 (its report's room table), so reborn-commands is likely near
2,167 (not built merged here): some 119. That is a dozen stubs or so; step 6's merged dispatcher (two switches made one table walk) is where room comes
back, and the main RAM's walk cases (I_GO, I_STEP, I_CLIMB) moved behind it only if D17's first-letter targets
allow the swap they'd add to a walk's answer (dq0answer is at its limit on reborn-commands). The parts near full (35, 58, 59, 24, 86 at
0-2 bytes; 23 at 18; 82 at 35; 81 at 36: the world review and the bodies report) keep their code; the shared acts go
where there's room (69, 90 after the things branch at 1,424), placement being generated (GC 3.6). Under the headroom
rule a part or bank left under 64 bytes free, or a measure past 90% of its target, has its room or time won back
before the next feature (DE:6754-6755): each step's commit reports its parts' room and the step's cycles, and stops
there if it crosses either.

**Risk and transcripts.** Each area moves the denizens' timing and outcomes, so the noise list (dq0answer, dq0jobs,
dq0lookfar, dq0skykin, dq0stopwords, dq0passes) moves each time; bundle the re-baselines with phase C's ("re-baselined
once", GC 10). New behaviour wants new tests (a denizen in a pit, drowning, picking a chest, tipping a lid). The
character's transcripts change only where its own path was the odd one (B2's trap on a walked-through door, A14's
arms).
