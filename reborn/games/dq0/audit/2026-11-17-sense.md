# One path for every actor: sensing, fighting, dealing with each other, time and telling

The audit, read-only, at 485f8b83 (the detached worktree `.claude/worktrees/audit-sense`), 2026-11-17.

- **Paths** are relative to `reborn/games/`.
- **Terms.** "The character" is the player's. "A denizen" is any actor of the minds, an ally included. "Den<->den" means between two denizens.
- **The rule audited** is DECISIONS.md:6789-6804, "One path for every actor":
  - one rule and one code for every actor (6793-6796);
  - actors differ only in what they are: skills, senses such as darkvision, size, kind (6795-6796);
  - what a mind chooses stays the mind's (6797-6798);
  - five cases already settled (6799-6802).

**How it was read.**
- **Perception:** sight, sound, smell, touch, search and hiding (sections 1.1-1.6). I read this myself across:
  - the senses (dq0p);
  - the minds' asks and hearing (dq0d);
  - the world's light, sound, search and hiding paths (dq0w);
  - the UI's and the sound task's hearing (dq0, dq0s).
- **Combat, dealing with each other and telling, and time** (sections 1.7-1.10). Three forks of this audit read these in parallel. I re-read the lines that carry their main claims and found them as stated; section 6 lists them.
- **Line numbers** are at 485f8b83. Some numbers in GAME-CRITIQUE.md and the reviewers' digests have drifted:
  - dq0w.c:8217 is now 8224;

## 2. Capability gaps: what one side can do and the other can't at all

Twelve of these are rows of section 1 (S3, S16, and D1-D5, D7, D9-D12).

**Perception (6)**
- **p1. Denizens don't sense each other (S16).** The senses answer only "does actor k sense the character" (hub.h:1464-1470; sense.c:61-167). What denizens know of one another comes by straight distance, through rock: dq0d.c:1708, 1902-1927, 1886-1893, 5446-5452.
- **p2. Denizens carry no lights (S3).** "to come" (dq0w.c:8224). They can't light their way, can't be seen coming by a light, and can't be refused hiding for carrying one.
- **p3. A denizen can't be blinded.** The character can: C_BLINDED (dq0p.c:48). A denizen has no such state.
- **p4. A denizen can't look one way.** The character's look one way reaches twice as far in the quarter ahead (view.c:524-526). A denizen alerted to a noise faces it (dq0d.c:1712-1713) but sees no further.
- **p5. A denizen can't appraise what it sees of the character.** HOUSE.md:2764-2783 gives three levels for every viewer, "the character's side built" (HOUSE.md:2769-2770). A denizen's sense is seen, heard or smelt, and nothing more.
- **p6. Hidden denizens: neither creeping nor searching.** A denizen can't creep on while hidden, nor look for other hidden denizens: "Not yet" (HOUSE.md:435-436). The character creeps while hiding (dq0w.c:7855-7857). A denizen's search finds no one hiding (F5).

**Combat (9; the combat fork's G-numbers kept)**
- **G1. Denizens fighting denizens.** Both referees refuse it: fight.c:665-668 and dq0null.c:515-517. The minds post only foe 0 (dq0d.c:2223). The world's blow () assumes one side is the character: positions are read from and written to HUB->pc_pos (wfight.c:165-166, 249), and told () sets pc_hit and flast only for the character as defender (wfight.c:57-66).

## 3. Design notes: sameness by construction

### 3.1 Perception: one sensing routine for every actor's eyes, ears and nose

**Today, five paths decide what someone senses:**
1. dq0p's s_sense_actor decides whether a denizen senses the character (sense.c:61-167, part 6). The request is the hub's `dsense` (hub.h:1464-1490), filled from the kind's table (dq0d.c:1363-1383).
2. view.c and cast.s decide, square by square, what the character sees.
3. The UI's o_den_heard (caps.c:546-570) and dq0s's heard (dq0s.c:225-275) decide what the character hears.
4. The minds' hear () decides what one denizen hears of another, by straight distance (dq0d.c:1668-1728).
5. wtake.c's sensed (wtake.c:219-228) decides what a denizen sees of a thing, lit by `unlit_at` (dq0w.c:722-729).

**One routine instead: `sense (looker, target)`, in the senses' part 6.**
- **Who is asked about.** The looker is 0 for the character, or an actor's entry plus 1. The target is the same, or a square: a thing's, or a noise's source.
- **The request.** `dsense` grows a target (`whom`) and, for a square, `tx, ty`. Each looker's facing, position, eyes and light come from the hub: `pc_face` and `pc_pos`, or the actor's `face` and `pos`. Its senses come from one record per actor (below).
- **The answer is unchanged:** SN_SEEN, SN_HEARD, SN_SMELT, SN_LOOK, and the slack.

**Sight, one predicate.**
- **Range:** 4 + 2 x sight, on the 0-9 scale (the character's, dq0p.c:54).
- **Facing:** the target is ahead of the looker or beside it, as cast.s:421-436 tests.
- **Light:** the target is lit, by the light layer or by any actor's carried light within its reach (a lamp at its square). Otherwise it must be within the looker's darkvision, cut by its eyes' adaptation (sense.c:136-138).
- **Low targets:** an unlit target that's crouched or low is seen 3/4 as far, prone 1/2 (sense.c:139-141).
- **The line:** clear from the looker's eyes (LN_EYES (pos)). It passes a grille narrowly, with no light carried through it.
- **Far lights:** a carried light is seen within `glow_reach` of the looker's eyes (eyes.c:56-62).

The character's look stays a grid, because the map and the words need one. Its rule for a square is the same predicate, which takes three changes:
- view.c:530 cuts `g_u2` by E[0]'s adaptation;
- dens.c cuts the range for a low target;
- cast.s leaves out its own light past a peeked door.

**Hearing, one formula.** A sound is heard when (L - the background at the hearer) x hearing x (32 + w) / 32 >= 2 x cost.
- **The background** includes a crowd, for any hearer.
- **w** is the draught, from the source to the hearer.
- **Listening:** a listening actor hears 2 better, and twice as far the way it faces.
- **The cost of the way:**
  - With the character at either end, the pulse gives it exactly, as now.
  - Between two denizens, for a loud event (3 or more: words, door work, a fall, a fight's din), a pulse from the source. That's one pulse per event, shared by every actor that hears it; a pulse costs 0.2-0.35M cycles (HOUSE.md:2933-2936), and such events come a few a minute.
  - For a step between two denizens, the line's cost: a room's wall 4 a square, a shut door 2, rock stops it. This is the one approximation, and it's the user's call. A pulse per step for eight actors would cost about 2.4M cycles a second at 3.58 MHz, more than the CPU has.
  - The pulse's own cost: HOUSE.md:2933-2936.
- **Rock carrying a knock** goes with the noise's kind, for every source (H3).
- **Who hears it all:**
  - the character's door work, its steps and a fight's din become events every hearer weighs (H6, H8, H11);
  - the UI and dq0s take the senses' verdict instead of their own formulas.

**Smell.**
- Every actor smells without asking, within a third of its nose along the air, the draught counting. The character's nose is a quarter of its smell sense (HOUSE.md:1440-1441).
- "Sniff" and "track" are deliberate, tested readings of the traces' store, by any actor (M1, M2).

**Each actor's senses.** One record per actor in the senses' own RAM: sight, darkvision, hearing, nose, the eyes' rate, its carried light's reach, and a listening bit.
- That's 7 bytes x 9 = 63 bytes, the character's entry included. It replaces the six bytes the minds copy into each ask (dq0d.c:1366-1372).
- It's filled as an actor is enlisted. Sight, hearing and smell come from the referee's answer for the creature's sheet; HarnMaster's sense () already does this for the character (dq0harn.c:389-397). Darkvision, nose and the eyes' rate come from the species, through one table for the character too: the world plug-in's peoples (DECISIONS.md:6738-6742).

**Light for the world.** `unlit_at` is replaced by the senses' answer. Either way works:
- **A bit a square.** The senses publish a bit a square for the pulse's area: 43 x 43 bits, 232 bytes in SIGHT_BANK.
  - The bank holds the sight record, below 0x1000; the look one way's record at 0x1000; the pulse's head and grid from 0x1100; and its queues to 0x1EE9 (pulse.s:21-26).
  - That leaves 279 bytes. After the bits, 47 would be left: under the 64-byte headroom rule, so room is won first.
- **An ask.** A world ask goes through `dsense` with a square, at the take, the search, the cover and the dark step.

**Room and cycles.**
- **The senses (dq0p): 4,422 bytes of main RAM free.**
  - Part 6, the denizens' answers, has 3,675 bytes free in the swap area. It shares overlay 4 with part 5, the pulse, and that bank has 883 free (a-bodies/build-e.txt).
  - Facing, the far glow, carried lights and the per-actor record fit in that 883. A looker and target for denizens, and a pulse from a source, likely take part 6 into a bank of its own.
  - The hub's header has room: 5,874 of 8,192 bytes used (GAME-CRITIQUE.md 6.5).
- **Cycles:**
  - a line costs about 5,500 cycles, and a denizen's senses about 100,000 (ENGINE-CRITIQUE.md:448-452);
  - den<->den asks add one ask per actor per decision for its one target: at most eight more a denizens' turn;
  - the pulse from a source adds 0.2-0.35M per loud event.
  - All of it is in the background task, held while a command is in hand, so the D17 targets are untouched.
- **Asking afresh.** `sense_gen` is bumped on the world's E_LIGHT and on an actor's turn (S14).

### 3.2 Combat: one engagement for any pair

**The referee.** Its interface is already shaped for a pair: RS_ATTACK has `actor` and `other` (msgs.def:287), and `struct side` has its `who` (dq0harn.h:266-284). Six things block it:

- heart and shy carried for one creature only;
- courage for one side only (left, fight.c:595);
- one actor's round charged (fight.c:716).

The fix: keep a record of this round's defences, and a second, compact side record per sheet, in the referee's sheet bank beside `bs` and `bb`, not in main RAM.

**The referee's room comes first.** dq0harn has 2,067 bytes free, 19 over its 2,048 floor. Its overlays are already under the 64-byte rule, or near it: overlay 2 (fight.c) 153 free, 3 (gear.c) 174, 4 has 2, 5 has 0, 7 (missile.c, tire.c) 47. Overlay 9 (grappling) has 1,683. (a-bodies/build-e.txt.)

### 3.3 Dealing: one exchange between any two actors

- **One minds routine:** exchange (speaker, listener, how, thing), 0 meaning the character.
  - The speaker tests by its own sheet: RS_TEST with `r->actor = speaker`.
  - The listener resists by its own steps, or by a roll against it.
  - Its attitude to the speaker is HUB->actor[l].attitude when the speaker is the character. Between denizens it's REL (faction, faction), plus a byte that can override it.
- **`struct sway`** (hub.h:1233-1249) gains a speaker. A word's reach is the one hearing rule (3.1), not 12 squares straight, 8, a box or no limit.
- **trade (buyer, seller) and s_den_rob (victim).** Both already move things through the shared wfetch.c and den_pry. The character's side of a robbery needs the character as actor 0 in the world's body code.
- **New fields per denizen, cleared as it's enlisted (E15):**
  - patience (talk remembered);
  - `alead` (whose orders it takes);
  - the topics it has told;
  - a robbed or robbing mark.

  That's 3-4 bytes x 8. Patience and `alead` live in `struct den`, so they outlast its time as an actor.

### 3.4 Telling: one wording by viewpoint, one sound table

- **One wording.** Every row reads "{Actor} verb{s} {target}", in three cases: the character acts, it's the target, or neither.
  - FIGHT's melee branch is rewritten with {@d}.
  - The SA rows and the denizens-only BEAT rows merge into shared pieces.
  - New DEN rows cover a search (F3) and speech (D10).
- **One gate.** An act is told if the actor's square is seen (T_VIS). Otherwise it's told if heard by the one hearing rule, with the throttle on heard sounds only (W1). The fix goes in overlay 23's o_den_heard: the UI's main RAM has 3,043 free against its 3,000 floor, already under the headroom rule (DECISIONS.md:6781-6783).
- **One sound table** for both queues, indexed by a shared act code, with PO_* and DE_* mapped onto it (W2, W9). Each event carries its actor's square and its loudness; today the character's E_PORTAL plays at a fixed 9 on its own square (dq0s.c:404-406). dq0s has 17,526 bytes free.

### 3.5 Time: one give-way on one clock

- **One module (E7):** gw_held (struct gw*, kind), in dq0lib, on real ticks.
  - It keeps a six-byte record per task: its state, the line's seq, ui_cmd, ui_keys and its start.
  - Two constants live in one header: GW_CMD, one value in place of 30 tenths, 50 ticks and 100 ticks; and GW_KEYS, 600 ticks.
  - It replaces dq0d's held (), dq0p's sense_held, and dq0w's den_held and ui_first. Each task gets back the bytes of its own copy.
  - The world's free pass for long jobs (kfree, kcut, kmore, kburn: dq0w.c:7014, 7060-7068) becomes a flag of the module, or stays the world's own.
- **"Catch up in full" needs four things:**
  1. drop CATCHUP (wbody.c:232-235), or keep it only as a guard of about 2 rounds against a stuck task;
  2. let a late actor decide again and again in one pass (dq0d.c:3373, as the turns pace's 64 already does);
  3. stamp steps with their intent's time, as blow_t does, so that positions, engagement (wbody.c:286-289) and den_on (dq0w.c:4806) agree on who was where, and when;
  4. key each denizen's dice to it (DECISIONS.md:6759-6760), so its late decisions roll as they would have on time.

  A late decision must also see the character as it was at that moment. Otherwise catching up hands out Y6's foresight. The character's square at any moment of its walk is pt0 + t on its path.
- **Room:** each denizen's "decided through" time and its dice state are about 8 bytes x 8 = 64 bytes of the minds' main RAM. They belong in E15's struct.
- **One body clock (Y9-Y14).**
  - Each body keeps an exertion count (2 bytes x 8), folded in lazily by o_tire.
  - It rests only on a wait or a sleep.
  - want_of runs per sheet.
  - RS_TIME's `when` stamp reaches past RS_ATTACK, to timed tests.

### 3.6 How it fits E15's struct and the referee's interface

**E15's struct holds the mind; the rules' state belongs to the bodies, shared with the character.**
- **E15's per-denizen struct** (GAME-CRITIQUE.md 8, E15) holds only what a mind chooses and remembers:
  - its target and that target's last-sensed square and answer;
  - patience;
  - `alead`;
  - the time it has decided through, and its dice;
  - whether it's listening.

  That's about 12-16 bytes x 8 = 96-128 bytes, against the 916 spare over dq0d's 2,048 floor (2,964 free). It's cleared with one memset as it's enlisted, which also ends the stale-mind class (built/2026-11-17-bodies.md, item 3).
- **The rules' state** lives with the body or the sheet, in the task that owns the rule, and has the same shape for the character (actor 0):
  - the senses: each actor's senses, eyes, light and facing (dq0p);
  - the defences used this round, fatigue and afflictions (the referee's sheet bank);
  - shock, the zone, the hold and the stealth grade (the world's body).
- **The minds' tight places:** part 2 (harmed, s_shoots) has 18 free, part 5 (ally) 323, part 6 (morale) 699, part 15 has 144 and part 9 has 356 (a-bodies/build-e.txt). So targets and the one exchange land first in parts 4 (1,330 free), 14 (1,146) and 20 (2,480), or room is won.

**The referee's interface needs three things to be the same for every actor:**
1. **One summary per actor.** The character's `pcsum` (sight, hearing, smell, step, gaits, load, the senses in the dark) has no creature counterpart; a creature has only its kind's figures. An RS_ answer "summary of actor k" at enlisting gives every actor the same shape, and the senses and the world read it alike.
2. **Requests about a pair:** RS_ATTACK for any two (3.2), and RS_TEST with the asker as the actor for every exchange (3.3).
3. **Room first:** E10's "the referee's library into overlays", since dq0harn sits 19 bytes over its floor.

## 5. The ten most serious differences

1. **Denizens perceive only the character.**
   - The senses answer only whether actor k senses the character (hub.h:1464-1470; sense.c:61-167).
   - What denizens know of each other is straight distance through rock: noises (dq0d.c:1708), a hostile actor within 4 squares (dq0d.c:1902-1927), a kin's fall (dq0d.c:5446-5452).
   - Without this, allies and factions can't fight fairly.
2. **No denizen fights another.**
   - Both referees refuse it (fight.c:665-668; dq0null.c:515-517).
   - The minds target only the character (dq0d.c:2223).
   - keep_off runs before ally (dq0d.c:2114, 2119), so an ally steps away from the character's foes.
3. **Hearing has three formulas, and noises reach one way.**
   - A denizen hears the character by sense.c:103-115, the character hears a denizen by caps.c:546-570, and two denizens hear each other by dq0d.c:1708.
   - The character's door work makes no noise for the minds (wdoor.c:60-114, 179-192). A denizen's is heard by everyone (wport.c:67-70, 255-277).
   - A denizen digging, or working a door, is heard through rock (wport.c:85-99). The character doing the same isn't.
4. **A denizen's eyes have no facing** (sense.c:139-147). The character's do (cast.s:421-436).
5. **Light.**
   - Denizens carry no lights (dq0w.c:8224).
   - The character's torch is seen only within the kind's sight (sense.c:134-147), not the 31 squares DUNGEON.md:1080-1085 gives. glow_reach is called only at eyes.c:79.
   - Denizens have a second light rule, `unlit_at` (dq0w.c:722-729), that counts the character's torch only where the character looks and a lamp only where it sees. That's against the settled "a denizen sees by any light" (DECISIONS.md:6801-6802).
6. **Defence.** A creature dodges or counterstrikes without limit, and folk never block with a weapon (fight.c:330-346). The user's rule (DECISIONS.md:784-789, built for the character in gear.c:548-610) is one foe per blocker per round.
7. **Typing costs denizens time; the character walks and fights on.**
   - There are three hold-backs on two clocks (dq0d.c:269, 3167-3200; dq0p.c:97, 127-153; dq0w.c:7058-7104).
   - A late denizen makes up 3 seconds at most (wbody.c:232-235), deciding once a pass (dq0d.c:3373).
   - Meanwhile the character's walk and blows go on (dq0w.c:4527, 7359-7360).
   - The user's rule is that denizens catch up in full (DECISIONS.md:6726-6727).
8. **A defender is ruled on by who it is.**
   - A sleeping character defends in melee; a sleeping denizen defends against arrows (wfight.c:168; wshoot.c:222).
   - A senseless character is struck only by a killer; a senseless denizen by anyone (fight.c:375-405).
   - A denizen's shock is never read: RS_ATTACK_HEALTH is written at fight.c:726 and read nowhere in dq0w or dq0d.
9. **Engagement is the character's alone.**
   - The character has no zone (engager, dq0w.c:5288-5310).
   - Only the character is put through the disengage and the grope test (dq0w.c:6197-6240, 6091-6127).
10. **Search and smell.**
    - A denizen's search reaches one square, finds no doors or traps, makes all its tests at its start and is never told (wtake.c:343-361). The character's reaches three squares, finds doors, traps and things, covers what its time allowed, and is told (wtrap.c:107-189; things.c:6490-6560).
    - Smelling and tracking are tested for the character (wtrace.c:563-571). For denizens they happen without asking and without a test (sense.c:148-155; dq0d.c:1960-1994).

**Close behind:**
- harmed () only for the character's blows (dq0d.c:1793);
- steps' loudness on two scales, a denizen's armour silent (dq0d.c:2205-2206 against dq0w.c:7836-7876);
- denizens tiring only in fights, and resting as they run (tire.c:30-62);
- a denizen's act seen but told only if heard (main.c:1036-1040);
- talk, trade, orders and robbery one-way (D1-D7).
