# history.txt - (the first world: games/dq0/WORLDS.md) the words a region's history is made of, in the world the game
# began with (G3: its own peoples and names, none of HarnWorld's), its peoples, its lands, what the peoples made and how
# it ended, the strata's names, the clues each era leaves, the rooms' words and their light; its tongues' sounds, the
# realms and mints of today, and which of its peoples hold, settle or raid.  themegen's make.js makes it this world's
# history.dat (lib/worlds/first/history.dat) with goals.txt and the bestiary (dq0d/kinds.txt, factions.txt);
# themegen reads it as it starts (themegen -w first, or no -w: the default world).  The same line format as a theme's (a
# key and its values between bars; a line begun with spaces goes on with the one before).  In the words, a slot is a %
# and a letter, filled as the history's made:
#   %R the region's name        %P an era's people         %K what they are (dwarves ...)
#   %N the era's great one      %X a place's name, theirs  %O the people who came after
#   %C how many centuries ago   %W what the era did (the era's words, for a clue)
#
# The keys:
#   people   what a people is (its name made by namegen, in its tongue), its tongue (a tongue line's: 1 ...), how it
#            builds, the lands it lives in, what it makes (the works below), its tongue's word (the referee's: what
#            the denizens speak and a character's "language" lines name, 8 letters at most) and its script (the
#            game's own, G3: what its writings are in, a character's "script" lines, 8 letters at most; the plan
#            for the game between dungeons, its step 8)
#   land     a land, the words for a region of it (one picked: a plural, the region's name said as "the Kharum
#            Hills"), its rocks, its waters, its climates
#   work     what a people makes: the strata it leaves, from the top down, each a purpose and its styles
#   name     a stratum's names, by its work and its place in the work's strata (0 its top): one picked that fits
#            (19 letters at most)
#   built    an era's words, by what its people made
#   reused   an era's words, its people making an older work their own (the older's word in %W: noun's)
#   noun     a work's word, as the eras after it speak of it
#   ended    how an era ended: its words
#   clue     what an era leaves to be read, by what its people made: a kind of the theme's (theme.txt's kind line's
#            values), and the words it's read in
#   rooms    the rooms' eight words (a theme's rooms line), by the people above (default: any other)
#   uses     the uses' twelve words, by the people above
#   light    a room's light, by the people above
#   folk     a faction (the bestiary's, dq0d/factions.txt) in words: the bestiary's factions that live in this world,
#            each once (one with no folk line isn't of this world: never an inhabitant)
#   now      the story's words for who has it now, by the faction that's strongest there
#   open     the story's first words
#   ago      how long ago an era ended, in words (%C the centuries)
#   tongue   a tongue's sounds, its number first (in order, 1 ...: the people lines' numbers, namegen's), its onsets,
#            its vowels, its codas (words, spaces between) and a people's name's ending
#   common   the tongue strangers speak (a tongue line's number): today's realms', and those who ask the character
#            to go (a patron, a seeker, a prisoner)
#   realm    a mint of today's (the theme's mint lines, now), in order: the tongue it's named in, its ways (the theme's
#            mint line's: l the region's own, g gold, p taken at its worth everywhere, h hard to pass, - none; ?h hard
#            to pass one game in two, by the seed)
#   coin     a people (the people line's what) that struck coin in its era, and its ways (g gold, or -); a people
#            with no coin line struck none
#   wild     the lands beasts live in (land lines' words)
#   raiders  the people (a people line's what) that comes to a region only last, digs its own mines and keeps a
#            prisoner or raids from them (a prisoner's goal and a scourge's: none, and neither is made), and its
#            faction (the bestiary's)
#   holds    a faction (the bestiary's) that's a people's (a people line's what) where an era of theirs holds its
#            works still: its kinds there, and its leader (a kind, or -)
#   colony   a faction (the bestiary's) that settles a people's works lost (an era of theirs not holding them, and
#            none holding theirs), in the lands given; its leader (a kind, or -); its kinds a tribe line's
#   tribe    a colony's tribe: its faction, its kinds, and where it's found (a land and a climate) or - (the first
#            tribe line the colony's own where it's found, or where none of the rest is picked; the rest by the dice)
