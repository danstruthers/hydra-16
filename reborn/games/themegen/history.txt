# history.txt - (themegen) the words a region's history is made of: the game's own world (G3: its own peoples and
# names, none of HarnWorld's), its peoples, its lands, what the peoples made and how it ended, the strata's names,
# the clues each era leaves, the rooms' words and their light.  make.js makes it themegen's data file
# (lib/dq0/themegen.dat) with goals.txt and the bestiary (dq0d/kinds.txt, factions.txt); themegen reads it as it
# starts.  The same line format as a theme's (a key and its values between bars; a line begun with spaces goes on
# with the one before).  In the words, a slot is a % and a letter, filled as the history's made:
#   %R the region's name        %P an era's people         %K what they are (dwarves ...)
#   %N the era's great one      %X a place's name, theirs  %O the people who came after
#   %C how many centuries ago   %W what the era did (the era's words, for a clue)
#
# The keys:
#   people   what a people is (its name made by namegen, in its tongue), its tongue (namegen's: 1-6), how it
#            builds, the lands it lives in, and what it makes (the works below)
#   land     a land, the words for a region of it (one picked: a plural, the region's name said as "the Kharum
#            Hills"), its rocks, its waters, its climates
#   work     what a people makes: the strata it leaves, from the top down, each a purpose and its styles
#   name     a stratum's names, by its work and its place in the work's strata (0 its top): one picked that fits
#            (19 letters at most)
#   built    an era's words, by what its people made
#   reused   an era's words, its people making an older work their own (the older's word in %W: noun's)
#   noun     a work's word, as the eras after it speak of it
#   ended    how an era ended: its words
#   clue     what an era leaves to be read, by what its people made: a kind of the theme's (theme.txt's kind line's
#            values), and the words it's read in
#   rooms    the rooms' eight words (a theme's rooms line), by the people above (default: any other)
#   uses     the uses' twelve words, by the people above
#   light    a room's light, by the people above
#   folk     a faction (the bestiary's, dq0d/factions.txt) in words
#   now      the story's words for who has it now, by the faction that's strongest there
#   open     the story's first words
#   ago      how long ago an era ended, in words (%C the centuries)
