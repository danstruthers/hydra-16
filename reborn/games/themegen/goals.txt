# goals.txt - (themegen) the goal kinds (the user's, DesignIdeas.md: "Each dungeon needs a goal, and it may be to
# find a specific artifact, clean out the evil denizens, rescue a prisoner, or just find a way through to a distant
# exit"; G5: all four, maybe more, so they're data: a kind more is a block more here).  make.js makes them part of
# themegen's data file (lib/dq0/themegen.dat), checking each one's lines as a theme's.
#
# A goal kind is a block: its goal line, then its things, then the theme's lines it adds, in the theme format with
# slots in them (history.txt's %R, %P, %K, %N; and these):
#   %A the goal's name (its first thing's, or a far exit's)   %a its first thing's kind (a theme kind's name)
#   %B its second thing's name                                %b its second thing's kind
#   %M a person of the goal's (a prisoner, a seeker, an old priest, a raiders' chief)
#   %S the goal's stratum's name   %s its number   %E its era (its number)   %G its guardians (a faction, or -)
#   %O a stratum for another way out (below the top, by the seed)
#   %g the guardians in words ("the dead")   %U a stratum above it, its name   %u its number   %e its era
#   %T the top stratum's name
#   %Q the one who asked (a why line's patron: a name the seed's, or a rumour's)
#   %Y why the character came (its why line's words, their slots filled: in the story, where "you came down to try" was)
#   %D what the patron pays, in words ("87 pennies": a why line's reward words)
# The lines:
#   goal     its name (the theme's goal line's), the mission (escape, rule), and where its stratum is: built (a
#            stratum an era built, not the deepest), cursed (an era's that ended cursed: the history makes one),
#            lair (a faction's lair that keeps a prisoner, or sends out raiders: kobolds'), or deep (the deepest
#            stratum); and heard, if the kind's only heard of (a rumour's, between two dungeons, or a theme's why line):
#            a history made without one picks among the others, the four a first dungeon's always were
#   thing    a thing it may have (one of each role picked): its role (a, b), its name ("the Horn of %N"), and its
#            kind (a theme kind line's values)
#   exit     a far exit's name, one picked (%A)
#   called   the goal's name (its goal line's), its slots filled
#   title    the theme's name, one picked (31 letters at most; the first that fits)
#   why      a reason to come (the plan's step 7: one picked by the seed, or a rumour's), its values: its name; the
#            rumour's words (heard between two dungeons: %R and %Q its only slots, the rest not made till the dungeon
#            is); why the character came (%Y: told in the story as the dungeon begins); what solving brings: the pennies
#            the patron pays (a range, the seed picks), the standing it earns (the life's reputation), a thing given
#            too (a kind of the catalogue's, or -), and the words that tell it (the account's between dungeons)
#   and the theme's lines: kind, piece (the goal's own thing first: dq0 -T -T begins by the first), rule, story (said
#            after the history's words), lock, opened, ending, wayin, wayout (one of those two, Q1's: the way in open behind
#            the character, or, the reason shutting it, another way out to find)
