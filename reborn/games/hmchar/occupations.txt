; occupations.txt - hmchar's occupations (games/hmchar: C's hmchar, hmchar.hl and hmchar.fs read it, a line at a
; time, as they need it; on the card in games/lib/hmchar, beside them): the book's Occupational Skills table
; (Character 15), its military units by culture (Character 27) and its churches (Character 23), in harn.h's skill names.
;
; A group of occupations follows its header, [KIND] or [KIND QUALIFIER], then its title (said as the group's listed).
; Each kind begins with its own letter (the menu's: u lists the unguilded).  A qualifier is a culture's name (the
; group offered to that culture's characters: the soldiers by their units) or a deity's (offered to its followers, or
; to a character who has none, the church making its deity theirs: the clerics).  An occupation is its name (19
; letters at most: the game keeps no more), its years, and its skills, a | between.  A kind's line named "all" is
; every one of its occupations' skills too (Character 23, 27: a skill both list, the higher OML).
;
; A skill: SKILL/N, opened at SB times N; SKILL alone, at its OML (Script: the native script, 70 + SB); Ritual/N, the
; character's deity's ritual skill; weapons N/M, N weapons chosen (Axe ... Blowgun), each at its OML plus M times SB;
; crafts N/M, N crafts chosen (Agriculture ... Woodcraft), each at SB times M.  An automatic skill listed takes the
; occupation's OML, not the two added (Character 16); a skill listed twice, the higher.
;
; Left out: a second language and more scripts (harn.h has one Language and one Script), the specialties (each is its
; skill: Ratcraft and Dogcraft Animalcraft, Horsecraft too, Needlework Textilecraft, a lance Polearm, a falchion or a
; longknife Sword, a hartbow Bow, a roundshield Shield ...), the temple tongues, the military units' option skills; the
; mages (Character 25: their skills come with magic, later); a soldier's unit by its culture only (the book's
; Tribal have none).
