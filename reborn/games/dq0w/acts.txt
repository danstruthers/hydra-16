# acts.txt - the world's act table (GAME-CRITIQUE.md's E6, ONE-PATH.md's 2.1: the one-path plan's stage 1): one row an
# act, the same numbers for every actor, the character's (HOUSE.md's) the reference.  games/dq0w/acts.js makes the
# table of it as the build begins (tools/dq0build.js's generate): obj/gen/dq0acts.inc, the rows in the paged ROM's
# library (modules/dq0lib/table.s: AC_ROW, no main RAM), and obj/gen/dq0acts.h, the rows' numbers (AC_*) and their
# record (struct act) for the world (acts.h's act_get, a part's copy).  The world's senders read a row by its act:
# its time (busy), its noise (E_NOISE for the character, E_DEN for an actor), its test (RS_TEST by who).
#
# A row: its name (AC_ and the name, upper case) and its fields, between bars:
#   time      tenths of a second; "N step" N of the actor's step (a door's: its own, an actor's bd.step)
#   test      the referee's purpose (rs.h's P_*, its P_ left out), or -
#   steps     its test's steps the easier (signed: the act's own; a lock's, a door's make come on top, the caller's)
#   loud      its noise, how far it carries (0-9: the one hearing rule's), 0 none
#   noise     the character's E_NOISE's what (hub.h's NZ_*, NZ_ left out), or -
#   den       an actor's E_DEN's what (hub.h's DE_*, DE_ left out), or -: the same act told and heard of a denizen
#   fatigue   the fatigue it costs (rs.h's GA_WORK: work), or - (the denizens' fatigue the one body clock's, to come)
#   task      a long task's kind (hub.h's LT_*, LT_ left out): timed, its test at its end, broken off by a command or
#             a blow; - none (done at once)
#   tool      the thing it wants in hand (items.h's KI_*, KI_ left out), or -
#   hands     the hands it wants (0, 1, 2)
# Rows are added at the end (the ids are their order); a row no code reads yet is the next step's, said so.
