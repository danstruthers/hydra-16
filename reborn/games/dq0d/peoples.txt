# peoples.txt - (dq0d) the peoples of the game's own world and their ways of building: who comes to a region in its
# history, what they build there and how it looks.  make.js makes it lib/dq0/dq0f.dat (lore.h's struct people a
# people, 64 bytes, the first none, its name the count; their words after), which the history's generator reads: an
# era's people builds a stratum to its purposes and styles, and the theme's words for it (its rooms, its uses, its
# light) are that people's; its walls, doors and marks are the stocking's and the clues' words; the kinds of its folk
# that live on are the inhabitants it can leave.
#
# A people's lines, its first a people line; each a key and its values between bars (a line that starts with a space
# goes on with the line before):
#   people    its name, as the history says it (23 letters at most) | its key (7 at most)
#   kinds     its folk among the bestiary's kinds (dq0d/kinds.txt's keys, 4 at most) that may still live where it
#             built, or -
#   tongue    the tongue it speaks (7 at most: the referee's word for it)
#   lands     the lands it lives in: m mountains, h hills, f forest, s marsh, c coast, p open land; * any
#   climate   c cold, t temperate, w warm; * any
#   builds    what its works are for (a stratum's purposes, theme.h's): halls keep temple cellars crypt works
#   styles    the plans it builds to (a stratum's styles, level.h's): b built, n natural, m mine, x mixed
#   deities   the deities it worships (deities.txt's)
#   walls     its walls, as they're described
#   doors     its doors
#   light     a room lit by its own light: by what
#   marks     what it carves and paints on its works
#   rooms     its eight words for a room, by its kind (the theme's rooms: a chamber whose use is lost, a hall, cells,
#             a vault, a crypt, a gallery, a cave's chamber, a storeroom)
#   uses      its twelve words for a built room's use (the theme's uses: a kitchen, a pantry, barracks, an armoury, a
#             chapel, a bedchamber, the lord's solar, a well room, a cistern, a gatehouse, a guardroom, a workshop)
