# deities.txt - (dq0d) the deities of the game's own world: its pantheon, the one every region shares (modelled on
# what HarnMaster Religion's entries cover: a deity's domains, temper, followers, holy days, symbols, temples,
# servants and enemies; the names and the words the game's own).  make.js makes it lib/dq0/dq0g.dat (lore.h's
# struct deity a deity, 64 bytes, the first none, its name the count; their words after), which the history's
# generator reads (who worshipped what, what their temples were like, which of the bestiary serve which god), and the
# UI (each deity's name a word of the commands': "call on Imma for help").
#
# A deity's lines, its first a deity line; each a key and its values between bars (a line that starts with a space
# goes on with the line before):
#   deity     its name: one word (11 letters at most), as it's said and called on
#   title     its title, as the history and the clues give it ("the Hollow King")
#   domains   what it's god of, words of these: war fire cruelty death undead chaos wealth pleasure bargains beasts
#             change serpents honour battle protection shadow deceit nightmares thieves healing harvest mercy
#             courage storms cold knowledge puzzles law dreams magic stars stone
#   temper    good, neutral or evil | order, balance or chaos
#   followers who worship it
#   rite      its monthly rite: the day of the month (1-30), or - none | what's done
#   feast     its yearly feast, or - none
#   number    its sacred number
#   colours   its colours
#   sign      its sign, as it's carved and painted
#   temple    what its temples are like (the stocking's and the clues' words)
#   burial    how its faithful lie: crypt (in a crypt or a tomb), barrow, pyre, earth (a grave), or none
#   servants  the bestiary's kinds that serve it (dq0d/kinds.txt's keys), or -
#   enemies   the deities its faith is at war with, or -
