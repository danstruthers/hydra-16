# kinds.txt - (dq0d) the denizens' kinds: a stand-in theme's bestiary, till themes are data of their own.  make.js
# makes it the denizens' task's data file (lib/dq0/dq0d.dat: 40 bytes a kind, the first none; then the factions,
# factions.txt's), which it reads into a bank of its own as it starts.  The numbers are the game's own, not a
# ruleset's: the referee's sheet for a creature comes from its key (later).
#
# A line a kind, its fields between bars:
#   name      what the character sees it as (11 letters at most)
#   key       the referee's creature (7 at most)
#   ways      letters: s shy (it keeps its distance from the character), a bold (it comes towards it), l it keeps
#             to its lair, w its lairs are by the stratum's ways in and out (where everyone passes), or - none
#   styles    the strata's styles it lives in: b built, n natural, m mine, x mixed
#   strata    the strata it lives in, from the top (0) down: first-last
#   group     how many in a lair: least-most
#   lairs     lairs a stratum, at most
#   range     squares from its lair it goes
#   sight     squares it sees the character by the character's light, and with none (its darkvision)
#   hearing   squares it hears the character
#   step      tenths of a second it takes to go a square (5 feet)
#   faction   its faction (factions.txt's): its tongue, its attitude to the character as it first meets it, and to
#             the other factions
#   mind      m mindless (nothing sways it), b a beast (Animalcraft only), l low (easily fooled, hard to reason
#             with), a average, c cunning (hard to fool)
#   sway      steps (each about 5%) for or against swaying it, besides its attitude and mind: a bear's hard to calm
#
