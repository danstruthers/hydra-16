# kinds.txt - (dq0d) the denizens' kinds: the game's bestiary, modelled on the HarnWorld Bestiary's range of creatures
# that suit a dungeon (its numbers the game's own, in its own words; the referee's sheet for each, a ruleset's, is
# the referee's: games/dq0harn/fight.c's bestiary, by the key).  make.js makes it the denizens' task's data file
# (lib/dq0/dq0d.dat: 40 bytes a kind, the first none; then the factions, factions.txt's), which it reads into a bank
# of its own as it starts; the history's generator chooses a region's inhabitants by the habitats (land, climate,
# light, the strata and styles), the nature and the size, and the population's generator (popgen) groups them by
# their lairs.
#
# A line a kind, its fields between bars:
#   name      what the character sees it as (11 letters at most)
#   key       the referee's creature (7 at most)
#   ways      letters: s shy (it keeps its distance from the character), a bold (it comes towards it), l it keeps
#             to its lair, w its lairs are by the stratum's ways in and out (where everyone passes), f it keeps
#             lights (a mind that uses fire, needing light: its lair's rooms kept lit, DUNGEON.md), t it keeps its
#             lair's floors swept (no dust there, its traces scattered); and its way with a foe it has brought down
#             (the character helpless: senseless, or in shock), by its nature (the user's, 2026-10-21): k it
#             finishes it (a killer, a predator, the dead), r it robs it (takes what it holds, but a light, and its
#             pennies, off to its lair) and leaves it (cunning folk), neither it only defended itself, and goes; or -
#             none of them
#   styles    the strata's styles it lives in: b built, n natural, m mine, x mixed
#   strata    the strata it lives in, from the top (0) down: first-last
#   group     how many in a lair: least-most
#   lairs     lairs a stratum, at most
#   range     feet from its lair it goes (its distances in feet, in fives: the map's squares, rs.h's units)
#   sight     feet it sees the character by the character's light, and with none (its darkvision)
#   hearing   feet it hears the character
#   step      tenths of a second it takes to go a square (5 feet)
#   faction   its faction (factions.txt's, a stand-in theme's): its tongue, its attitude to the character as it
#             first meets it, and to the other factions; a theme's factions (the theme file's inhabitants) take
#             their place, and speak the tongue of their first kind's
#   mind      m mindless (nothing sways it), b a beast (the animals' test only), l low (easily fooled, hard to reason
#             with), a average, c cunning (hard to fool)
#   sway      steps (rs.h's, each 5%) for or against swaying it, besides its attitude and mind: a bear's hard to calm
#   eyes      its eyes' adapting to the dark (the senses keep each actor's: DUNGEON.md's perception as layers): tenths
#             of a second to a 16th of a light level darker (a man's 19: a torch's light to the dark in some 4.5
#             minutes); 0 at once (the dead's sight isn't the eyes').  Its darkvision (sight's second) is the less as
#             its eyes are still used to more light than there is
#   size      1 tiny (a bat), 2 small (a rat, a snake), 3 smaller than a man (a kobold), 4 a man, 5 large (a bear),
#             6 very large (the greatest beasts)
#   land      the lands of the regions it's found under: m mountains, h hills, f forest, s marsh, c coast, p open
#             land (plains, heath); * any
#   climate   c cold, t temperate, w warm; * any
#   light     d it keeps to the dark (deep, or out only by night), l it wants light (it carries its own: folk), *
#             either
#   nature    a an animal, v vermin, m a monster (a beast of old magic), f folk (it speaks, uses tools and
#             builds), d the dead
#   arms      how it fights: bite, claws, sting, crush (it squeezes or hugs), horns (or tusks), talons, touch (a
#             draining touch), weapon (one held)
#   remains   what it leaves dead: body, or bones
#   tread     how heavily it treads, 0-9 (0: it leaves no prints, a flier, a shade): how clearly its marks show in the
#             dust and the earth (DUNGEON.md's traces on the floor: slice 4.1)
#   marks     the words for its marks (23 letters at most; - none): "small clawed feet"
#   smell     how strongly it smells, 0-9: how long its scent lingers (a quarter of an hour a point: HOUSE.md's)
#   scent     the words for its smell (23 letters at most): "a musky smell"
#   nose      its own sense of smell, 0-9 (0 none: the dead, by the eye alone): 6 or more, a hunter by smell, a
#             hostile one following the character's scent where it can't see it (dq0d.c's K_NOSE)
#   keeps     its way with what it doesn't carry (the outfits' plan, denizens.md's beds; 2026-10-27, HOUSE.md's the
#             denizens' belongings): b all on its body, o open at its bed (its mates trusted), h hidden at its bed
#             (a stash), c in a locked box at its bed, l given to its leader (a robber's takings to its faction's
#             hoard), H a leader (its lair keeps its faction's hoard); a robber's takings go to its faction's hoard if
#             it has one placed on the stratum, else hidden at its home if it hides its things, else there
#   home      the uses of the rooms it makes its lair's home in, the best liked first (three at most; * any room):
#             c chamber, h hall, l cell, v vault, y crypt, g gallery, a cave, s store, k kitchen, p pantry, b
#             barracks, r armoury, e chapel, q quarters, o solar, w well, i cistern, t gatehouse, d guardroom, f
#             workshop (world.h's U_*); none of them in its chunk, any
#   standing  0-4, its means (denizens.md's): 0 none, 1 tribal, 2 common folk, 3 people of standing, 4 lords; its
#             personal things', as it's first met: outfits.txt's [purse KEY], else its [standing N]
#
# The kinds after the first six came in with the history (Phase 3D), each modelled on a creature of the HarnWorld
# Bestiary (the page its entry's on): the bat (Bats 1, p10), the adder (Snakes 3, p88), the basilisk (Dragons 13,
# p36), the lindworm (Dragons 14, p37), the wight (the death god's mindless dead, Morvrin 5, p78), the shade (its
# willing dead, Morvrin 3, p76); and folk of the game's own: the kobolds' lord, bandits and their lord, cultists and
# their priest.  Phase 4's peoples (2026-10-29, the user's answer 11: the HarnWorld Bestiary's denizens with the
# Khuzdul, the Sindarin, men and the Gargun): the five Gargun (HarnMaster 3.5's Gargun 4-8: the small orc, Gargu-arak;
# the brown orc, Gargu-hyeka; the black orc, Gargu-khanu; the white orc, Gargu-kyani; the red orc, Gargu-viasal) and
# their king (the house's); the Khuzdul (a warrior, a miner, an elder: their halls and works, HarnWorld's Azadmere); a
# Sindarin warden; men-at-arms and their captain (a keep's garrison).  Their numbers by the books where they speak
# (the Gargun's senses, night vision, pace and groups; the species' modifiers, Character 7-8), the house's else
# (HOUSE.md's the peoples, for review).
