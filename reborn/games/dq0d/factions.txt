# factions.txt - (dq0d) the denizens' factions: a stand-in theme's, for the games whose theme has no inhabitants of its
# own (a theme file's factions take their place, popgen's: kinds.txt names each kind's, and a theme's faction speaks
# its first kind's faction's tongue).  make.js puts them in the denizens' task's data file after the kinds
# (lib/dq0/dq0d.dat: 32 bytes a faction, the first none), 15 at most.  A stand-in game has the first 7 (the stand-in
# themes' kinds are theirs: dq0d.c's NFACT); the rest only a history's (themegen's, as its inhabitants: Phase 4's
# peoples, 2026-10-29), each game's factions 7 at most, numbered as its theme has them.
#
# A line a faction, its fields between bars:
#   name       as kinds.txt names it (11 letters at most)
#   tongue     the tongue it speaks (8 at most: the referee's word for it, which the character may speak), or - none
#   attitude   its attitude to the character as it first meets it: h hostile, u unfriendly, w wary, i indifferent,
#              f friendly
#   others     how it stands with the other factions, by name: h hostile, w wary, f friendly (the rest indifferent);
#              each pair the worse of the two ways it's said
#
# name     | tongue | attitude | others

dead       | -      | h        | vermin h, crawlers h, beasts h, kobolds h, outlaws h, cult f
beasts     | -      | w        | kobolds w, outlaws w
kobolds    | kobold | u        | vermin h, dead h, outlaws h
outlaws    | common | u        | dead h, kobolds h, cult w
cult       | common | h        | dead f, outlaws w, vermin w
dwarves    | khuzdul  | w        | gargun h, kobolds h, dead h, crawlers w, outlaws w
elves      | sindarin | w        | gargun h, dead h, cult h, kobolds w, outlaws w
gargun     | gargun   | h        | kobolds h, outlaws h, dead h, cult w, beasts w
soldiers   | common   | u        | outlaws h, gargun h, kobolds h, dead h, cult h
