# Part Two. Lab Sheets

Twenty-six labs, one sitting each. Every lab goes with a chapter of the manual: read the chapter (or the sections
the lab names) first, then do the lab at a Hydra or an emulator.

Each lab has things to **do**, with places to write down what you saw; **questions** to answer afterwards; a list to
**check yourself** against; and a **challenge**, for when the rest was easy.

* Labs 1 to 3: meeting the Hydra
* Labs 4 to 9: inside HydraOS
* Labs 10 to 14: devices
* Labs 15 to 23: programming languages
* Labs 24 to 26: machine language
