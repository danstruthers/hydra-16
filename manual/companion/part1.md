# Part One. The Build Log

A place to plan the build, tick off every part as it goes in, write down what you measured, and track down
whatever doesn't work the first time. It follows the manual's Chapters 3, 4 and 5, step for step.

A build log isn't busywork. When a Hydra doesn't start, the first question is always "what changed since it last
worked?", and the builders who can answer it find their fault in minutes.
