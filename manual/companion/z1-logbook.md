# The Logbook

One line for each time you sit down at the Hydra. It takes a minute, and it's the first thing you'll want when you
come back after a week away: where was I, and what was I about to try?

## How to keep it

* **What I did**: the lab, the project, or the part of the build.
* **What I found out**: one thing you didn't know before. A surprise is best.
* **Next time**: the very next thing to do. Write it while you still remember.
